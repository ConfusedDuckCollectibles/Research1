## Structural premises and normalizations

Cyclotomic orthogonality gives D(E)⊂U_A and D(A)⊂U_E, with uniform occupancy six. For any affine line containing a selected points, summed occupancies of its 14 incident affine planes are 78+13a: off-line points count once, on-line points fourteen times. An entirely uniform pencil would give 84, impossible. A plane with at least 14 points determines all 14 within-plane directions by quotient pigeonhole; this would be such a pencil for the other member. Thus the cap is thirteen. Every pencil intersects each U: in the other member any family of thirteen parallel planes has some pair in a common slice, giving a within-plane difference direction in that pencil. Therefore the affine line cap is six because some incident plane has occupancy six.

A six-point line gives 156≤6K+13(14−K), hence K≤3 in that pencil. The other member’s corresponding thirteen slices can contain only configurations giving collectively at most three directions. A six-point noncollinear slice violates this by the exact planar gate (line cap five within any noncollinear six-set). A larger slice contains a noncollinear six-subset, since the original line cap is six. Average six forces thirteen collinear sixes. With at most three row directions, two rows are parallel. Their **eleven** cross-directions follow by Cauchy–Davenport on two six-element sets, and together with their common direction give twelve. Thus the first member has K≥12, which implies layers by the Radon calculation below; the other member has actual six-lines, so the same reasoning exchanges the conclusion. The entirely nonlayered branch has cap five in both members.

A plane cap ≤11 for E would imply K_E≤9, by the K≥11 Radon classification and verified projected K10 cap-11 exclusion. A size ≥11 plane in A contains an eleven-subset of cap five; its ten directions would contradict that pencil bound. Thus A would have plane cap ≤10 and also K_A≤9. All pencil sections of both U’s contain at least five points: average six in partner parallel slices, line cap five, then six-point direction gate. By the weighted identity below K_A≤9 gives |U_A|≤66; but lines through a point outside U_A partition U_A and each contributes at least five, giving ≥70. Symmetry proves each member has an affine plane with **at least** twelve points. When using a 12-point direction gate, take a 12-subset if the plane has thirteen, and retain the full plane’s actual normal; the present argument has not guaranteed an exactly-twelve occupancy. Similar ≤11-to-K≤9 argument is valid with the initial cap-13 Radon classifications.
