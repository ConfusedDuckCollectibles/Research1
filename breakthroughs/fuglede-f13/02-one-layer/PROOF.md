# A size-78 spectral pair cannot have exactly one six-layer member

Step 2 of the size-78 exclusion. This complements the independently checked [neither-layer proof](../01-neither-layer/PROOF.md) and does not exclude size 78. A layer means an affine plane with 13 selected points, not a completely filled 169-point plane.

Let E be layered in six such parallel planes, and suppose its partner A is not. Use the standard plane and line caps, cyclotomic duality, profiles and weighted incidence from the companion nonlayer proof. A six-point line in either would force layers in both, so both line caps are five. No pencil of the uniform-normal set U_A has ≥12 points: the Radon lemma would give A six layers. Every such pencil has at least five points because parallel slices of E average six and any six points with cap five give at least five directions. Thus the same elementary degree-eight incidence argument as in the companion applies to U_A, and A has all mixed moments through eight. This step uses only the cap on U_A, not on U_E.

Within E's layer plane, the union of all within-layer directions is contained in the tangent projective line of U_A, so it has at most eleven points. By the checked (12, ≤9 directions, line cap five) gate, any 12-point subset of a 13-point layer already determines at least ten directions. Consequently there exist at least three missing directions common to all six planes. Choose any three and send them by a single horizontal linear coordinate change to the vertical, horizontal and slope-one directions. In every layer the 13 selected points form a graph of a permutation f of F13 for which f(x)−x is also a permutation. The layer coordinate z is independent of the chosen horizontal coordinates.

## Exact lower bound of 90 nonhorizontal directions

Use projective chart z=1 for differences between different layers. If P_i and P_j are the horizontal point sets at heights s_i<s_j after picking integer representatives, their chart contribution, up to a common harmless negation convention, is (P_i−P_j)/(s_j−s_i) as a subset of F13². The orientations are chosen consistently throughout. Suppose the chart union has at most 89 points.

**Graph normalization.** A translation of each single planar graph puts f(0)=0 for enumerating shapes. The exact DFS tests every remaining height f(x) while tracking injectivity of f, f−x, and no point with five other points on the same slope (line cap five). There are 61,750 normalized graphs (1,404 determining ten directions and 60,346 determining eleven). Quotient by all horizontal translations: with origin normalization, enumerate x-shifts f'(x)=f(x+a)−f(a), retaining the lexicographically least record. There are 4,750 shapes. To reduce the first graph only, also quotient by a subgroup of the simultaneous linear stabilizer of the three chosen missing directions. Swapping x and y, mapping (x,y) to (x,x−y), and common dilation by two generate its needed finite orbits; the replay produces 84 first-graph representatives. The use of a subgroup is safe even without assuming it exhausts the stabilizer: each applied transformation is a single global horizontal change preserving the class for every layer and the common cardinality of the chart union. Do not apply independent linear graph automorphisms.

**AP anchor.** All 1,716 six-subsets of F13 contain a three-term arithmetic progression, checked directly. Relabel it by an affine change of z to heights 0,1,2. For the first two graphs, independent translations can both be performed by a *single* ambient affine map (x,y,z)↦((x,y)+b+cz,z); on the normalized difference chart this only applies one common translation. Thus set their shapes to canonical translation representatives; all shifts of the third relative to those are retained. Global linear stabilization of the first graph can be followed by a shear to recover the first-two translation normalization. The third graph is not forced to any additional normalization.

**Exact unions.** For each of 84 first shapes test second and third shapes in its adjacency list, retaining only shapes whose individual undilated difference set with the first has at most 89 points; dilation and translation do not change that size. The 47,893 ordered candidate shape triples reduce to 8,371 with the same bound also on the second–third difference. Use 169 third-graph translations; if the union of the first–second difference and the appropriately half-scaled first–third difference has more than 89, discard, which is safe by monotonicity. There are 253 partial passes and only two triple passes once the correctly translated second–third pair is included. Both residuals have the same translated graph at each of the three positions and have union sizes 81 and 85. The verifier records both complete 13-value graph arrays.

For any fourth height s≠0,1,2, enumerate all compatible shapes from the corresponding first-graph adjacency list and all 169 translations. Check each pair with the three anchored graphs, rescaling by each reciprocal s−i; prune only if one pair or an accumulated union exceeds 89. All 278,850 candidate translations are rejected. (These counts are after the three individual pair-cardinality gates.) Therefore even four of the six layers cannot coexist with chart size≤89. The affine invariant claim for the original E is that at least 90 nonhorizontal projective directions occur, and hence |D(E)|≥100 when combined with the at least ten within-layer directions.

## Contradiction on the nonlayer member

Because D(E)⊂U_A, we have |U_A|≥100 and |V_A|≤83. The general nonnegative variance weights k_E(v)=Σ(n_t−6)^2/26 satisfy Σ k_E=489. Under only the plane cap 13 and mass 78, the maximum k_E is 21: convexity of the sum of squares puts the maximizing counts at six 13s and seven zeros. This bound does not assume moments of E or integer weights.

In the equation 21|V_A|=Σ_v k_E(v)|V_A∩v⊥|, the right side is at most 1743. Relative to 4Σ_v k_E(v)=1956, the deficit is at least 213. As U_A pencil cap is eleven, only the lines with 11 uniforms can supply positive deficit, capped by 21 each. At least eleven distinct such lines are forced (213>210). The degree-nine and degree-ten moment forms of A vanish on U_A and thus on eleven projective points of each of these lines. Each line would divide either form if nonzero, impossible at its degree. Combining with A's moments through eight gives affine residue profiles. Constant residue six is uniform; zero corresponds to six layers (excluded for A); nonconstant affine residues are permutations of 0,…,12 with energy seven. Hence Σ k_A=489 is a multiple of seven, contradiction.

Together with the exclusion when neither member has layers, any remaining spectral pair has six parallel 13-point layers on **both** sides. This does not imply that arbitrarily chosen layer normals are orthogonal. The [remaining steps](../03-linked-layers/PROOF.md) reduce and then exclude the both-layer case.
