# Independent audit: exactly one six-layer member at size 78

**Verdict:** under the independently checked spectral caps and nonlayer78 results, an exactly-one-layered size-78 spectral pair over F13³ is impossible. Together with the companion no-layer result, both members of any remaining pair occupy six parallel affine planes of thirteen selected points each (not full 169-point planes). This does not settle size 78 or assert any relation of arbitrary layer normals.

## Geometric normalization

Suppose E is layered and A is not. A six-point line in either would force both layered by the verified pencil/Cauchy–Davenport argument, so both line caps are five. K≥12 Radon implies layers, hence K_A≤11. Every pencil of U_A has ≥5 normals: the corresponding thirteen parallel slices of E average six and some cap-five six-subset determines ≥5 directions. The projective degree-eight bound applies to U_A alone and makes A’s mixed moments through eight vanish. No moments of the layered E are assumed.

Let H be the common direction plane of E's six layers. The union of internal directions lies in U_A∩PG(H), so has at most eleven points. It has at least ten: take twelve points from one layer and apply the audited (12, direction budget 9, cap 5) negative gate. The stronger (13, budget 9, cap 6) zero gate was separately reproduced by substituting these parameters in the companion fixed-abscissa replay, which has a single support and zero full leaves; it is unnecessary. The independent mixed replay also directly obtains minimum ten in the graph catalogue.

Choose three directions missing from the entire union and map them by one linear coordinate change on H to vertical, horizontal and slope one. To see the existence, map two to coordinate axes and scale each axis to make the third slope one. Each layer is now a total function graph x↦f(x): missing vertical means x is injective, and the other two missing directions make f and f−id permutations. No layer is assigned independent linear transformations.

Horizontal translation by (r,s) followed by subtracting the height at x=0 yields f_a(x)=f(x+a)−f(a), a=−r. These exhaust the quotient by planar translations. A thirteen-point cap-five graph has no nonzero translation stabilizer: otherwise it is a single thirteen-point orbit of an affine line. Thus origin-normalized translation orbits each have size 13. The author's simultaneous stabilizer generators—axis swap, (x,y)↦(x,x−y), and dilation by two—are invertible and preserve the unordered missing triple; even a subgroup suffices for the orbit reduction. My independent full group enumerates all matrices with columns αd_j,βd_k for distinct missed rays and with column sum on the third ray; it has exactly 72 matrices and gives the same 84 orbits. Only the first layer is reduced by this simultaneous transformation, while every other transformed layer remains free over the full catalogue.

The AP gate was checked by testing distinct endpoints a,b in each six-level set and the midpoint 7(a+b) modulo 13. All 1,716 sets have such a distinct midpoint. Affinely send its three levels to 0,1,2. Let t_i denote translations of the three canonical shapes. One ambient shear and translation (X,z)↦(X+b+cz,z), b=−t_0, c=t_0−t_1, remove the first two translations. This simply adds c to every high-minus-low projective chart coordinate. The third relative shift is arbitrary. It remains arbitrary after a common horizontal linear transformation and redoing the first-two translation normalization.

## Chart and replay

Use chart contribution (P_j−P_i)/(j−i) for lower level i and upper level j. The original contributions have the opposite common sign, a global bijection, not a claim that individual sets are symmetric. Write raw differences of canonical shapes as H_ij. ## Weighted finish

The pair identities hold even when E is layered. Since each occupancy n≤13, n²≤13n and Σ(n−6)²=Σn²−468≤546, so k_E≤21 without any integrality assumption. The chart has at least 90 points and the disjoint horizontal line contributes at least ten, giving |D(E)|≥100 and |V_A|≤83 by D(E)⊂U_A. Compare with constant load four in the weighted pair identity: deficit ≥4·489−21·83=213. Only a line with eleven U_A points can contribute positively because K_A≤11, and its contribution is at most 21. There are therefore at least eleven distinct 11-uniform pencils (213>210), even if some lines contribute negatively. A directional moment form of A of degree 9 or 10 vanishes on U_A, so eleven points on each of these eleven lines force each as a factor if the form is nonzero. This is impossible by its degree. Together with moments through eight, all reduced plane profiles of A are affine. Constant six has weight zero, a nonconstant affine permutation of 0,…,12 has weight seven, and zero residue would give six elevations to 13, excluded by A nonlayered. Thus Σ k_A=489 would be divisible by seven, contradiction.
