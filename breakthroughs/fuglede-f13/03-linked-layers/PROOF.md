Inputs are the audited nonlayer and mixed exclusions; both members therefore have six parallel 13-point layers. ## 1. No six collinear points

If E contains a six-point affine line, its pencil has total occupancy 156, so at most three of the 14 planes have uniform six occupancy (others cap 13). The corresponding thirteen parallel slices of A have at most three directions altogether. They average six; by the nonlayer proof's planar gate every noncollinear six-set gives at least five directions, and any larger slice under line cap six contains one, so all thirteen are six-point lines with at most three directions.

Group those lines by orientation, size m each. Through projection along the group's common direction d, its m distinct planar points have no collinear triple (three original six-lines in one plane would yield 18>13). For m≥2 their quotient secants determine at least m if m is odd, at least m−1 if m even, by counting unordered pairs and at most floor(m/2) pairs per direction. A singleton contributes zero. Every quotient direction, originating from two parallel six-sets, forces a separate projective line through d with ≥12 points of D(A): eleven cross-directions from Cauchy–Davenport plus d. By the Radon lemma, this projective line has 13 points in U_E. Lines arising from different orientation groups cannot coincide; each high line meets the original common slice-direction projective line at exactly its group's orientation.

Sum these lower bounds over the ≤3 positive sizes partitioning 13. It is at least 11 except possibly (1,2,10), (1,4,8), (1,6,6), with bound 10. The exact line-cap-two planar gate excludes (10, ≤9 directions), (4, ≤3), and (6, ≤5) respectively, sharpening at least one class in each partition by one. Using the existing normalized two-absent-direction DFS, the final visit histograms for those gates are respectively [165,3360,18536,24008,5520,244,0,0,0], [55,100,0], and [330,2880,1480,0,0]. Thus U_E has at least 11 rich lines, impossible by the opening paragraph. This works without assuming E and A already both layered and gives both line caps five.

## 2. At least one perpendicular pair of layer normals

Suppose no layer normal of either member is perpendicular to any layer normal of the other. For any A-layer normal c, its tangent pencil in U_E has at most 11 uniform directions; otherwise the Radon lemma produces a new layer normal of E on that projective line, perpendicular to c. Thus the union of within-layer directions of A in the orientation c has at least three common missing directions. For each such direction d, the projection of A along d is injective and consists precisely of six full 13-point parallel quotient lines, yielding a K13 pencil in U_A with unique missing point c. These are distinct for distinct d, and distinct across layer normals c because K13 pencils have a unique missing point, which by Radon has a 0/13 profile.

The symmetric cap-five E meets the hypothesis of the [exact four-layer AP proof](../02-one-layer/PROOF.md) (at least three missing tangent directions), so |D(E)|≥100 ⇒ |V_A|≤83. Compare weighted identity with 4×489 to force deficit ≥213. Only K11 and K13 lines accrue positive deficit: K12 is impossible by the Radon lemma. Each K11 line contributes at most 21. For K13 lines having the same unique missing c∈V_A, corresponding v are in c^⊥, and Σ_{v⊥c} k_E(v)=21+t_E(c)=21; t_E(c)=0 by c∉U_A. Thus each such group contributes at most 3×21=63, though it already gives at least three distinct rich pencils as above.

Let c₀ be the number of A-layer normals. If c₀≥4 there are already ≥12 rich pencils. If c₀≤3, there are ≥3c₀ K13 lines and deficit forces ≥ceil((213−63c₀)/21)=11−3c₀ K11 lines. Thus ≥11 rich pencils regardless, contradiction. So we may choose perpendicular normals. This establishes existence only, not perpendicularity of arbitrary choices.

## 3. Linked graph coordinates and identical in-layer direction palettes

Choose E's normal e_z and A's perpendicular normal a_t along the second coordinate in dual frames, so E rows (x,f_s(x),s) for s∈S, and A rows (u,t,g_t(u)) for t∈T, where |S|=|T|=6 and each x or u ranges over all 13 values. The simultaneous coordinate representation is exact: n_A∉U_A implies the projection of E along n_A is injective, and similarly n_E∉U_E for A, so the graph forms follow by count 13 in each layer. All graphs have line cap five.

For r∈F13 define I_r as the union, over distinct E-layer indices s,s' and pairs x,x' with (x−x')/(s−s')=r, of the normalized difference (f_s(x)−f_{s'}(x'))/(s−s'). Its size is the number of nonhorizontal E-difference directions at chart coordinate r. Let C_t(r)=Σ_u ζ^{ru+g_t(u)}, and a(r)=#{t:C_t(r)≠0}. Orthogonality and the field automorphism ζ↦ζ^{(s−s')⁻¹} show that for every w∈I_r, Σ_t ζ^{w t} C_t(r)=0, independently of which layer pair supplied w. Thus if r is active (a(r)>0), |I_r|≤a(r)−1 by the invertible submatrix of the prime Fourier matrix of order 13 (Chebotarev), using only active columns. This is a constraint on the whole union, not separate pairwise bounds.

For a fixed r, every plane x−r s=x₀ contains one E-point for each s and has six selected points. The directions in that plane are all finite slopes in I_r because the levels s are distinct. By cap5 and the six-point gate it has ≥5 directions. On active r this gives 5≤|I_r|≤a(r)−1≤5. Thus |I_r|=5 and a(r)=6. By cyclotomic orthogonality, C_t(r)=0 iff u↦ru+g_t(u) is a permutation, i.e. iff the finite corresponding direction is missing from that planar graph. Therefore all six A slice graphs have exactly the same direction set, and similarly the E slice graphs by duality. Their count is at least ten by the 12-point planar gate, so there are at least ten active r. The E cross-chart is at most 5m_A+13(13−m_A) where m_A is this count. If E's within-layer count m_E≤11, the exact AP mixed geometry applies and E chart≥90, contradicting chart≤89 from m_A≥10. By symmetry both counts are 12 or 13, both charts at most 73. In every active-r cross-plane the six-point graph has S as its labelled abscissa set, so S and T have one of the five affine mask types 111/187/215/311/427 from the audited planar gate.

The remaining exact constraints include the integer 6-column phase-level arrays. For each active r let n_t(j)=#{u: ru+g_t(u)=j}, t∈T. Each is a length-13 integer profile of mass13, entries 0 to 5, nonuniform (a(r)=6). For each of five w∈I_r all cyclic sums Σ_{t∈T} n_t(j−wt) equal 6 (not merely modulo 13). Geometric consistency across every r and spectral duality impose more.
