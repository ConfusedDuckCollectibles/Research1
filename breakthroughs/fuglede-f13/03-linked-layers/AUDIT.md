# Audited analytic refinements of the both-layer size-78 residual

The independent neither-layer and one-layer derivations leave both sides consisting of six 13-point layers. The following check narrows that remaining case without ruling it out. Write C_X for projective normals in which X has six occupied parallel planes of size thirteen, U_X for normals of uniform occupancy six, V_X for its complement, and K for the occupancy of a projective line in U_X. The companion Radon lemma implies that K≥12 means K=13 and this line has one missing normal, in C_X. K=14 is impossible.

## No affine six-point line in either member

Suppose E has a six-point affine line. As in the earlier cap argument, the pencil through it has at most three normals of U_E, hence the thirteen parallel slices of A for that tangent direction plane are all collinear six-sets in at most three orientations. Group these distinct rich affine lines by their direction d. In a group of m, their images in the affine quotient by d are m distinct points with no three collinear (three original parallel six-lines in one affine plane would give 18>13). Every planar slope contains at most floor(m/2) disjoint pairs. Consequently the number of directions is at least m for odd m≥3, m−1 for even m, or zero for a singleton. Each quotient direction means two parallel original rich six-lines with that quotient difference. Their differences give at least eleven cross directions by Cauchy–Davenport, plus d, on the associated projective direction line. Thus its corresponding K in U_E is at least twelve and hence thirteen. Within the same group distinct quotient directions give distinct projective lines through d. Different groups cannot repeat these lines: every such line comes from points in distinct original slices (since there is only one rich line per slice) and is not the common horizontal projective line, meeting it exactly at the group’s direction d.

There are 21 partitions of 13 into at most three positive group sizes. The lower count above falls below eleven only for (1,2,10),(1,4,8),(1,6,6). Independently replayed planar cap-two computations with fixed abscissae give zero at (size,direction-budget)=(4,3),(6,5),(10,9), yielding ≥4,6,10 directions respectively in those groups and hence ≥11 total. These exact searches are necessary only for the slight even-size improvements; cap-two denotes no three collinear. The independent code covers all admissible sets because a failing direction bound leaves a missing projection direction; use a coordinate along its quotient and exhaust all affine-canonical supports and all affine/scaled height residuals. In particular the supports classes are 7,14,3 in the displayed order.

Thus U_E has ≥11 K13 lines. For every j≤10, its degree-j directional moment form vanishes on U_E. If nonzero, each of those eleven lines divides it because it has thirteen distinct rational roots in degree≤10; impossible. Degree zero already vanishes since 78=0. All occupancy residues are therefore affine by the standard pairing calculation, and each profile is uniform (weight zero), the permutation 0,…,12 (weight seven), or six thirteens plus seven zeros (weight 21). The last case follows when the residue polynomial is identically zero. Now Σk_E=489 is not a multiple of seven, contradiction. Exchange E,A. This gives line cap five for both even in the entirely layered residual.

## At least one perpendicular pair of layer normals

Suppose no c_E in C_E is perpendicular to any c_A in C_A. For each c_A, the target tangent projective line c_A⊥ in U_E must have K≤11: otherwise its unique missing E-layer normal supplied by the Radon lemma would be perpendicular to c_A. Hence there are at least three tangent directions d not determined in any layer of A (its layer direction union is in U_E∩c_A⊥). Project A along each such d. Its horizontal six layers project bijectively to six distinct full affine quotient lines, because d is absent within each layer and quotienting by d preserves the level c_A. In the corresponding normal pencil d⊥, all directions except c_A are uniform: a nonparallel line in the quotient meets each of the six full lines once. Thus these are ≥3 K13 pencils in U_A for each c_A, distinct because each has unique missing point c_A, and within a fixed c_A different d gives different pencils.

Apply the audited AP chart bound to E in any one of its layers under this setup: its corresponding tangent line in U_A has K≤11 (by the same no-perpendicular assumption) and hence it has three common missing directions. With the cap-five conclusion above, that gate gives ≥90 off-tangent directions. A size-12 subset of a layer gives at least ten tangent directions, so |U_A|≥|D(E)|≥100 and |V_A|≤83. As before the deficit relative to constant load four is ≥213. Each K13 pencil has just one missing point c in C_A, and its exact contribution is 3 k_E(v) (where v is its polar point). For any fixed c, all corresponding v satisfy v⊥c, and Σ_{v⊥c}k_E(v)=21+t_E(c)=21 since c∈V_A. By nonnegativity this bounds their total contribution by 63 for that c; this includes all K13 pencils. There are no K12 pencils, and a K11 pencil contributes at most 21. If c=|C_A|=1,2,3, the number of positive K11 pencils is at least ceil((213−63c)/21)=11−3c. Adding the ≥3c already constructed K13 pencils yields ≥11 distinct rich pencils. If c≥4 the K13 pencils alone already give ≥12. Those lines force all degree-through-ten directional moments of A to vanish, producing the same weights 0,7,21 and the same contradiction 489 mod 7. Therefore at least one pair of layer normals is perpendicular. This does not show every pair or a canonical pair is perpendicular.

## Linked layer chart bound

Given one perpendicular pair, choose dual coordinates where the E-layer levels are s in a six-set S along z, and A-layer levels are t in a six-set T along x. Because c_E is nonuniform, it is a missing direction of A; likewise c_A is a missing direction of E. Thus E and A, in dual coordinate systems, can be written as (f_s(y),y,s) and (t,u,g_t(u)), respectively. Choosing dual coordinates is allowed: take c_E as the third covector, c_A as the first vector and complete to dual bases, since c_E(c_A)=0. Affine translations do not affect directions or orthogonality.

For E points across levels with nonzero gap d, their normalized chart coordinates are (i,r)=(Δx/d,Δy/d). The spectral sum in A equals zero at d(i,r,1). Apply the cyclotomic automorphism ζ↦ζ^{d^{-1}} to get
\[
\sum_{t\in T}\zeta^{it}C_t(r)=0,\qquad
C_t(r)=\sum_{u\in\mathbb F_{13}}\zeta^{ru+g_t(u)} .
\]
For each fixed r, this single vector of coefficients serves **all** cross-layer gaps, so its Fourier zero set contains the union I_r of all horizontal chart values i at that r. With a(r) active coefficients, the prime [Chebotarev Fourier-minor theorem, e.g. Tao Lemma 1.3](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0308286) gives |I_r|≤a(r)−1 when a(r)>0; if all coefficients vanish, the trivial bound is 13. A coefficient vanishes exactly when u↦ru+g_t(u) is a permutation, by independence of the sole cyclotomic all-ones relation among length-13 residue counts. Thus a(r)>0 precisely for the finite slopes −r occurring in some A-layer graph. If m slopes are active and d_t is the number of finite directions of A's t-layer, total E chart size is ≤Σ_t d_t−m+13(13−m) ≤5m+13(13−m). The cap-five planar twelve-subset gate gives each d_t≥10. Initial E chart bound is therefore ≤89. If E had ≤11 within-layer directions, the independently replayed AP gate would give E chart ≥90, a contradiction. Swap E and A, so each has 12 or 13 within-layer directions along the chosen orientations, hence each opposing chart ≤73 (and ≤65 when all thirteen opposing slopes are active). This does not exclude arbitrary graph choices or close size 78.

## Saturation at active chart slopes

For fixed active r, choose any plane y−rs=y₀ in these E coordinates. It contains one point from every one of the six layers: y is uniquely y₀+rs, and f_s is defined for every y. These six points determine at least five directions by line cap five and the audited six-set planar bound. All their directions have nonzero level difference and are of the form (i,r,1) with i∈I_r. Thus 5≤|I_r|≤a(r)−1≤5, and equality gives a(r)=6. Consequently all six A-level graphs have the same direction set, exactly its own union of 12 or 13 directions. By exchanging the sets, the same holds for the E-level graphs; this does not say that the A and E direction sets equal one another. The six points in each such fixed plane are an injective planar graph over the six level coordinates S, determining exactly five slopes, so S must have one of the companion’s five affine-canonical six-abscissa masks; likewise T. This remains a necessary restriction rather than a finite census over the linked graphs.

Equivalently, for each active r, the six count profiles n_t(y)=#{u:ru+g_t(u)=y} are supported on 0…5, have mass 13 each, and for all five i∈I_r satisfy Σ_t n_t(y−it)=6 for every y. The upper count is the cap-five condition for one affine line within a layer. The identity is exact integer uniformly occupied planes, not merely a field congruence: its polynomial evaluated at ζ vanishes, and the thirteen coefficients sum to 78. No closure of this system has yet been asserted in this audit.
