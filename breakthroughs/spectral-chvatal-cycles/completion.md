# Closing the shared-marker 2-regular anchor family for r ≥ 5

Research1 completion note, November 2026. Not peer reviewed. This closes the earlier residual weighted-theta problem for all simple 2-regular anchor graphs on r≥5 anchors with n≥2r+1 old points, including disconnected cycle unions and minimal n. It uses the analytic n≥32 theorem and verified type bound for n≥26 and selected (n,r) at 22–25. The remaining 141 partition-and-size cases are handled here, not subsumed by a floating margin. This is not unrestricted Spectral Chvátal H; the original two-layer weights within this family follow by the crossing/endpoint argument in proof.md.

## Notation and exact factored defect

Use proof.md §§1–2. Let S be surviving old triples, D the deleted triples, X the full old-coordinate incidence on S, and X_D on deletions. Put K=binom(n−1,2), q=binom(n−4,2), L=K−2r, Π=X(X^T X)^−1X^T, E=I−Π. Write P_s and P_D for survivor and deleted principal blocks of the full Kneser residual P*=I+A_disj/q−J/K. Its cross block is R=P^*_(S,D); its gap on the full incidence complement is μ=1−1/q. Define
    U=X(X^TX)^−1 X_D^T.
For signed bridge and tip blocks use the constructive exact incidence solves of proof.md: C^T x_i is 1 except for the bridge's own anchor (0), and is λ=(K−1)/K for tips; V_t is 1 on t-incidence and X^T V_t=(K−1)e_t. Define g=1−2C1_r, W=C−J/K, h=W1_r, B=2CC^T+gg^T/L+VV^T/[K(K−1)]. Ties use exactly the old set I = anchors plus untouched coordinates.

Let M retain locked entries (distinct intersections and the diagonal). The requisite correction to the projected core P0=E P_s E is D0=M(I−B−P0). Before masking the formula for its entries on all locked positions is the low-rank array
    D_raw = −2WW^T−4hh^T/L−VV^T/[K(K−1)]−UR^T−RU^T−U P_D U^T.
Indeed B−J/K equals its three displayed positive summands; P_s X=−R X_D and X^TP_sX=X_D^T P_D X_D because the complete P* kills all incidence. On a locked position P_s=I−J/K. Thus M(D_raw)=D0. The exact checker computes only matrices of old-coordinate dimension in the incidence solves and the feature factors in this formula; it need not construct a large Gram inverse.

## n ≥ 15: exact actual masked Frobenius replay

For symmetric locked compatible D, the operator T(D)=M(EDE) has Frobenius coercivity κ=1−6(n−4)/(binom(n−2,2)−6) by the local incident-column Schur argument in proof.md. If ||D0||_F<μκ, it follows that P=P0+E T^−1(D0) E is PSD on the full old incidence complement with the mandated locked entries. As in that theorem the final bridge Schur is P+gg^T/L+VV^T/[K(K−1)]⪰0. Compatibility includes all old coordinates, not just tied ones. For n≥15, κ>0.

All feature values are exact rational solutions. Use two factors L_A,R_B for −D_raw(A,B) from successively pairing (W,2W), (h,4h/L), (V,V/[K(K−1)]), (U,R), (R,U), (U,U P_D). The checker takes exact integer floors of F times each factor entry at F=2^26. If rounded factor maxima in coordinate j are m_j,n_j, expansion of a dot product proves an absolute entry error bounded by
    ε1=Σ_j(m_j+n_j+1)/F².
The Integer dot products between features are evaluated in JS Number only after bounding every signed dot partial sum in absolute value by Σ_j m_j n_j ≤ 2^52. They are therefore exact integers. Divide by F² and round once more to a grid of spacing 2^−22; this introduces at most ε2=2^−23. Sum all squared rounded values exactly, counting offdiagonal positions twice. Integer chunks below 2^52 are accumulated as BigInt. For Lck locked entries and integer squared sum R2 the true norm is bounded by sqrt(A)+sqrt(B), where
    A=R2/2^44,   B=Lck (ε1+ε2)².
Comparing to μκ uses only rationals: require D=(μκ)²−A−B>0 and D²>4AB. Nothing is decided by a floating norm, and no error interval sign assumption is needed.

The original checklist is generated by every sorted partition of r into cycle lengths ≥3 and every n from 11 through 31 subject to r≥5 and 2r+1≤n. This covers connected and disconnected graphs; the labeling of edge tips is immaterial because they are all distinct. It has 468 cases; the earlier type bound covers 327 of them (all n≥26, and r=5 at n22; r=5,6 at n23; r=5..8 at n24; r=5..10 at n25). Of the 141 remaining, exactly 133 have 15≤n≤25. Every one passes the rational test above.

## n = 11,...,14: exact residual repair after an approximate solve

The eight cases are C5 at n=11,12,13,14; C6 and C3+C3 at n=13,14. The stated κ bound is nonpositive here; neither extrapolate it nor assume invertibility of T. Use a bounded floating CG solve on the masked operator only to suggest a dyadic locked symmetric matrix D (entries rounded at 2^−48). The exact checker first verifies
    ||D||_F < μ−1/3
by squaring nonnegative rationals, so P=P0+EDE is positive with margin α=1/3 on ker X^T before fixing its locked error. It then **computes** the exact rational remaining masked residual R0=D0−M(EDE), with common denominators from the rational incidence inverse; support/compatibility is checked literally. Do not infer these exact properties from stopping tolerance. Adding R0 restores every old locked entry but changes the tied star columns. Apply the earlier constructive disjoint-support right inverse to −R0 X_I (in the same exact common denominator), and obtain a disjoint-supported Δ with
    (R0+Δ)X_I=0.
The right inverse uses nonreference rows linked to one-tight two-tip rows and a spanning tree between each pair of reference groups (there are ≥5 tips). It is verified by exact support and zero residual at every tight coordinate for every row. Thus the Gram H continues to satisfy the weighted-tight-star equations; bridge block C is fixed. The correction E'=R0+Δ has spectral norm bounded by the exact maximum row sum of absolute values, since it is symmetric (and the two supports are disjoint).

For completeness, an analytic strict margin on the correct full coupled Schur subspace is as follows. The only forced nullspace is
    K_t = {Xc : c_tips=0, Σ_(i∈I)c_i=0}.
Take x⊥K_t and write x=y+Xc with y=Ex. Let c_J be its tip coefficients and c0=Σ_(i∈I)c_i. Then Xc differs from X times the coefficient vector (c0/|I| on tight centers, c_J on tips) by a vector of K_t. As Xc⊥K_t,
    ||Xc|| ≤ ||X|| (|c0|+||c_J||).
The exact incidence identities give
    L(c0+λ Σ c_J)=g^T(x−y),  (K−1)c_J=V^T(x−y).
For upper bounds p≥||X||, b≥||g||, c'≥||V||_op, d≥1+sqrt(r), it follows that
    ||x|| ≤ A0 ||y|| + A1 |g^Tx| + A2 ||V^Tx||,
    A0=1+p(b/L+d c'/(K−1)),  A1=p/L,  A2=p d/(K−1).
The checker takes p=ceil(sqrt(3K)) from the max row sum of X^TX and upper integer square-root roundings of g and V Frobenius norms for b,c'. For the unperturbed old Schur S=P+gg^T/L+VV^T/[K(K−1)], Cauchy–Schwarz now gives on K_t^⊥
    x^T S x ≥ ||x||² / [A0²/α + L A1² + K(K−1) A2²].
Both S and E' kill K_t. Exact comparisons in all eight cases give max-row bound of E' strictly smaller than this positive margin, so S+E' is PSD everywhere. At the smallest C5,n11 the analytic margin is 148225/591641427; the exact repaired row-sum bound is less than 3·10^−13. No numerical eigenvalue, and no inverse of T at these small n, is assumed.

Combining this with the bulk replay and the earlier large and type certificates exhausts the original checklist, then all n beyond it. For a normalized resulting Gram H, a common tight star s satisfies Hs=1 and s^THs=K, hence KH−J≽0 gives weighted theta ≤K; a tight star attains it. The full two-layer downset across all ratios follows from §4 of proof.md with its explicit weight merge, marker leaf extension, and endpoint interpolation. The proof below r=5 is not asserted; the real repair here specifically used five or more tips and its support range theorem.
