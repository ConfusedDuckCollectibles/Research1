# Breakthroughs

Results that materially advance or resolve a major research question and withstand independent verification. Other publishable findings belong in [Discoveries](../discoveries/).

- [Fuglede's conjecture on the 13 × 13 × 13 grid](fuglede-f13/): proves the entire finite-field case at prime 13. The last possible counterexample size, 78, is ruled out with exact arithmetic, explicit proof coverage, and independent replays. Other finite groups and the general conjecture are outside scope.
- [The prime-power case of one-dimensional Fuglede](fuglede-prime-power/): proves spectral-to-tiling and its converse for prime-power-size sets at every finite cyclic order, classifies their normalized integer spectra, and proves the real-line spectral-to-tiling implication for prime-power spectrum periods at measure one. Includes a separate sharp maximal-orthogonality counterexample family. A [follow-up](fuglede-prime-power/twice-prime/) covers twice-prime sizes in any cyclic group and the matching real-line periods. The unrestricted problem remains open.
- [Weighted Spectral Chvátal for the cycle family](spectral-chvatal-cycles/): proves equality for every eligible size and weight ratio in the shared-marker two-layer cycle family, including disconnected cycles. Includes the analytic proof and exact small-case replays. Unrestricted Conjecture H remains open.
