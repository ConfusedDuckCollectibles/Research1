# Research1

An archive of research notes and reproducible checks. Each project explains what it establishes, what remains uncertain, and how to run its checks. Notes are not peer reviewed unless stated.

- [Discoveries](discoveries/): original, reproducible results with a clear scope.
- [Breakthroughs](breakthroughs/): results that resolve or materially advance a major research question and withstand independent verification. Includes the [equality cases in Borg’s signed-set conjecture](breakthroughs/borg-signed-sets/), [Fuglede's conjecture on the 13 × 13 × 13 grid](breakthroughs/fuglede-f13/), the [prime-power case in one dimension](breakthroughs/fuglede-prime-power/), and the [cycle family of Weighted Spectral Chvátal](breakthroughs/spectral-chvatal-cycles/).
- [Projects](projects/): open research tools, reproducibility work, and work in progress.
