# Research1

An archive of research notes and reproducible checks. Each project explains what it establishes, what remains uncertain, and how to run its checks. Notes are not peer reviewed unless stated.

- [Discoveries](discoveries/): original, reproducible results with a clear scope.
- [Breakthroughs](breakthroughs/): results that resolve or materially advance a major research question and withstand independent verification. The first is [Fuglede's conjecture on the 13 × 13 × 13 grid](breakthroughs/fuglede-f13/).
- [Projects](projects/): open research tools, reproducibility work, and work in progress.
