# Primary sources and attribution

The contribution is a finite, calibration-locked two-grating inequality with sharp residual-direction constants, a positive-amplitude collective-model violation, and an exact characterization of general fixed-time positive-path responses. The underlying gray phonon model, ballistic sinc response, ambiguity of oscillatory thermal gratings, convex order, martingale coupling, and minimax theorem are prior work.

## Closest phonon-transport work

- [Chuang Zhang, Samuel Huberman and Lei Wu, *On the emergence of heat waves in the transient thermal grating geometry*](https://doi.org/10.1063/5.0102227), with [open version 2](https://arxiv.org/html/2111.01578v2), §II and §III.2. They explicitly distinguish a heat-capacity-defined temperature from relaxation-weighted collision temperatures (Eqs. 4, 9–11), derive the gray three-dimensional ballistic sinc (Eq. 25), and already compute the same class of gray dual-relaxation, continuous-angle forward problems (Figure 7). They also show circumstances in which ballistic/quasiballistic signals oscillate. The new use of this known model is a certified violation of a two-grating positive-path inequality, not a new transport equation or first calculation of gray waveforms.
- [Chuang Zhang and Zhaoli Guo, *A transient heat conduction phenomenon to distinguish the hydrodynamic and (quasi) ballistic phonon transport*](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ijheatmasstransfer.2021.121847) (2021). This is important prior art for a physical discriminator: they investigate a local temperature dropping below the original ambient minimum after localized heating. A real-space undershoot below the initial minimum and a negative Fourier amplitude from a sinusoidal initial condition are different observations. The present test can reject the specified null on two *positive* grating amplitudes even when each separately exceeds its ballistic response.
- [J. Callaway, *Model for Lattice Thermal Conductivity at Low Temperatures*](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRev.113.1046) (1959), foundational separation of normal momentum-conserving relaxation and resistive scattering. [Hua and Minnich](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevB.89.094302) (2014) and [Forghani, Hadjiconstantinou and Péraud, Appendix D](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevB.94.155439) (2016) provide energy-conserving relaxation-time/ballistic transient-grating baselines.
- [Nikhil Malviya and Navaneetha K. Ravichandran, *Callaway approximation to the Boltzmann equation fails to predict phonon hydrodynamics*](https://doi.org/10.1088/1361-648X/ade219) (2025). This is a reason not to treat the collective witness as a quantitative model for real samples. [Martinez-Margolles and Schelling, arXiv:2607.07644v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.07644v1) examine coherence, interference and modifications of conventional phonon response calculations. The positive-path test does not identify or exclude those additional mechanisms by itself.
- [Yan Huang, Wanying Liu, Yangyu Guo and Hong-Liang Yi, *Spatiotemporal dynamics of heat-pulse propagation in graphite via phonon Boltzmann transport equation*](https://doi.org/10.1103/w75t-4cz7) (October 2026; publisher abstract inspected). This studies spatial heat-pulse propagation with first-principles input and reports ballistic and hydrodynamic regimes. [Xin Qian et al., arXiv:2611.16161v1](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.16161v1) (November 2026), especially Eqs. 17–18 and Figure 4, derives a two-field hydrodynamic-window criterion and models TTG temperature dips. Neither of these adjacent forward-model approaches is the fixed-time multigrating feasible-set theorem here.

## Experimental context

- [Huberman et al., *Observation of second sound in graphite at temperatures above 100 K*](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.aav3548) (2019).
- [Ding et al., *Observation of second sound in graphite over 200 K*](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-021-27907-z) (2022); Eq. 9 supplies a heat-capacity-weighted ballistic reference.
- [Xie et al., *Room-temperature second sound in isotopically pure graphite*](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-026-70807-3) (2026); tunable transient-grating measurements and material-specific modeling.

These measurements establish the scientific context only. No native traces, modal calibration, common time-zero or optical-to-energy transfer calibration were used to perform an experimental test in this note.

## Mathematical sources

- [V. Strassen, *The existence of probability measures with given marginals*](https://doi.org/10.1214/aoms/1177700153), *Annals of Mathematical Statistics* 36 (1965), Theorem 8. The two-marginal specialization is the martingale coupling used for the fixed-time path realization.
- [M. Sion, *On general minimax theorems*](https://doi.org/10.2140/pjm.1958.8.171), *Pacific Journal of Mathematics* 8 (1958), 171–176, [publisher PDF](https://msp.org/pjm/1958/8-1/pjm-v8-n1-p14-s.pdf). This supplies the affine minimax step in the multigrating supporting-hyperplane formula.
