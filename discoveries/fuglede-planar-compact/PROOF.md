# Proof and finite certificates

Tao Zhang’s 2026-07-17 preprint constructs two size-60 seeds in G = Z_60 × Z_12 and lifts them to counterexamples in real dimension two. Corollary 4.5 displays a 564,540-square spectral non-tile, and Corollary 3.5 uses unspecified sufficiently large repeats for a nonspectral tile. Credit Zhang for the seeds and original real-plane counterexamples. The findings here have yet to receive external review. There is no minimality or priority claim. [Zhang, arXiv v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.15632v1)

The finite seeds are reconstructed from the formulas in Zhang’s preprint and checked by two independent implementations, [`verify.js`](verify.js) and [`independent-verify.js`](independent-verify.js). The lattice–Euclidean spectral equivalence used on both sides is [Kolountzakis and Matolcsi, Tiles with no spectra, Theorem 4.2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0406127#page=7).

### 4,320 unit squares, spectral and non-tiling

Map Zhang's A_+ by (x,y)↦(2x+15y−23 mod 60,x+8y mod 12) and use canonical representatives S. This is affine bijective, with invertible linear map mod 12 and factor 2 on x mod 5. Let F = S + {(60i,12j): 0≤i<8, 0≤j<9}. The 4,320 squares are F+[0,1)^2. All squares are indexed by their distinct integer anchor points; the count is not a claim about connected components. Exact cyclotomic division checks all 1,770 pairs in Zhang's finite spectrum. After the group transform, a finite spectral index (a,b) gives frequencies ((a+s/8)/60,(b+t/9)/12) for 0≤s<8, 0≤t<9. Unequal repeat indices give a vanishing geometric sum. Equal repeat indices with distinct finite labels give a vanishing seed sum. The 4,320 frequencies are distinct and exhaust the dimension. Adding Z^2 gives a Euclidean spectrum.

The finite packing number of A_+ is exactly 10. A_+−A_+ covers every nonzero Z_5 layer in the CRT decomposition. Its zero layer is the union of three V_i−V_i differences in Z_12^2. Packing centers all lie in one layer. Exact clique search gives a 10-center witness and excludes eleven in 66,684 nodes. A separately structured search anchors (0,0), observes that an eleven-center packing uses eleven distinct x-coordinates, and rules out each possible missing x via y-assignment propagation. The packing number transfers to S.

The replay checks all 171,981 differences d in [-436,436]×[-98,98], the pair-difference rectangle of a 437×99 lattice window. For each of 146,755 modular conflicts (including zero), some actual S−S difference e has |d_x−e_x|≤420 and |d_y−e_y|≤96. Because deviations are multiples of the periods, d belongs to F−F. Thus centers from any packing inside one such window give distinct residues forming a finite packing and there are at most ten. Partitioning the lattice into these windows caps center density at 10/43,263. A tiling by F needs density 1/4,320 by fixed-width boundary counting. Since 43,263>43,200 they contradict. A hypothetical Euclidean tiling by the half-open squares induces a lattice tiling by intersecting with a generic translated integer grid. Avoid the null overlap and gap set and the boundaries of each translated cell by countable union. The translate set is countable because its translates have disjoint nonempty interiors. Every square in a given translate meets the grid at the same offset, giving translates of F on the grid. Repeated sampled origins would overlap on the grid, so the resulting integer origin set is a genuine lattice tiling.

This packing-density proof gives a construction with about 131 times fewer unit squares than Zhang’s displayed example. It supplies an independent lift from the same finite seed. It has no bearing on the user’s F13 or F17 finite-field grids.

### 37,800 unit squares, tiling and nonspectral

The check reconstructs A_- and B_- from Zhang. Their sums cover all 720 residues, and the factor sizes 60 and 12 make the sum unique. In the dual CRT zero layer, A_-'s exact Fourier zeros coincide with the common zeros of the three B_i. Their exact graph maximum is 10. A finite spectrum of size 60 would have at least 12 frequencies in one dual Z_5 layer, so A_- is nonspectral.

Take the centered representatives S_- in [-30,29]×[-6,5] of the affine image (x,y)↦(7x+20y−9 mod 60,x+y mod 12). Form F_- = S_- + {(60i,12j): 0≤i<30, 0≤j<21}, or 37,800 squares after adding [0,1)^2. The linear image of B_- repeated with periods (1800,252) tiles with F_- in the lattice. Modulo (60,12), a sum determines the seed and complement residues uniquely. The repeat indices then exhaust the enlarged period fiber uniquely.

There are 191 nonzero finite Fourier coefficients and each has magnitude greater than 131/25. The rational replay gives a uniform squared lower bound 2,750,150,124 / 100,000,000, via Machin's identity, alternating arctan bounds for pi and a rational Taylor remainder for cosine. The affine map permutes the finite Fourier magnitudes. The transformed seed has sums of absolute coordinates 720 and 108.

In a hypothetical spectrum of F_- write the frequencies as ((a+r)/60,(b+s)/12), with r,s in [0,1). Divide (r,s) into 30×21 bins. The equal-bin averaging idea is from Kolountzakis and Matolcsi, Theorem 4.1; the new step is a concrete parameter certificate. One bin contains at least 60 frequencies. For distinct frequencies in that bin, differences of fractional parts lie strictly within (−1/30,1/30) and (−1/21,1/21), so neither repeat sum vanishes. The seed Fourier sum vanishes at the full difference. Replacing r,s by zero changes its value by at most 2π(12/30+9/21), strictly below 1276/245 < 131/25. Every rounded pair difference is therefore an exact finite Fourier zero and cannot be the zero character. The rounded frequencies give a spectrum of the nonspectral seed, a contradiction. Theorem 4.2 gives Euclidean nonspectrality for the square union.
