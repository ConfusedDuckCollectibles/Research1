# Independent audit: smaller rectangular lifts of Zhang’s planar Fuglede examples

**Pass.** The 4,320-square spectral non-tile and the 37,800-square nonspectral tile are exact independently checked constructions from Tao Zhang’s finite seeds. They are smaller, quantitative lifts, not new finite seeds, not a first disproof, and not claims of minimum complexity. The 4,320 compares to Zhang’s explicit 564,540 squares (roughly a factor 131). The 37,800 is an explicit sufficient size where Zhang v1 Corollary 3.5 gives “sufficiently large,” not a demonstrated ratio to a previously proved best finite count.

## Primary source and exact finite data

Read [Tao Zhang, arXiv:2607.15632v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2607.15632v1), §§3.1–3.2 and §§4.1–4.2. Its July 17 v1 is the sole version listed by the inspected arXiv abstract; the manuscript HTML states CC BY 4.0. The audit independently encoded the published 12-value graph \(E\), the three \(B_i\) from (15), \(V_0,V_1,V_2\) from §4.1, layer assignments \(0,2,2,1,1\) and \(0,0,0,2,1\), and the point/frequency CRT from (22)–(24), (29)–(30). The point formula \(x+12(3(t-x))\bmod60\) equals the printed \(25x+36t\) modulo60 and agrees with the natural group isomorphism \(\mathbb Z_{12}^2\times\mathbb Z_5\cong\mathbb Z_{60}\times\mathbb Z_{12}\). The dual CRT is \((5u+12r,v)\), verified by substitution in the character pairing. The raw 1,770 spectral pair differences all vanish exactly; checked also on the transformed seed with the contragrediently transformed spectrum. The \(60\cdot12\) seed/complement sums occupy all 720 residues exactly, before and after the stated negative affine map.

The positive map \(L_+(x,y)=(2x+15y,x+8y)\) and negative \(L_-(x,y)=(7x+20y,x+y)\) define group maps: the coefficients allow each coordinate period to act trivially where needed. On the CRT mod12 coordinates their determinants are \(+1,-1\) modulo12; on mod5 each multiplies by 2. Both are therefore bijective, and adding fixed translations to the seeds preserves properties. The negative tile complement is transformed by **the linear part alone** (or can itself be shifted independently, which only shifts the full tiling), not interpreted as an extra copy of the seed's shift in an equality with fixed origins. The transformed positive Fourier check used \(L_+^*(U,V)=(2U+5V,3U+8V)\) in the native frequency units. Independent exact Fourier tests used the 16-element tensor basis \(\zeta_{12}^i\zeta_5^j\) for \(0\le i,j<4\), with \(\zeta_{60}^k=\zeta_{12}^{5k}\zeta_5^{3k}\), not the originating long-division routine.

## One exact finite Cayley graph for both bounds

In positive \(A_+-A_+\) every nonzero \(\mathbb Z_5\) layer is full. Hence translates in a packing all have the same CRT layer, and only the common compatibility of the \(V_i-V_i\) in \(H=\mathbb Z_{12}^2\) matters. Negative Fourier zeros in the dual CRT layer zero agree exactly with the common zeros of the three \(B_i\), checked at all 144 frequencies. In fact the two compatibility graphs on \(H\) are literally identical. For \(i=0,1\), the nonzero Fourier supports of \(B_i\) are respectively \(u=0\) and \(u=v\), just \(V_i-V_i\). From Zhang’s product (19) the support of \(B_2\) is \(v\) even, \(u+v\equiv0\bmod3\), \(u\not\equiv2\bmod4\). This is \(H_0\cup(H_0+c)\cup(H_0-c)=V_2-V_2\): the first layer \(u\equiv0\bmod4\) has exactly the six \((4k,2k)\), and \(c=(1,2)\) or \(-c\) shifts to \(u\equiv1,3\bmod4\) while preserving the two other conditions.

This Cayley graph has exactly 105 allowed nonzero differences. Any clique has at most one point for each \(x\). An 11-clique can be normalized to have missing \(x=11\) by translation in \(x\), then the occupied \(x=0\) point can be made \((0,0)\) by a separate \(y\)-translation. The independent fixed-order exact height search for that **single** normalized 11-row root failed after 61,092 visits, and separately found the explicit ten-clique in the transcript. Thus positive packing maximum10 and negative common spectrum maximum10. A putative size60 negative finite spectrum has ≥12 frequencies in at least one of five dual CRT layers, impossible. A larger clique than 11 would contain an 11-clique, so no extra coverage assumption is needed.

## 4,320-square lift and window transfer

For the transformed positive representatives \(S\), let \(F=S+\{(60i,12j):0\le i<8,\ 0\le j<9\}\). Distinct period boxes and residues give \(|F|=4320\). If \((a,b)\) ranges over the contragredient 60-spectrum, use lattice frequencies \(((a+s/8)/60,(b+t/9)/12)\). They are distinct modulo \(\mathbb Z^2\); when repeat indices differ one geometric factor vanishes, and when they agree a nonzero finite seed difference is required and vanishes. Therefore 4,320 orthogonal vectors form a basis; [Kolountzakis–Matolcsi, Theorem 4.2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0406127#page=7) gives the spectrum extended by \(\mathbb Z^2\).

In a 437 by 99 window, enumerate the difference rectangle \([-436,436]\times[-98,98]\). Independently formed actual differences by adding every allowed \((60i,12j)\) with \(|i|\le7, |j|\le8\) to every unique actual \(S-S\) difference; tested equality with modular-conflict membership on the entire rectangle. All 146,755 of 171,981 tested vectors at modular conflict are actual differences, including all period-repeated residues and zero. Thus two integer packing origins in this window cannot even have the same residue, and distinct residues must pack the finite seed. There are at most10 origins per window. Partition the lattice into these windows: packing origin density at most \(10/43263\), strictly below \(1/4320\). For clarity, a lattice tiling necessarily has origin density \(1/|F|\): counting point cover in a large rectangle and the same rectangle shrunk/enlarged by the fixed sup-norm support of \(F\) bounds center counts with relative error tending to zero. One can also directly use rectangles composed of \(k^2\) windows and compare the \(43263k^2-O(k)\) interior points to at most \(4320\cdot10k^2\) covered by internal centers.

If the unit-square union tiled the plane even only almost everywhere, the presumed translation set would be locally finite: two distinct translations at sup-norm distance less than one would overlap even the same chosen open cell in positive measure. Choose a shift of the integer grid avoiding the null exceptional set, all individual translated cell boundaries, and integer translates of these null sets; the countable union is still null. For any one translate, each cell hits this grid exactly once at the same offset modulo the integer shape, giving an integer translate of \(F\) after identifying the grid with \(\mathbb Z^2\). The resulting copies are disjoint and cover; offsets of different tiling copies cannot coincide. This contradicts the exact lattice density bound and is independent of Zhang’s large-window threshold.

## 37,800-square lift and quantitative nonspectrality

Use transformed centered representatives \(T\) and \(F_-=T+\{(60i,12j):0\le i<30,\ 0\le j<21\}\). With the linear image of the 12-point complement, periodic modulo \(1800\times252\), their sums enumerate all 453,600 period residues exactly once (replayed directly). This proves lattice and Euclidean tiling. The orthogonality obstruction is independent of where those centered cells straddle the original period box.

Exactly 191 Fourier coefficients of the centered seed are nonzero on the finite dual lattice. Independent directed rational cosine arithmetic, with alternating arctangent bounds for Machin’s identity and a bounded degree-29 Taylor remainder (the \(x^{29}\) coefficient is zero), gave squared lower bound \(2750155278840/10^{11}\) for each nonzero coefficient, strictly larger than \((131/25)^2\). This rechecks and slightly tightens the originating rational floor, not the integer square count. Centered coordinate absolute sums are 720 and 108.

If \(F_-\) had a lattice spectrum, write each frequency in \([0,1)^2\) as \(((a+r)/60,(b+s)/12)\), with the integer parts \(a,b\) and remainders \(r,s\). Among 37,800 frequencies a single \(30\times21\) half-open bin of the remainder torus contains at least60. Distinct frequencies there differ in each remainder coordinate by strictly less than \(1/30,1/21\). Both finite repeat factors are nonzero at every such pair difference; zero remainder difference makes a repeat factor its size, not zero. By orthogonality the seed Fourier factor at the full shifted pair difference is zero. Using \(|e^{it}-1|\le |t|\), rounding both remainders away changes the seed coefficient by strictly less than \(2\pi(12/30+9/21)<1276/245<131/25\). Thus any rounded pair difference must be an exact finite Fourier zero, not merely small. A zero character is nonzero Fourier value (60), hence no two rounded frequencies coincide; they give a forbidden 60-point finite spectrum. The lattice-to-Euclidean equivalence of [Kolountzakis–Matolcsi, Theorem 4.2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0406127#page=7) finishes. The bin/rounding idea is their Theorem 4.1, now given a concrete threshold and separate coordinate factors; do not claim a new general transference principle.
