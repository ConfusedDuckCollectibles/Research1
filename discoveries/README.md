# Discoveries

Original, reproducible findings whose scope and limits are documented.

- [Least periods for power-of-two wrapped grids](wrapped-grid/): proves that half the width squared is the shortest repeat length in the toric no-three-in-line problem at every power-of-two width. Includes the proof, prior work, and a dependency-free verifier.
- [Timing and period-fit checks for 3I/ATLAS](atlas-timing-replay/): reproducibility note on two preprint versions. Replays the reported light-curve fits and measures the effect of substituting archived Hubble exposure times. Does not estimate the comet’s true spin.
- [Local barriers for the Calderbank–Fishburn 236-cap](ternary-cap-local-barriers/): exact checks rule out enlarging this construction with at most 16 deletions or with changes confined to one proper affine subspace. The global size limit is unchanged.
- [A closed construction route for Ramsey R(3,10)](ramsey-r310-kalbfleisch/): rules out every 40-vertex candidate that contains the Kalbfleisch graph. Includes the proof and a replay of all three certified cases; the Ramsey number itself remains 40 or 41.
- [A polynomial obstruction for Erdős–Straus](erdos-straus-polynomial-descent/): proves that fixed Gaussian-norm polynomial identities cannot use the two coordinates independently to produce positive denominators; the conjecture remains open.
- [Arbitrary replacements in the sixteen-runner problem](lonely-runner-sixteen-replacements/): proves a strict gap above 1/16 after exactly two, three, four, or five replacements of the standard speeds by distinct larger integers, with no upper limit on the replacements. The general case remains open.
- [A tighter finite bound for Frankl-complete configurations](frankl-four-set-bound/): proves that any 24 distinct four-element subsets of ten points force an element of their support to appear in at least half the sets of every union-closed extension. The exact threshold and general conjecture remain open.
- [Two excluded families in the perfect cuboid problem](perfect-cuboid-two-families/): proves that neither of two named infinite families of near-perfect boxes can yield a perfect cuboid. Other constructions and the general problem remain open.
- [A tighter bound for the 42 × 42 Zarankiewicz problem](zarankiewicz-42/): proves at most 283 marked cells without a rectangle of four marked corners, improving the previous upper bound of 287 found in the literature. A 279-cell example leaves the exact value open.
- [Two rectangle-free grid bounds near order ten](zarankiewicz-order-ten/): proves ceilings of 1211 for 111 × 111 and 1197 for 110 × 110. The linked net-transfer proof also improves an earlier bound along infinitely many grid sizes tied to nonexistent finite planes. An [unequal-side extension](zarankiewicz-order-ten/rectangle-bound.md) gives 1203 for 110 × 111. None of these maxima is known exactly.
- [An all-size improvement for distinct subset sums](distinct-subset-sums-11/bandwidth-gap.md): for every size from six upward, the bandwidth lower bound can never be attained. The largest integer must exceed it by at least one; for 1-separated real sums the [sharper gap is at least 1/5](distinct-subset-sums-11/sharper-real-gap.md). The [original eleven-integer proof](distinct-subset-sums-11/) gives the example 527.
- [A tighter reset bound for three Černý configurations](cerny-parity-bound/): improves the directly derived leading coefficient from 7/4 to 5/4 for three cases left open in a recent paper. A parity test proves the conjectured bound for a subfamily, not for all three cases or for arbitrary automata.
- [Bell-test margin near an asymmetric detector-loss threshold](chsh-detector-loss-crossover/): gives a uniform formula for the transition between known quadratic and cubic regimes. When the better detector's residual loss and the threshold clearance are equal in tilt units, the leading margin is only 36.1% of the perfect-detector approximation. This is theory under an independent loss model, not a new threshold or experiment.
- [A finite size limit for asymmetric CSS quantum codes](css-small-block-bound/): proves when repetition blocks force a lower qubit count, how a rank correction accounts for logical operators on smaller blocks, and how to recognize the condition even in a heavier check presentation. No hardware or code-record claim.
- [A directed-triangle obstruction at the one-third threshold](caccetta-haggkvist-critical-support/): with three times r vertices and respective outdegree/indegree floors r and r−1, a counterexample requires at least seven critical vertices with a directed cycle. Uses the proved second-neighborhood theorem; the unrestricted conjecture remains open.
- [Hidden dissipation from three binary snapshots](three-snapshot-dissipation/): applies an existing scalar formula for marked excursions to three binary observations at fixed lag, with a same-data comparison and a sharp limiting family. On an exact constructed example the bound captures 94.4% of the true model rate versus 74.8% for a prior bound from the same observations. No experimental or general estimator claim.
- [Finite exact-resonance groups for surface waves](gravity-capillary-resonance/): for mixed deep gravity–capillary waves on rational periodic rectangles, proves that each arithmetic group has finitely many modes and a separate conserved action. Exact-resonant models cannot move finite starting data to arbitrarily short wavelengths under these conditions; near-resonance and physical dynamics are outside scope.
- [Three points for three-direction polygons](hadwiger-debrunner-three-strip/): proves a sharp three-point bound when every four shapes contain three with a common point and all polygon sides use at most three fixed directions. Generalizes the one-orientation triangle case, not the unrestricted planar problem.
- [More dissipation evidence from short multi-category records](multicategory-window-dissipation/): constructs a reversible-looking Markov model in which the first-exit bound is at least 4.55× the earlier optimized bound on the same censored table. Binary recodings give zero, and the richer earlier uncensored method still does better when the full record is available.
- [When the flag–volume upper bound is exact](flag-volume-equality/): proves an all-face geometric equality criterion, settles the simple and Hansen classes in all dimensions, and replays the centrally symmetric classification through dimension seven on published catalogue data. The unrestricted classification remains open.
- [Dense active wave resonances at finite depth](dense-water-wave-resonances/): at any fixed positive surface tension on a square torus, constructs and counts exact noncollinear three-wave matches with nonzero coupling. Proves a scalar cubic simplification fails on a residual, dimension-zero set of nonresonant depths, not a failure of the PDE.
- [Six symmetry orders excluded for a Hadamard 2396 construction](hadamard-2396-width28/): in the published width-28 template, proves that a common pure scalar multiplier on the order-37 coordinate can have order only 1, 2, or 3, for all eligible abelian groups. Neither this construction without the symmetry nor order 2396 itself is ruled out.
- [A sharp two-use feedback separation for phase sensing](two-use-quantum-metrology/): proves analytically that a definite-order parity-reset protocol beats the full ancilla-assisted parallel class for all nonzero survival pairs except both noiseless, with exact rational examples. Prior equal-case and selected unequal numerical comparisons are credited; no experiment or asymptotic claim.
- [A 22-contact condition for five-dimensional kissing configurations](kissing-five-partial-24-cell/): proves a sharp cap of 40 if 22 contacts are vertices of one regular 24-cell. With 23, attaining 40 forces the missing vertex back. The unrestricted five-dimensional kissing number is still open.
- [What the walls change in a mixing flow](couette-wall-locality/): proves a sharp finite-width error for passive-scalar mixing with arbitrary bounded shear histories, identifies the bulk singular threshold, and gives an exact reflecting-wall reversal example with over four times the unbounded-model worst residual. Not a laboratory or turbulent-flow result.
- [What repeated heating can tell you about steady warming](finite-frequency-thermal/): gives the exact worst-case ambiguity for a surface heat-loss coefficient from finitely many complex measurements in reciprocal passive heat networks with known capacity, with attainable or limiting witnesses and a matching estimator. Not an Earth feedback estimate.
- [Two gravitational lenses, exactly the same signal along one track](wave-lens-track/): constructs different positive finite-mass lenses with identical calibrated broadband wave amplification along an entire straight source track, and identifies extra measurement geometry that removes the ambiguity under stated assumptions. Exact thin-lens theory, not an observed event.
- [A 21-line obstruction in dimension 18](equiangular-18-pasch-wings/): the specified 21-line Pasch-wing patterns cap an arbitrary equiangular extension at 57, removing the four additional anchors in earlier proofs. Two exact enumerations agree; the unrestricted 57–59 range is unchanged.
- [The storage problem hidden inside an average](robust-storage-chronology/): exact robust feasibility and capacity for a scalar lossy store from coarse chronological totals and power bounds. A certified interior cut refutes a boundary-only rule, and separately metered surplus/deficit can change the verdict. Not a grid reliability study.
- [A two-grating test for how heat travels](grating-positive-paths/): gives a sharp constraint and exact fixed-time response set for calibrated positive carrier paths. A standard collective phonon model violates the constraint with both signals above their ballistic predictions. Model result, not a material measurement.
- [A sharp spectral certificate for compressed set families](spectral-chvatal-near-cones/): proves the inertia form of the spectral Chvátal conjecture for every one-coordinate-compressed downset, including shifted families of mixed rank. It also identifies exactly when the natural fixed cross block fails. The general spectral conjecture remains open.
- [How much can two brief moments reveal about an exoplanet atmosphere?](atmosphere-before-contact/): gives an exact geometric optimum and conditional photon-information ceiling for using only the windows outside opaque-core contact. In a TRAPPIST-1 e-scale benchmark ten ideal visits cannot reach 59.1% power at 5% size. Not a full-transit or atmosphere finding.
