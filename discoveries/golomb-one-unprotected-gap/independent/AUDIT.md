## Proof and precision

Write all positive labelled distances in increasing reference order, with virtual zero first. If \(1,\ldots,g\) are occupied, the length-\(g\) adjacent gap has rank \(g\), so every chamber point has corresponding gap \(\ge g\). Every internal distance is a sum of those protected gaps. Call a distance crossing if its interval spans the designated gap. When translating the right block left by \(b\), every crossing distance falls by \(b\) and every internal distance stays fixed. The only inequalities becoming tighter are those from a lower internal \(i\) to an upper crossing \(c\). Their tightest original slack is precisely \(b=\min(c-i-1)\). The set is nonempty (virtual zero, whole-ruler crossing), its entries are integers \(\ge 0\), and any minimizer is consecutive in the *full* ordered distance list: an intermediate internal or crossing can replace the appropriate endpoint to decrease slack. The shift therefore remains feasible, including positivity. Conversely, for a minimizing pair any feasible chamber point has its crossing distance at least \(i+1=c-b\); the protected gaps outside the crossing interval contribute at least the original \(L-c\). Thus the shift attains the exact length \(L-b\).

If the distinguished gap happens itself to be protected, its own reference value \(M\) is a crossing distance and \(M-1\) is internal (or virtual zero when \(M=1\)), so \(b=0\) and all gaps are protected. Treat this degenerate choice separately when discussing surviving internal prefixes. For proving optimal-face uniqueness, it is sufficient that all *protected adjacent gaps* are unchanged in the candidate and still bounded below in the original chamber: no prefix-survival assertion is needed. Equal length eliminates the exceptional perturbation. Tight rows then impose signed linear inequalities on nonnegative protected perturbations. If all positive entries are already forced zero, the negative entries force further zeros. Complete propagation certifies uniqueness; failure to propagate is inconclusive. The author's code omits the comparison against virtual zero in the propagation phase, which only weakens this sufficient certificate. Do not extrapolate finite uniqueness to all prime powers.

For a cyclic perfect difference set modulo \(v\), a cut of length \(G\) gives length \(v-G\). Every residue \(1\le r<G\) appears as an ordinary distance: a reversed representative would require a length \(v-r>v-G\). The circle gaps are pairwise distinct by uniqueness of ordered modular differences, so a second-largest cut leaves just one gap \(M>G\), and all other gaps are protected. Also \(G-1\) is internal (or virtual zero), while \(M\) is crossing. Hence \(b\le M-G\) and the second-cut chamber optimum is at least \(v-M\), the exact largest-cut length of the *same* circular ruler.

The lower-cut control modulo 31 has circular gaps \(1,10,8,7,2,3\). Therefore its largest-cut length is 21; the chamber of the cut at 3 uniquely attains 19. All five individual gaps are nevertheless blocked for a one-gap reduction, by the tight comparison coefficients. The independent replay evaluates all labelled rows, recomputes the positive dual with total 19, verifies tightness at the new ruler and determinant \(-1\), and independently confirms the largest-cut comparison. There is no implication for the best unconstrained six-mark ruler.

## Prior art and significance

- [Notik, arXiv:2611.22656v1, §§2, 6–7](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2611.22656) provides the formulation, ordinary LP rows/duals and exact all-cut computation through order 47, including nonmaximal shorter optima and ties. Do not present individual finite small-order sample improvements as new undiscovered LP verdicts. In particular, the order-5 modulus-31 candidate belongs to the covered family, even though the prior prose does not give this particular structural certificate.
- [TruReTuring Largest-gap cuts and real Golomb chambers](https://the-omega-institute.github.io/trureturing-mdbook/Blueprint/D5/S3/Geometry/FiniteGeometry/NotikSingerChamber.html) already supplies the entire prefix argument, coordinatewise domination and normalized uniqueness for largest cuts. The single exceptional block formula is a direct, elementary LP consequence of that technique plus an exact two-block translation. I found no matching uniform formula for second cuts in these sources or a bounded exact-phrase search.
- [TruReTuring Bose and Ruzsa chamber assertions, Theorem 1.12 commentary](https://the-omega-institute.github.io/trureturing-mdbook/Blueprint/D5/S3/Geometry/FiniteGeometry/NotikBoseRuzsaChamber.html) contains an earlier *multi-gap* counterexample for Bose: length 29 to 28 by simultaneous half-unit moves. Independent checks confirm **every** gap is singly blocked there too (a tight pair for each is \(11\to12,2\to3,9\to10,0\to1,3\to4,1\to2\)). Thus the interaction phenomenon is not new in general. The modulus-31 control puts it specifically in cyclic perfect difference sets and shows a smaller cut can outperform that set's largest-cut length, which cannot happen at a second cut.
