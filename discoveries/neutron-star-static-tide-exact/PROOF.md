## Exact theorem and scope
There exist two cold isotropic, nonrotating perfect-fluid GR compact-star models, one with a smooth interior barotrope and one with an inhabited first-order transition of density jump ≥3% of the outer-side density (at p>0), whose mass, radius, and static electric quadrupolar Love response are identical. Their density, pressure, enclosed mass and metric match at every radius outside r=a=R/200 (with common asymptotically normalized lapse). Each pure phase satisfies 0<dp/de<1, and each star is strictly linearly radially stable under its usual barotropic radial problem, also for rapid conversion of the interior discontinuity (slow conversion included). Both share a self-bound density jump to vacuum at p=0; “smooth” always means no positive-pressure/INTERNAL jump, not a vanishing surface density. Compactness lies in [0.075,0.087) and model is schematic self-bound, not fit to real nuclear matter, masses/radii, NICER ranges, realistic crust, arbitrary catalogue, dynamical tides, rotation, or microphysics. Chemical equilibrium at one pressure is possible: integrate dn/n=de/(e+p) independently in each phase, align integration constants to make (e+p)/n equal there; piecewise Maxwell p(e) extends by a zero-slope coexistence interval. For smooth comparator the minimum bulk sound speed is positive per fixed model but no assumed externally imposed strong smoothness/speed lower bound on that narrow interval. Phase conversion perturbation law specified below. Off observed density endpoints the EoS can be continued causally; no inference above the realised model range.

## How to use/interpret
Even perfect *same-star* M, R and static quadrupolar tide at one core-penetrating configuration do not in general identify an interior Maxwell jump against causal narrow smooth alternatives, even after the common exterior has been fixed. This is an exact counterexample in a specified barotropic class and not a re-use of an M–R/Λ correlation. It does not say M and R are statistically independent of Lambda, invalidate radius+tide improvements for primary model classes, or say that better/cross-mass/spectral measurements cannot help. A next measurement must add information not functionally constrained by this matched exterior/single configuration (e.g. distinct probing central pressures, independent core-sensitive dynamical modes, or validated EoS smoothness; none shown sufficient). No evidence of an actual phase transition.
