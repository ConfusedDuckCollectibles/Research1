# Priority and provenance audit, November 25, 2026

## Closest antecedents, checked directly

- Paul Concus, [1962 original standing capillary–gravity paper, pp. 568–576](https://electronicsandbooks.com/edt/manual/Magazine/J/Journal%20of%20Fluid%20Mechanics/1962%20Volume%2014/S0022112062001457.pdf), formulates the denominator excluding an integer temporal harmonic of the fundamental, with spatial harmonics in one horizontal coordinate. In [1964 corrigendum, pp. 264–266](https://electronicsandbooks.com/edt/manual/Magazine/J/Journal%20of%20Fluid%20Mechanics/1964%20Volume%2019/S0022112064000702.pdf), especially p. 265, he proves a countable dense set of excluded depths for zero tension and explicitly states that the extension to nonzero relative capillarity has the same result. He also discusses small-divisor/secular limitations and viscosity. Do not claim novelty for the first dense resonant depths, first density for positive surface tension, nor first recognition of nearby small divisors in standing waves. These are temporal-harmonic obstructions, not the fixed-degree three-wave vector momentum condition on a two-dimensional periodic lattice with noncollinear modes. [Abassi–Wilkening §3.1, Lemma 3.1 and Thm 3.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2401.00844v3) repeats Concus's proof for the pure-gravity denominator, gives parity refinements and proves information about measure and small divisors for that different harmonic family.
- Durey and Milewski, [Theorem 1, §3.1, Appendix A](https://arxiv.org/html/2301.02163v2), prove a unique critical depth for each geometrically permitted pure-gravity triad in finite-depth cylinders, including how cavity eigenmode correlations differ from translation-invariant momentum. Their normalized-frequency ratio monotonicity is exactly the elementary mechanism used here and extends to fixed positive dispersion prefactors. They also prohibit the pure-gravity triads for rectangular cylinders. No priority on the uniqueness method.
- Lawrence McGoldrick, [1965 actual three-wave gravity–capillary resonance](https://www.cambridge.org/core/journals/journal-of-fluid-mechanics/article/abs/resonant-interactions-among-capillarygravity-waves/4A07CEAC1951561856D48ADDCB65FF01), and Haudin et al., [2016 full article, Eq. (3)–(5)](https://harvest.aps.org/v2/journals/articles/10.1103/PhysRevE.93.043110/fulltext), establish real-vector wave matching and coupling; the latter gives finite-depth dispersion and verifies laboratory effects. [Aubourg–Mordant](https://arxiv.org/abs/1503.04110) relate nearly collinear/nonlocal mixed interactions to spectral widening. Their continuum or laboratory settings do not prove density of exact lattice-compatible depths at fixed capillarity. No novelty on mixed three-wave coupling itself or on interaction of widely separated scales.
- [Craig–Sulem, Numerical Simulation of Gravity Waves](https://doi.org/10.1006/jcph.1993.1164), [Berti–Feola–Franzoi, especially §§3 and 5 and Lemma 4.4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1905.05424), and the [previous fixed-algebraic-depth companion at Research1 commit 79bb301](https://github.com/ConfusedDuckCollectibles/Research1/tree/79bb3017701a00fb83a68686a62bd39c9d4d16b3/discoveries/gravity-capillary-resonance) supply Hamiltonian/DNO background. Berti–Feola–Franzoi has one horizontal dimension and finitely many cubic resonances for each parameter set there; our high transverse integer b is essential. The previous companion already proves algebraic-depth exclusion for algebraic strengths, a per-list bound on positive exceptional depths, and one isolated explicitly coupled example. It does not use the noncollinear dense sequence, root count or residual null cubic obstruction. The usual absence of shell-breaking exact finite lists for almost every h already follows from its analytic per-list argument.
- [Ionescu–Pusateri](https://arxiv.org/abs/1807.02932) and [Feola–Montalto–Murgante 2026 §§1, 3.1 and 6.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2604.20968v1) handle multi-horizontal-dimensional periodic small divisors in deep water. The latter proves almost-every surface tension (not fixed alpha and variable finite h) lifespan results by retaining controlled quasi-resonances. [Berti–Delort](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1702.04674), and [Berti–Maspero–Murgante](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40818-024-00182-z) develop periodic finite-depth normal forms with a one-dimensional surface and almost every tension, not the fixed-tension exceptional depths at issue. [Giannoulis](https://arxiv.org/abs/1602.00255) studies modulated **continuum** plane packets in finite depth, assuming relevant low-order nonresonance.
- Contemporary alternative mechanisms checked: [Guan, July 2026](https://doi.org/10.1017/jfm.2026.11815), bifurcating noncollinear gravity–capillary standing waves in deep water; [Singla–Gorce–Falcon, 2026](https://doi.org/10.1103/2w88-kh5j), finite-direction confinement, linewidth and wave statistics measured in tanks; [Zuccoli–Kadri, Oct 2026](https://doi.org/10.1017/jfm.2026.12161), an acoustic branch in a finite-depth compressible fluid; [Pezzi Jan 2026 author manuscript](https://catenadiatomica.altervista.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/chain_of-res_capillary_gravity_waves.pdf), continuum collinear chain with cutoffs. None of these observations or computations is a replay of the lattice-depth result, nor are they contradicted by it.

## Claim boundary

The candidate narrow addition is the constructive dense fixed-alpha set of **active noncollinear cubic lattice** resonances, local root asymptotics including the count, and the coupling-controlled residual dimension-zero obstruction to a full cubic Hamiltonian generator. Topological and measure arguments for small divisors are standard, and coupling expansion and normalized ratio monotonicity are classical; the result is the application with the explicit family and the coefficient sign. Queries included dense resonant depth, fixed tension capillary triads, two-dimensional finite-depth torus small divisors, and Liouville/Baire exceptions; no direct overlap with the exact conjunction above was found in this bounded scan. Do not assert priority. No observations, laboratory engineering, external contact or dissemination were performed.
