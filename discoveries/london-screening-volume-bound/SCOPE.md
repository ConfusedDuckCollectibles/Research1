# Scope and prior work

This result concerns a local, linear equilibrium Maxwell–London model. The ball sits in vacuum with uniformly prescribed, nonresponding applied sources. The ordinary permeability does not vary and there are no vortices, independently trapped currents, hysteresis, or cell response in the forward model. The data functional is the normalized trace of the induced dipole response for three orthogonal uniform excitations. An arbitrary single-axis response and an arbitrary known nonspherical enclosure are different optimization problems.

The result proved here is the global bound across arbitrary measurable scalar layouts under a pointwise stiffness cap and an integrated budget; the bound therefore also applies to a support-volume budget. Standard sphere and shell responses and examples of thin layers screening a larger region are older. The exact full exterior match here is between two **radial** profiles in a uniform excitation, not a full electromagnetic boundary-map indistinguishability result. A separately calibrated higher harmonic resolves this pair by a radial single-crossing comparison. Neither the theorem nor its numerical examples measure or classify any real sample.

## Primary sources and comparisons

- [Kittel, Fahy and Louie, Phys. Rev. B 37, 642 (1988)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevB.37.642): thin-layer screening in a cylindrical geometry. We do not claim that shielding by an outer layer is a new mechanism.
- [Prat-Camps et al., IEEE Magnetics Letters (2016)](https://doi.org/10.1109/LMAG.2015.2501281): hollow-sphere demagnetization, including the finite-London shell interior field; an independent check of the radial normalization, not an arbitrary-layout fixed-budget optimization.
- [Brandt, Phys. Rev. B 58, 6523 (1998)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevB.58.6523) and [Grant et al., Phys. Rev. B (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1103/rvkn-1sm8): classical solid-sphere response and more recent fixed-shape penetration-depth calculations.
- [Hofer and Aspelmeyer, Physica Scripta (2019)](https://doi.org/10.1088/1402-4896/ab0c44): analytic finite-depth fields for a homogeneous sphere under quadrupole excitation. This note uses standard harmonic separation to compare two profiles, not to introduce quadrupole response.
- [Laurain, Winckler and Yousept, SIAM J. Control Optim. (2021)](https://doi.org/10.1137/19M1294150): superconductor shape optimization with a Bean critical-state law and an electric-field tracking objective. The governing model and objective differ from the static trace extremum here.

A bounded primary-literature check did not find the same arbitrary-layout fixed-budget trace theorem. Priority has not been established by exhaustive review.
