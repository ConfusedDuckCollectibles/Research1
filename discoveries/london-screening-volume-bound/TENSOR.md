# Tensor London screening: independent audit, positive floor, and exact interior-data converse

An isotropic positive floor changes the optimal fraction and equality threshold explicitly, but does not cure local non-identifiability even with fixed trace and fixed full support. A converse below requires **all five interior quadrupole field maps** and a positive radial reference; it is not an exterior-only uniqueness statement. None of these claims addresses empirical LaH10 stiffness, phase volume, or measurements.

## Definitions and functional analysis (including singular stiffness)

Let V=|B_R|, K a measurable symmetric tensor on B_R and zero outside, with 0≼κ I≼K≼QI on B_R for constants 0≤κ≤Q. Use vacuum permeability everywhere, fixed external sources, linear static local London response in the zero-winding sector, no vortices, independent transport or magnetic background. A_inc is a prescribed Coulomb-gauge representative of the regular source-free incident magnetic field near B_R; only its values on B_R enter the material term. Write A=A_inc+a, with induced a in divergence-free homogeneous W^(1,2)(R³) represented in L⁶. Let
  G_K=closure in L²(B_R;R³) of {K^(1/2)∇φ : φ∈H¹(B_R)}.
Minimize (2μ0)^−1 [||curl a||²+||K^(1/2)(A_inc+a)−w||²] over that a and w∈G_K. This is the same infimum as over actual H¹ phase gradients without closure. Bounded measurable K has a unique bounded measurable nonnegative square root; no K^−1 is used for this problem, including at zero/rank-one cells. Phase minimization is orthogonal projection for each a, and coercivity and strict convexity in a follow from its curl norm on the solenoidal space (Sobolev controls ball L²). The minimized weighted gradient w is unique too. At κ>0 the weighted-gradient subspace is already closed by the ordinary Poincaré inequality after subtracting phase means; at κ=0 actual scalar phase attainment is unnecessary.

Write W=K^(1/2)A−w and j=−K^(1/2)W/μ0, extended by zero. Orthogonality to G_K gives distributional div j=0, including interface conditions; the a variation gives curl curl a=μ0j, equivalently −Δa=μ0j with div a=0. The usual response energy E=−(1/2)∫j·A_inc follows by testing with a and w. For fixed uniform induction B0e, this is −B0 e·m/2, m=(1/2)∫x×j, and the response normalization is the audited scalar one. The trace is the sum over orthonormal applied modes. The forward problem is linear in A_inc and depends boundedly on its ball-L² norm (test the coercive linear equation for a, then use bounded projection to recover j and curl a). Monotonicity of the minimized energy in K holds using unprojected common a,φ trial spaces; comparing different G_K directly would not justify it.

## Exact null family for all regular static magnetic excitations

For x≠0 put P_r=x x^T/|x|², P_t=I−P_r. Values at the origin are immaterial. For any bounded q_t(r)≥0 and bounded measurable q_r(x)≥0, set K=q_t P_t+q_r P_r on B_R. For a homogeneous harmonic solid H of degree ℓ≥1 let B_inc=∇H and A_inc=−x×∇H/(ℓ+1). The identity curl(x×∇H)=−(ℓ+1)∇H follows from homogeneity and ΔH=0. A_inc is tangent, solenoidal and harmonic (the rotation generators x×∇ commute with Δ), and homogeneous of degree ℓ.

The scalar reference q_t I solution is A=f(r)A_inc with zero phase gradient and
  (r^(2ℓ+2)f′)'=r^(2ℓ+2)q_t f.
Indeed Δ(f A_inc)=[f''+(2ℓ+2)f'/r]A_inc. This regular solution starts at f(0)>0 with weighted derivative initially zero; the Volterra integral for f′ proves positivity and f′≥0 on the interval, and gives f′=O(r) at zero. Normalize its vacuum continuation at infinity to 1, which is possible since the exterior limit from this initial normalization is f(R)+Rf′(R)/(2ℓ+1)>0. The decaying correction gives a∈D^(1,2); bounded q_t entails matching of f,f′ at interfaces. The current q_t A is solenoidal even for discontinuous radial q_t: angular divergence vanishes and the radial component is zero (equivalently use Fubini by sphere or radial approximation). In the tensor problem KA=q_t A pointwise regardless of the radial eigenvalue, so the same A with w=0 solves both variational equations. By uniqueness, the full physical B and j inside and out agree with the scalar tangential reference. No derivatives or regularity of q_r are needed; it may depend arbitrarily on angle. In this family E agrees for each input as well.

Finite superpositions follow by linearity. Every curl-free/divergence-free incident field harmonic in a neighborhood of the **closed** sample ball has a potential with regular solid-harmonic expansion there; its toroidal A_inc series converges on the ball in L² (indeed locally smooth with margin). Continuity extends equality to the corresponding infinite sums. This is not a claim for imposed current sources **within** the sample, fluxoid changes, vortices, nonlocal response, ferromagnetic permeability, or dynamic/electric excitations. For incoming solid harmonics of higher degree the external field need be calibrated near the sample, not a polynomial field physically realized throughout infinite space. Only the induced field is compared at fixed external sources.

The rank-deficient schematic example with a/R=98/100 uses K_A=QP_t 1_shell, K_B=K_A+QP_r everywhere. Any-positive-stiffness support fractions are exactly 7351/125000 and 1; integrated traces divided by 3QV are 7351/187500 and 69851/187500. K_B has only a rank-one response in the inner core. This is not isotropic three-direction superconductivity there. It has no measurement/fabrication inference.

**Strictly positive equal-trace local aliases.** Fix 0<κ<c<Q and a smooth bounded h supported in a compact subannulus, with ∫h=0 and h nonzero. For example choose radial smooth χ supported away from zero and R, and h(x)=χ(|x|)x_1/|x|. Compare cI with cI+εhP_r on the ball; for |ε| ||h||∞<min(c−κ,Q−c) the eigenvalues obey the strict floor and cap, and the two tensors have identical exact integrated trace 3cV. Both have full support, are smooth inside (perturbation vanishes near zero and near the boundary), can be made arbitrarily close, and have identical full B,j under every regular magnetic excitation. The space of such perturbations is infinite-dimensional. They are not related by a necessary unknown spatial deformation: the interior fields are equal in the same fixed coordinates. The equal-trace condition is integrated, not prescribed trace at each point. One must not infer a distinct-support-volume alias when imposing the positive floor everywhere in the ball.

## Trace-budget optimum and exhaustive equality cases

Assume first κ<Q. Budget means ∫tr K≤M and feasibility requires M≥3κV. Put
  f=min(1,(M−3κV)/(2(Q−κ)V)),  a=R(1−f)^(1/3), q*(r)=κ+(Q−κ)1_(a,R)(r).
The optimizer K*=κP_r+q*P_t is feasible. Its maximal average dipole is exactly the scalar response of q*I, even though that reference profile may exceed the allowed tensor trace. At κ=0 this is the published scalar shell response at the effective volume fraction M/(2QV), clipped at one. At κ>0 the scalar comparison is a positive uniform core with a saturated outer layer; do not substitute the empty-core analytic formula unchanged. For M beyond saturation the objective stays at the all-Q scalar response.

For uniform input along e_i, the three optimal trial potentials are A_i=(B0/2) f_1(r)(e_i×x), so their outer-product sum is c_1(r)P_t with c_1=B0²r² f_1²/4, strictly increasing. For a complete fixed-ℓ multiplet of equally normalized real solid harmonics an identical representation follows: sum their tangential outer products, rotate the basis, and note that stabilizer rotations at fixed r act transitively in the tangent plane. The sum is c_ℓ(r)P_t with c_ℓ=(positive angular constant)r^(2ℓ)f_ℓ² strictly increasing. Evaluating the actual optimal a_i and zero phase at K* as trials for any K yields the sum-energy upper difference
  (2μ0)^−1 ∫c_ℓ(r)[tr(P_t K)−2q*(r)] dx.
Put d(x)=tr(P_t K)−2κ. Then 0≤d≤2(Q−κ) and ∫d≤M−3κV, because the radial compression is ≥κ. The outer-shell density 2(Q−κ)1_shell maximizes its linear weighted integral: subtract c_ℓ(a) and use pointwise signs and budget. Strict increase forces exactly that density below saturation, and full cap everywhere above it. This holds for each ℓ separately and simultaneously for the same K*. Sum-energy is positive constant times the diagonal static multipole sum if measured in matching normalized channels: exterior vacuum Green identity pairs an incoming r^ℓ toroidal harmonic with its matching decaying r^(-ℓ−1) coefficient, factor (2ℓ+1)R^(2ℓ+1) times the squared incident potential normalization. Anisotropic cross-channel scattering is not optimized term by term, nor is any single excitation asserted maximal.

At or below M=(2Q+κ)V, equality in the objective requires d equal to the shell density and the radial compression =κ a.e. since the entire allowed budget above baseline is consumed by d. Where tangent compression saturates Q, positive semidefiniteness of QI−K implies any tangent vector in its quadratic null cone is in its kernel, forcing cross-block entries zero. Where tangent compression is κ the analogous argument uses K−κI. These and tangent compression traces give uniqueness a.e., including endpoints f=0 and f=1 at exact saturation. For M>(2Q+κ)V, the allowed equality family is precisely
  K=QP_t+q_r(x)P_r, κ≤q_r≤Q, ∫q_r≤M−2QV.
Necessity follows from the strict positive weighted trial saturation, not assuming K a priori radial. Sufficiency follows from the null family. For an exact budget ∫tr K=M in its feasible interval [3κV,3QV], replace the last inequality by equality; a constant radial value realizes it above saturation. For M≥3QV the weak budget may be redundant; exact budget above 3QV is impossible. If κ=Q the only feasible K=QI and no fraction formula with zero denominator is needed. If κ=0, M=0 gives K=0 a.e. For all M, optimizer response and these characterizations hold for each complete ℓ multiplet in the model.

## Converse for a complete quadrupole *interior* dataset

The preceding sufficiency becomes a precise local necessity even for measurable uniformly elliptic competitors when the datum is much stronger than an external field. Let q_t(r)>0 a.e. be the positive bounded radial reference on B_R; assume candidate K≽κ I with κ>0 and K bounded. If the **interior** B fields of the candidate agree with the radial reference for all five independently calibrated degree-2 regular magnetic excitations, then K=q_tP_t+q_rP_r a.e. on the punctured ball. Consequently around such a reference the entire interior equivalence class has exactly the radial scalar freedom (subject to the stated bounds). No such converse is asserted for exterior data, finite sensors, or the degenerate competitors.

The ball-phase minimizer is an actual H¹ gradient here. The common interior B implies the same j by Ampère, and the two total potentials have equal curl on the ball. Hence, for T=I−q_t K^−1 (bounded symmetric) and each reference A_m, the identity j=−q_t A_m/μ0=−K(A_m^#−∇φ_m)/μ0 implies curl(T A_m)=0 in distributions. Write A_m=f_2(r)V_m, with V_m=x×S_m x for a basis S_m of real symmetric trace-zero matrices and the common harmless incident scalar absorbed into the V_m. The columns V_m smoothly span x^⊥ at x≠0: any tangent value of Sx can be realized with a trace-zero symmetric S, and cross with x is an isomorphism on x^⊥. Let V be their 3×5 matrix and P=I_5−V^T(VV^T+P_r)^−1V the smooth projector onto their kernel away from the origin. For any of its five smooth columns c, one has ∑c_m T A_m=0. Curl and the product rule with smooth c, using curl(TA_m)=0, give ∑∇c_m×TA_m=0 distributionally, hence a.e. Differentiating ∑c_m V_m=0 now shows T∑c_m DV_m is symmetric a.e. (the antisymmetric part of T∑V_m(∇c_m)^T is precisely that curl condition up to sign and nonzero f_2). These five columns span the coefficient kernel at every x; after discarding a common null set the symmetry holds for all S such that x×Sx=0 at that point. This removes any differentiation of measurable K.

At x=r e_3 choose such S=diag(B,−tr B) with arbitrary real symmetric 2×2 B, and let J be the quarter-turn on the tangent plane. The derivative h↦h×Sx+x×Sh has zero radial column and output, and tangent block rJ(B+tr(B)I). The latter B+tr(B)I sweeps all symmetric 2×2 matrices C. Requiring T diag(JC,0) symmetric for all C forces the radial-to-tangent row of T zero (C=I), and forces T_tJ symmetric commuting with every symmetric C (take first C=I and then all C). Thus T_tJ is scalar; because T_t is symmetric, that scalar is zero, and T_t=0. Hence K^−1 acts as 1/q_t on the tangent plane with no off-diagonal, giving precisely the asserted class. This necessity uses exact internal B enough to differentiate distributionally for j; it is not a practical inverse procedure and does not rebut successful reconstructions under fixed crystal axes or different measurements.

* [Broun–Huttema, PRB 79 (2009), section IV including Figs. 2–3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/0810.2342), treats anisotropic spheres, but its layered c-axis is the fixed Cartesian z direction and its applied field is in the layer plane; the weak penetration depth changes its computed fields. That is not a freely rotating radial eigendirection of fixed tangent stiffness. [Prozorov](https://arxiv.org/abs/2307.12389) estimates tensor components with the principal crystal axes and cuboidal geometry known. Those forward/inverse restricted models are compatible with the present null family. [Hofer–Aspelmeyer](https://arxiv.org/abs/1806.07096) is prior exact spherical Maxwell–London quadrupole forward theory, not the trace design or full interior tensor equivalence.
* [Sánchez et al. antimagnet](https://doi.org/10.1088/1367-2630/13/9/093034), [Gömöry et al.](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1218316), and [Yampolskii–Genenko](https://arxiv.org/abs/1401.3565) demonstrate related magnetostatic exterior concealment using magnetic shells or permeability/diamagnetism cancellation, including finite London penetration in the latter. These do not keep permeability fixed while changing only an unexcited tensor stiffness eigenvalue, and the usual matching statement concerns exterior fields in specified excitations rather than exact interior equality here.

No coordinate pushforward or degeneracy is needed for the present local positive-stiffness alias; the mechanism is stationary tangential currents. Neither result implies the other.
* [Epstein–Rachh static London](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcp.2021.110892) is relevant to currents, boundary conventions and possible fluxoid/topological conditions. [Laurain–Winckler–Yousept](https://www.uni-due.de/imperia/md/content/mathematik/agyousept/sicon_lwy_2021.pdf) gives distinct critical-state/electric tracking shape optimization. The earlier scalar trace proof in this project and classical spherical-mode calculus supply much of the method. These are model results. They do not establish actual crystallographic texture, sample fraction or calibration in any real device.
