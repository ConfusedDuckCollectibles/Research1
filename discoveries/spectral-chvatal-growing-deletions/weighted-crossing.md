# Fixed-density theorem with arbitrary shared-marker anchors

**Theorem.** The budget-free two-layer equality holds at $n\ge1000,\ D\le n^2/400$. It covers every shared-marker anchor subset for the specified two nested layers, including all-touched patterns. It uses a different weighted complete-layer certificate at a crossing formerly outside the budget. It does **not** remove the bridge budget in the earlier survivor-only residual with arbitrary individual bridge weights. It does not settle general Conjecture H.

## 1. The crossing and weighted rank construction

Use deletion degrees $d_i$, minimum $d_0$, maximum $D$, $K=\binom{n-1}{2}$, and $S_0=n+K$. For a nonempty anchor set $A$ put $k=\min_{i\in A}(d_i-d_0)$. The earlier certified case has $|A|k\le n^2/8$, including $k=0$; empty anchors need no new crossing. On its complement $k>n/8$ since $|A|\le n$. At $(u,v)=(1,k)$, divide the triple and bridge weights by $1+k$. Put $\gamma=1/(1+k)$, $\beta=k/(1+k)$, $p_U=1$ on survivors and $p_U=\beta>0$ on deleted triples, $w_i=\beta$ on anchors and zero elsewhere. If $t_i=K-\gamma d_i,\ M=K-\gamma d_0$, then $w_i\le M-t_i=\gamma(d_i-d_0)$, including every anchor. The **normalized** mass $\alpha=\sum_i w_i\le n\le n^2/8$; the old unnormalized mass is not silently substituted. Put $D'=\gamma D$, $f_U=\sqrt{p_U}$, $\widehat X=\operatorname{diag}(f)X$ for complete triple incidence $X$, and $G=\widehat X^\top\widehat X$. All deleted triples are still present with positive weight here.

Let $\lambda_i=t_i/(M-w_i)$, $L=M-\alpha$, and use the physical $C_i,V_i$ fits with weighted dot product $\sum_U p_U y_U z_U$. $C_i$ is zero on incident triples and has weighted demand $\lambda_j$ at $j\ne i$; $V_i$ is one on incident triples and has weighted demand $t_i e_i$. Fit the $V_i$ at every positive slack $\lambda_i<1$, which can include a **positive bridge**. Matrices of the linear fits lose at most $3D'$ in absolute row-sum norm because only incidence weights at deleted triples are reduced, by $\gamma$. The $C_i$ baseline remains $1/a$ on avoiding rows, $a=\binom{n-2}{2}$. Demand error is a difference of two nonnegative terms, each at most $D'/[(12/25)n^2]$. This is exactly the earlier rank-block estimate with effective degree $D'$ and hypotheses $w_i\le\gamma d_i$, $\alpha\le nD'$, $\alpha\le n^2/8$. In particular $G^{-1}$ has absolute row-sum norm $\le2/(a-6D')$.

Use scaled columns $\widehat C=\operatorname{diag}(f)C$, $\widehat V_i=\operatorname{diag}(f)V_i$. Set $g=f-\widehat C w$, $F=\sum_i(1-\lambda_i)\widehat V_i\widehat V_i^\top/t_i$ over positive slack, $B=\widehat C\operatorname{diag}(w)\widehat C^\top+gg^\top/L+F$. Since $t_i=\lambda_i(M-w_i)$ and $\widehat C^\top\hat x_i=\lambda_i(\mathbf1-e_i)$ at a bridge or $\lambda_i\mathbf1$ without it, direct multiplication gives
$$
 g^\top\hat x_i=L\lambda_i,\qquad
 B\hat x_i=\lambda_i(f-w_i\widehat C_i)+(1-\lambda_i)\widehat V_i .
$$
On $i\in U$ the result is $f_U$. All quantities involving absent columns have zero coefficient. Exact rank cancellation now centers at $ff^\top/M$: with $W=\widehat C-f\mathbf1^\top/M$, the difference is $W\operatorname{diag}(w)W^\top+(Ww)(Ww)^\top/L+F$. Each old term is bounded by the prior calculation with $D'$ because the outside scaling $f_U\le1$ cannot worsen an entrywise bound. The rank error is $\le30D'/n^4$. No PSD inference is made from these rank identities alone.

## 2. Gap, weighted compatible inverse, and exact perturbation

Let $P_*=I+A_{\rm disj}/q-J/K,\ q=\binom{n-4}{2}$ on the complete unweighted layer. It annihilates $X$ and has gap $\mu=1-1/q$ on $\ker X^\top$. Write $\Pi=\widehat X G^{-1}\widehat X^\top,\ E=I-\Pi,\ \Delta=I-\operatorname{diag}(f),\ h=1-\sqrt\beta=\gamma/(1+\sqrt\beta)\le\gamma<8/n$. For $y\in\ker\widehat X^\top$, $X^\top(y-\Delta y)=0$; thus the projection onto the **unweighted** incidence span obeys $\Pi_X y=\Pi_X\Delta y$ and $\|\Pi_X y\|_2\le h\|y\|_2$. Therefore $P_0=EP_*E$ has a gap of at least $\mu(1-h^2)>0.99$ on the **weighted** complement. Compression is not being treated as preserving the full original gap exactly.

The mask $\mathcal M$ locks intersections and the diagonal. Compatibility is now $(Q\widehat X)_{U,i}=0$ for $i\in U$, not the unweighted incidence condition. $Q_0=\mathcal M(I-B-P_0)$ is compatible by the weighted lock identity. Every required row-incidence sum falls inside the mask, so $T(Q)=\mathcal M(EQE)$ preserves compatibility. A symmetric compatible column at $V$ has $(\widehat X^\top Q_{\cdot,V})_i=0$ inside $V$; each outside sum has at most $3(n-3)$ terms with weights $f_U\le1$. Consequently $\|\Pi Q\|_{\max}\le18n\|Q\|_{\max}/(a-6D')$, and absolute column sums of $\Pi$ are at most $6K/(a-6D')$. These are the prior estimates with weighted incidence. They give a max-entry contraction $\rho\le99/320$ on the compatible subspace, hence $Q=T^{-1}Q_0$, and the real bound $\|EQE\|_2\le3K\|Q_0\|_{\max}/(1-\rho)$. Here the row support bound is on the sparse $Q$, not on the generally dense $EQE$.

For first order,
$$
 \Pi P_*=-\widehat X G^{-1} X^\top\Delta P_*
$$
since $X^\top P_*=0$. Each entry of $X^\top\Delta P_*$ has magnitude at most $h(1+D/q)$: at most $D$ deleted terms occur, any one diagonal term is bounded by 1, and all off-diagonals by $1/q$. Use the row norm $6/(a-6D')$ and $q\ge(99/200)n^2$ to obtain first-order coefficient $4975/396<13$ at $h/n^2$. For the quadratic term insert $\Delta$ on **both** sides of $P_*$; the signs cancel. The column norm bound $\|\Delta X\|_{1\to2}\le h\sqrt D$ and $\|P_*\|_2\le2$ give $72h^2D/(a-6D')^2\le(25/32)h^2/n^2<h^2/n^2$. Bounds in this paragraph use $D$, not $D'$, on actual deleted-row counts; $D'$ only controls the Gram and rank fit.

The signed offset in the compatible defect is $J/K-ff^\top/M$, because $P_*=I-J/K$ on locked entries. It can be bounded using the actual signs but cannot be dropped. The two magnitude terms use $2h/M$ and $\gamma d_0/(KM)$, respectively, giving $(5h+0.011\gamma)/n^2$ with exact individual coefficient bounds $25/6$ and $25/2376$. Combine these with rank error $30D'/n^4$, both first-order terms and the quadratic term, using $h\le\gamma\le1$. The rational bound $16043/500<33$ gives $\|Q_0\|_{\max}\le33\gamma/n^2<264/n^3$. Since $3K\le3n^2/2$, the correction is bounded by $3168/5525$ using the exact $\rho$, or $66/115$ using $0.31$. Both are strictly below the certified gap $>0.99$ (the uniform rational lower bound using $h\le8/1000$ is $107414783/107421875$). Thus $P=P_0+EQE$ satisfies the locks and is real PSD. This is an analytic norm estimate, not an SDP or approximate spectral check.

## 3. Physical Gram and return to all ratios

The normalized coupled Gram has old block $B+P$, off-diagonal $\widehat C$, bridge diagonal $\operatorname{diag}(1/w)$, with PSD Schur complement $P+gg^\top/L+F$. Its triple diagonals are 1. Conjugate by $\operatorname{diag}(\operatorname{diag}(1/f),I)$ to the physical Gram, with triple reciprocal diagonals $1/p_U$; intersecting zero entries stay zero. A weighted tight star in physical coordinates consists of $p_U$ on its incident triples and $w_i$ for its bridge if present. Under the congruence the triple part becomes $\hat x_i$, which the normalized Gram sends to $f$ after adding the bridge contribution $w_i\widehat C_i$. Its bridge output is all ones by the fit plus diagonal. Rescaling the triple output returns all ones. A minimum-degree coordinate is always tight and has no positive bridge because $w_i\le M-t_i=0$. This yields theta $M$ by the weighted-star Cauchy–Schwarz certificate and the matching star lower bound.

Undo the factor $1+k$ and add the full lower ranks, each at weight $1+k$, giving crossing cap $s=(1+k)S_0-d_0$. In the dual Gram the bridge block is $tI-J$, $t=s/k$; its size is $|A|$ and the desired marker inner products are all $-1$. The squared projection length $|A|/(t-|A|)$ is at most the required $t-1$ because $s-(|A|+1)k=S_0-d_0+k(S_0-|A|-1)>0$. All other old faces are disjoint from the marker; no fixed entries arise for them. Thus the marker can be added without increasing the cap. The crossing lower bound is the minimum-degree old star. Pure inner $S_0-d_0$, pure outer $S_0+1$ for nonempty $A$ (otherwise $S_0$), and the small-crossing and $k=0$ cases are inherited. Subadditivity and homogeneity interpolate with the crossing; the corresponding stars supply equality on each ray. Thus the exact answer is $S_0(u+v)-d_0u+\max(0,v-ku)$, omitting the correction for empty anchors. Zero weights on outer faces at the pure inner endpoint are omitted, not divided by.

## 4. Independent exact controls and above-budget witness

The [standalone exact replay](weighted-crossing-replay.cjs) and its [expected output](weighted-crossing-expected.json) need only Node. In the distinct exact $n=10$ control delete all cyclic translates of $\{0,1,3\}$ except shifts 0 and 9. All coordinates are touched. Select anchors $\{1,4,7\}$, giving $k=1,\beta=1/2$, one tight bridge and two positive-slack bridges. Exact arithmetic in $\mathbb{Q}(\sqrt2)$ compares direct dense projections and the separate $\Delta$-incidence identities at all 14,400 entries each for $\Pi P_*$ and $\Pi P_*\Pi$. It checks 1,200 weighted null incidence entries of $P_0$, 360 normalized incidence locks and 360 actual mask compatibilities. The sign control omitting $J/K-ff^\top/M$ breaks all 360. An explicit incidence trade has $\|y\|^2=5$, unweighted projection squared $1/4-\sqrt2/6>0$, and rayleigh numerator $59/12+\sqrt2/18$, above the certified lower $-7/3+14\sqrt2/3$. This is an exact identity/gap control, not the uniform positivity theorem at $n=10$.

Above-budget all-touched example: in the earlier $n=1020=60\cdot17$ tripartite block construction delete all 180 products of three 17-point blocks, except restore one product. It leaves $879{,}427$ deletions, $D=2601=n^2/400,\ d_0=2312$, and 969 old coordinates of positive surplus $k=289$. Choose **all 969** as anchors. Its actual original crossing bridge mass is $280{,}041>130{,}050=n^2/8$, while its normalized mass is $280041/290<1020$ and $D'=2601/290$. The crossing star value is $150{,}708{,}078$, marker mass 280,330, and the analytic exact parameters certify a correction below .237764 with gap above .999986. This is a genuine beyond-budget case, not a limiting or pure-inner control. The underlying deletion density tends to 0.49722%; the theorem does not allow arbitrary downsets or nearly vanishing triple survival.

The [fixed-density residual construction](fixed-density.md) remains valid in its original stated scope. [Ellis–Filmus–Friedgut §5](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.28404v3#S5) is the scope comparator, not evidence of general H or novelty priority.
