# Independent restoration: synthetic wake-bout reporting

Run `python3 check.py` from this directory (standard-library Python 3.10+). The replay uses only synthetic tracks, not device data, patient data, or the original search. Literature is not an input to the replay.

## Conventions and matching

Two finite synchronized binary sequences, reference and prediction, both start and end in S. The paired word is over (SS, SW, WS, WW). A reported window histogram counts **all** occurrences of every contiguous paired word of each length 1 through K, not their locations. A dwell histogram counts maximal runs by stage and length. A wake bout means a maximal W run of at least 10 epochs, with 30-second epochs (declared endpoint; not a clinical standard). Compute event precision and recall by maximum-cardinality one-to-one matching of positive-overlap intervals satisfying IoU >= 1/2. Empty denominator conventions are irrelevant for all witnesses.

For inclusive 1/2, the graph already consists of isolated edges: if one run R were incident to two opposite runs, each intersection would have length at least |R|/2. Equality would force both opposite runs inside R and their union to fill R, contradicting their separating non-W epoch. This applies in both directions. For arbitrary thresholds strictly below 1/2 uniqueness can fail: two length-L fragments separated by one epoch inside a reference run have IoU L/(2L+1) each. The checker uses augmenting-path matching independently and verifies uniqueness, including an equality example (two length-12 runs shifted by four).

## Stage 1: moving reference but closed form

With L=16, six blocks, and a gap of 90 common S epochs before and after each block, put W length L at the reference block's beginning and the prediction W length L at right shift L-x. The paired non-S/S pattern is WS^(L-x), WW^x, SW^(L-x), where the parenthesized superscripts are lengths of symbols. Use 960 epochs, pad with common S. Compare x=(10,10,10,10,10,10) to (11,11,11,11,11,5). Overlaps lie in [4,12], both total 60. For words through length five the runs separating successive boundaries are at least four epochs, so a word crosses at most one boundary; the same symbol totals, same directed transitions and same endpoint symbols give all word counts. Both tracks have six length-16 wake runs. The report is not required to fix sleep dwell or the whole reference in this stage. The confusion is (828,36,36,60); accuracy 37/40 and epoch wake sensitivity 5/8. Qualifying shifts need 3x>=2L; the hits are 0 versus 5 of 6.

In the declared class with B equal-length intervals and allowed integer x in [a,b], total Sx, threshold q with a<q<=b, h hits are possible exactly when
`h*q+(B-h)*a <= Sx <= h*b+(B-h)*(q-1)`.
Each integer between these extremes is attainable by distributing increments across the B interval-valued choices. If q<=a or q>b the only possible hit count is B or 0 respectively, provided the total is feasible. The fixture's exact support is all 0..5. This is a sharp identified set **within the stated shift class**, not a sharp upper bound among all tracks producing those reports.

## Any declared finite K: the high-accuracy split/merge family

Take integers m>=2, K>=1 and T>=max(10,K-1). Put c_i=# ordered (u,v) in {0,...,m-1}^2 with u+v=i, zero outside 0..2m-2. The two multisets of fragment-length pairs are:
- A: (T+i,T+i+2) with multiplicity c_i.
- B: (T+i+1,T+i+1) with multiplicity c_i, except replace one central (T+m,T+m) by (T,T+2m).

Both have m^2 pairs and identical pair-sum multisets. Their pooled fragment-length multisets also agree: the uncorrected B minus A multiplicity at T+k is 2 c_(k-1)-c_k-c_(k-2), equal to +2 at k=m, -1 at k=0 and k=2m, and zero otherwise. The replacement cancels this difference without altering a pair sum.

Sort both by pair sum and order ties independently (any tie order works for the proof). For each (p,q), the reference in a split block is W^(p+q+1), the prediction W^p S W^q. Use a common-S gap G>=K before, between, and after all blocks. The paired W/W stack surrounds the sole W/S epoch by p and q, both >=K-1. Hence no length-<=K window containing that mismatch sees a block boundary; their counts only see a shared W/W neighborhood, including when p=K-1. Windows not affected by the singleton are what one gets by starting with the common all-W/W block then removing the same length-k stack from each affected k-word histogram. The blocks have identical total lengths in order, so the unsplit reference is exactly fixed. The fragment and full-bout histograms agree between worlds; sleep-dwell histograms agree too because inter-block gaps are unchanged and split channels have one extra one-epoch S run per split block. To equalize wake count and duration between channels while retaining cross-world equality of their marginal reports, append an identically constructed second group of blocks with reference and prediction swapped. This **does not** preserve the entire reference track between worlds in the balanced version; the exact-fixed-reference version is the first group alone.

Each block contributes one qualifying match iff max(p,q)/(p+q+1)>=1/2, equivalently |p-q|>=1, because all fragments are eligible and separated. A has all different-sized pairs; B has only one. Thus the balanced versions have each denominator 3m^2, and matched fractions 2/3 and 2/(3m^2). The fixed-reference unbalanced variant has denominators m^2 and 2m^2, with recalls 1 and 1/m^2 and precisions 1/2 and 1/(2m^2); it does not claim zero compared-channel wake-count bias. The control m=1 makes the two worlds literally coincide.

For the balanced case each mismatch direction occurs m^2 times, and WW = 2m^2(2T+2m) from the exact mean of u+v. Wake epoch precision and recall each equal 1-1/(4T+4m+1). Taking G=2T+2m+1 (or any larger G) also drives sleep epoch precision and recall and overall agreement toward one while leaving bout scores unchanged; this does not need rare-wake class imbalance. For m=2,T=10,N=960 (pad both worlds identically with trailing common sleep), (SS,SW,WS,WW)=(760,4,4,192), both channels have 98 wake minutes and 12 bouts, 99.17% accuracy and 48/49 wake epoch recall; matched fractions are 8/12 and 2/12. K=11 is covered. For arbitrarily small desired gap slack, choose m large; for arbitrarily good epoch rates choose T large after m and K. This is not a fixed-length asymptotic. The mechanism lies at IoU 1/2: wrong-side and right-side overlaps converge to 1/2 with large T, so do not generalize the gap to other fixed thresholds, onset tolerance, continuous scores or EEG arousals. No claim 2/3 is a general maximum.

## A sufficient report

If U bounds all maximal wake lengths in both channels and both endpoint epochs are S, a matched pair with lengths a,b has overlap >=(a+b)/3 and union span at most floor(4U/3). Count just the windows containing that union and one flanking epoch on each side. A word can certify the complete two maximal runs when the earlier start is at index 1 and the latest exclusive end at width-1. Each matched pair is counted once; no record-edge correction is needed. Thus words through floor(4U/3)+2 suffice for the hit numerator. Dwell histograms supply denominators, or recover them from one-channel matched run boundary windows through U+2 (also within the sufficient order). Sufficiency is not claimed optimal. The fresh checker validates this directly against general graph matching on 604 exact controls.

## Scope and closely matching prior

- [Hardarson, Islind & Óskarsdóttir (2023)](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s40675-023-00253-w), “Limitations” and “Clinical Relevance,” independently reassigns epochs in its simulations and explicitly discusses one wrong epoch splitting a long wake bout. Do not claim that mechanism itself is new.
- [Nguyen et al. (2021)](https://www.mdpi.com/2624-5175/3/2/17) compares chronologically localized transitions in paired wearable and PSG signals. [AnySleep (2026)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41746-026-03406-7) reports within-track stage triplets and a temporal-IoU arousal analysis with a 20% criterion (not the synthetic long wake endpoint).
- [Hibbing et al. (2024)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11257610/) uses extended Gale–Shapley pairing on time-in-bed interruptions within a minute. [Ward et al. (2011)](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/1535339/1/WLG10-draft.pdf) treats split/merge and timing errors in activity recognition. [Lea et al. (2017)](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_cvpr_2017/papers/Lea_Temporal_Convolutional_Networks_CVPR_2017_paper.pdf) scores temporal segments by IoU and distinguishes segmental metrics from frame accuracy. [Kirillov et al. (2019)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1801.00868) proves uniqueness of the general non-overlapping panoptic match at strict IoU >1/2. [Karhumäki, Saarela & Zamboni (2013)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1301.5104) formalizes these local-count equivalences.
- The contribution checked is exact ambiguity under unusually strong **joint** matched reporting, not inventing bout validation or inferring any device's performance, any patient sleep, or any clinical threshold or decision. No matching published construction found in the bounded search, and no unqualified priority claim is supported.
