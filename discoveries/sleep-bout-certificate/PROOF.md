# What paired sleep summaries cannot identify

The two benchmark criteria were fixed before the search. The examples and replays use exclusively synthetic binary tracks, not patient records or released device outputs.

## Estimand and closest established work

Align complete reference and test binary sleep/wake tracks on 30 s epochs, with common first and last sleep epochs. A bout is a maximal consecutive W run, eligible at length at least 10 epochs (5 min). Two eligible bouts match at intersection over union (IoU) ≥ 1/2; maximize one-to-one matching. Recall is matched reference bouts / eligible reference bouts; precision uses the device count. These are undefined for zero corresponding denominator. Awake time here equals within-window wake after sleep onset because both tracks begin and end asleep. This is not a clinical cutoff or a definition of EEG arousal.

Use the paired alphabet 0=(S,S), B=(W,S), A=(W,W), C=(S,W). The order-K report is every aligned contiguous paired word count of every length 1,…,K, never across nights. It already includes the full epoch confusion and adjacent paired transition table for K≥2. Dwell summaries count run lengths in each channel separately. The important difference from prior reports is an exact compatible range or counterexample despite unusually strong marginal and paired temporal summaries. Hardarson et al. already simulate independent errors and explicitly describe single-error fragmentation; Nguyen et al. validate temporal transitions; Ward et al. distinguish split/merge event errors; k-Abelian equivalence and IoU matching are established. See SOURCES.md.

## Frozen case 1: exact interval under full paired words to length 5 and wake dwell

Frozen gate: an eight-hour synthetic example with zero wake-minute and bout-count bias, at least 90% epoch accuracy, and sharp width ≥40 percentage points from at most .10 to at least .50. All that follows uses exact integers.

Only non-self adjacent letters in the reported witness are 0→B→A→C→0. So each nonzero island is B^u A^x C^v, and exactly one true and one later test bout occur there. If both marginal wake dwell reports put all n bouts at L epochs, then u=v=L−x. Absence in the order-K table of any word spanning two interior boundaries certifies that every interior homogeneous paired run has at least a=K−1 epochs. Otherwise a word beginning at its predecessor and ending at its successor would have length ≤K. The allowed overlap is x∈[a,b] with b=L−a, and the total WW epoch count X fixes Σx. IoU≥1/2 iff x≥q=ceil(2L/3).

For a<q≤b, each integer matched count h is possible exactly when hq+(n−h)a ≤ X ≤ hb+(n−h)(q−1). Necessity: sum the lower and upper coordinate bounds for matched and unmatched islands. Sufficiency: every integer between the sums can be obtained by adding units independently to bounded coordinates. Keep the long shared sleep gaps unchanged. A counted word either lies in one homogeneous run, where its count depends only on letter totals and run counts because every length is ≥K−1, or crosses a single boundary, whose count depends only on that boundary type. Thus all paired words through K remain identical while the chosen x redistribute. If q falls outside [a,b] all bouts are forced to one side, conditional on the summary being feasible.

N=960, n=6, L=16, K=5, X=60, a=4, b=12, q=11. Example vectors (10,10,10,10,10,10) vs (11,11,11,11,11,5); identical full paired order-5 and wake dwell reports. Sharp compatible hit counts are {0,1,2,3,4,5}. Six 8-minute bouts and 48 wake minutes in each track, 37/40 epoch accuracy, wake sensitivity 5/8. This case claims equal wake dwell, not equal full sleep dwell or the fixed exact reference track.

## Frozen case 2: fixed complete reference and both full binary dwell histograms

Frozen gate: identical entire reference, all sleep and wake dwell histograms, full paired words through K=4, eight-hour scope, ≥88% epoch agreement and ≥50% wake sensitivity, and a ≥40-point compatible range reaching .10 or below and .50 or above. Same shift-support regime; ref wake starts are 72+100i for i=0,…,7 (zero-based), L=10, N=960. Overlap x lies in [3,7], device shift is L−x, and its internal sleep gaps have length 90+x_i−x_(i+1). Leading and terminal device sleep lie in 75..79 and 171..175, outside the internal gap range 86..94. Therefore unlabeled device sleep-run histograms identify the leading 79 and terminal 174 distinctly, fixing x_0=3 and x_7=6. The seven other lengths, 87,89,91 and four copies of 90, identify the step multiset {-1,0,0,0,0,+1,+3}. X=44 comes from WW counts. Every ordering of these steps that stays within [3,7], ends at 6 and has total 44 constructs an eligible summary-compatible profile by holding the reference track and setting device shift to L−x. Joint shared-sleep gaps are all long, total occupancy and all run/boundary counts agree, and hence the paired word tables agree. This proves necessity and sufficiency for the complete published-in-this-example summary; the finite inventory walk can generally be performed by dynamic programming for equally spaced reference wakes.

Exactly 11 ordered trajectories qualify, and all hit counts {0,1,2,3,4} occur. Low x=(3,6,5,6,6,6,6,6), high x=(3,3,4,7,7,7,7,6). Both tracks have eight 5-minute bouts and 40 wake minutes, fixed reference, full binary dwell and paired order-4 equalities, binary confusion (SS,SW,WS,WW)=(844,36,36,44), 37/40 epoch accuracy, 11/20 wake sensitivity; sharp event recall and precision range is the discrete set {0,1/8,2/8,3/8,4/8}. The independent audit also scanned all 390,625 absolute eight-bout trajectories, with 31,752 threshold-eligible summary classes; the proof does not require that larger search to establish the exact result.

## General exploratory theorem: any fixed K and near-perfect epoch accuracy and wake sensitivity

For m≥2, T≥max(10,K−1), let c_i=min(i+1,2m−1−i) for i=0,…,2m−2, else 0. List U has c_i copies of (T+i,T+i+2). List V has c_(k−1)−1{k=m} copies of (T+k,T+k) for k=1,…,2m−1 and exactly one (T,T+2m). Each has m² pairs. The pair-sum histograms agree (the exceptional pair has the central sum). Fragment lengths agree as a multiset including multiplicities: for interior index k the degree identity is c_k+c_(k−2)=2c_(k−1)−2·1{k=m}; the outer endpoints appear once in each. Sort by pair sum. In separate time blocks with long common sleep separators, a pair (p,q) gives A^p B A^q. The fixed reference is one full wake run of length p+q+1; the device calls two wake bouts p,q with a one-epoch sleep interruption. The identical pair sums make the entire exact reference the same across U,V; the fragment-degree identity and fixed separators make both full track-specific dwell histograms the same. No word of length ≤K can see both the internal disagreement and an external shared-sleep boundary (each flank is ≥K−1). The internal disagreement neighborhood therefore has fixed K-word counts, and all other counts are determined by the pair sums and identical shared-sleep separators. Unequal fragments give one hit at IoU ≥1/2; equal fragments both fall below half. Thus recall=1 vs 1/m² (precision=1/2 vs 1/(2m²)); reference fixed, but the published device bout count is double in both worlds.

To remove this device bout-count and wake-minute bias, append to each world fresh copies with channels swapped, i.e. A^p C A^q. The reference is NOT fixed between these mirrored worlds; its full dwell and paired word reports still are. Within and across worlds both channels now have the same full wake dwell and totals; both have 3m² eligible bouts. Total hits 2m² vs 2 yield recall and precision gap (2/3)(1−1/m²). Each world has m² false-positive and m² false-negative wake epochs; true wake epoch denominator is 2Σ(p+q)+m², so for any fixed K, choosing m then T large gives a gap nearly 2/3 even as sensitivity and epoch accuracy approach 100%. Record length may grow. This is an attained lower bound on worst-case ambiguity, not an extremal theorem or a device result.

Concrete N=960, m=2, T=10, K=11: U fragment pairs (10,12),(11,13),(11,13),(12,14); V (11,11),(12,12),(10,14),(13,13) sorted by common sums 22,24,24,26. With mirror, confusion (760,4,4,192), each track 12 bouts and 98 wake minutes, epoch accuracy 119/120, wake sensitivity 48/49, hits 8/12 vs 2/12. The match set for this concrete example is unchanged for IoU cutoffs in (13/27,14/27]. The asymptotic statement, by contrast, is specifically at IoU=1/2; it does not automatically hold at any other fixed threshold.

A short-gap convention need not defeat the logical result. Replace the single disagreement by g identical epochs, and replace offsets i by d·i with 2d≥g. Margins and K-word proof are unchanged in structure; the larger unequal fragment meets half of p+q+g. This survives a fixed rule bridging at most g−1 epochs, given long outer separators, not arbitrary scoring/postprocessing. For g=3,d=2 and other concrete parameters unchanged, matched counts still 8/12 vs 2/12, at 118 wake min each, epoch accuracy 97.5% and wake sensitivity 224/236.

## What identifies the endpoint without individual records?

For any two fully observed binary tracks, IoU ≥1/2 already guarantees one-to-one edges. Distinct test maximal wakes are separated by at least one sleep epoch. If two each overlapped a reference wake by half or more, their disjoint intersections would need to exhaust it and leave no separating epoch, a contradiction. Same in reverse. The threshold is sharp: two test bouts of length v separated by one epoch inside a full reference of length 2v+1 approach 1/2 from below. The classic general-disjoint-segment theorem establishes strict >1/2; inclusion of exactly half here uses maximal bouts and the separator.

At the chosen threshold, the two eligible counts and the one aligned matched count are sufficient for the micro-ratios. For threshold/specific duration sensitivity release a sparse aggregate table (reference length, device length, positive overlap), and sum cells with 3×overlap≥reference length+device length. At another threshold τ≥1/2 use (1+τ)×overlap≥τ×(reference length+device length). Below half, do not add edges and call it a maximum matching: they can conflict. The counts alone do not give participant-cluster uncertainty or macro-average; disclose missingness, clipping, alignment, eligibility and retention separately.

If all wake bouts are bounded above by U epochs, order K0=floor(4U/3)+2 is sufficient even without structure. A hit with overlap o and lengths r,d has o≥(r+d)/3; its union span s=r+d−o≤floor(4U/3). Take its unique enclosing paired word with one epoch before and after the union (available because the records start/end asleep). The word shows both channels' relevant onset/offset boundaries, even if the other channel has elsewhere in the word a different wake. For each token of length s+2, count the matches evaluating the two channels whose union is exactly its interior. Multiply by that word's frequency and sum across s, including only fully bounded wake bouts. This counts each eligible paired hit once. Finite K not less than this sufficient K0 is not covered by the negative argument when U is fixed; K0 is not necessarily minimal.
