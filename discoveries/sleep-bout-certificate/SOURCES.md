# Public native aggregate scope and primary sources

No compatible independent native aggregate holdout for paired adult wearable/PSG natural maximal wake bouts ≥5 min at IoU ≥1/2 was found among the sources actually checked on 2026-11-25. This is a bounded no-go for an empirical device estimate, not a claim no such study exists. No participant files or examples were obtained.

- [Hardarson, Islind, Arnardottir & Óskarsdóttir 2023](https://doi.org/10.1007/s40675-023-00253-w), Error Propagation: explicitly models simulated independent per-epoch reassignments from confusion and recognizes single-misclassification fragmentation; awakening definition is at least two consecutive wake epochs. This preempts generic absence of endpoint information in epoch confusion; our contrast is exact fixed-summary ambiguity and sufficiency.
- [Nguyen, Le, Huynh, Setty, Vo & Le 2021](https://doi.org/10.3390/clockssleep3020017), [publisher PDF](https://mdpi-res.com/d_attachment/clockssleep/clockssleep-03-00017/article_deploy/clockssleep-03-00017-v2.pdf): 22 individuals and 30 eight-hour nights, section 3.4/Figure 5/Table 7 paired localized transition percentages and Table 8 stage sensitivity/specificity. Nguyen, not Chinoy, is first author. No locked overlap numerator/denominators.
- [Ward, Lukowicz & Gellersen](https://discovery.ucl.ac.uk/id/eprint/1535729/1/Ward_WLT06loca.pdf) establishes split/merge/insertion/deletion event diagnostics; [Karhumäki, Saarela & Zamboni](https://arxiv.org/abs/1301.5104) establishes k-Abelian equivalence for window histograms; [Kirillov et al. CVPR 2019, Thm. 1](https://openaccess.thecvf.com/content_CVPR_2019/papers/Kirillov_Panoptic_Segmentation_CVPR_2019_paper.pdf) establishes >.5 uniqueness for general disjoint segments.
- [Sitnick, Goodlin-Jones & Anders 2008](https://doi.org/10.1093/sleep/31.3.395) compared pediatric videosomnography and actigraphy, paired onset within ±5 minutes and inspected durations separately. Direct paired event validation exists, but its clock tolerance, comparator and ages do not match.
- [Willoughby et al. 2024](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.sleh.2024.02.007) directly assesses a PSG-timed experimental smartphone wake event, conditional on the device identifying sleep before interruption. One scheduled nocturnal event; window begins one epoch before alarm and extends to next sleep. Not natural all-eligible bout recall/precision. First author Willoughby, not Chinoy.
- [Grieger et al. 2026, AnySleep accepted article](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41746-026-03406-7_reference.pdf): counts distinct-stage triplets in 1.5-hour blocks and evaluates overlap for EEG arousals, not wearable 30-second maximal natural wake bouts. [Quality-controlled PSG 2026](https://doi.org/10.1093/sleepadvances/zpag054) uses .3 IoU on arousals and respiratory events. These are different units.
- [Kim et al. 2026 conference abstract](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1111/jsr.70434), section “Comprehensive Comparison…”: 46 participants, home PSG with five devices, transition probabilities (e.g. Apple/Oura wake maintenance .978/.981), no aligned qualifying-bout pair count. Conference abstract, not full native holdout.
- [Robbins et al. 2024](https://www.mdpi.com/1424-8220/24/20/6532), Tables 2–5: paired epoch sensitivity/precision and aggregate wake after sleep onset, with usable participant summaries 35/33/29 by device; no matched awake-run numerator. [Antwerp 2025 indexed study](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40303381/) 62 adults, and [2026 home PSG results](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.sleep.2026.109059) parallel n=20/19/15: no comparable matched endpoint verified in publicly accessible material. [Google/Fitbit 2026 sponsor abstract](https://research.google/pubs/performance-analysis-of-updated-sleep-tracking-algorithms-across-google-and-fitbit-wearable-devices/) distinguishes validation of sleep session timing (47 users) vs staging (50 withheld subject nights, 71→77% mean four-stage epoch accuracy); neither is natural bout match. [Google documentation](https://developers.google.com/health/data-types/sleep) distinguishes separate, potentially overlapping short-awakening overlays from consecutive nonoverlapping stage intervals; proof does not apply as written to overlays.
- [Saleh et al. 2025 MESA](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12406835/) studies 1,908 people with multi-night actigraphic fragmentation and PSG aggregates, not matched wake bouts. [2024 activPAL/Actiwatch](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC11257610/) matched peer device time-in-bed interruptions, not synchronized PSG-defined wake bouts.

A fitting native holdout would privately provide pooled eligible reference and device events, aligned compatible matched counts by prespecified duration/overlap, clock/window/boundary and gap definitions, numbers of scheduled/usable nights and missingness, and participant-cluster uncertainty. No outreach was done. These are reporting design findings, not guidance on measuring, diagnosing or managing an individual.
