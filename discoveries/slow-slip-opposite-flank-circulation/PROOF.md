# Opposite-flank circulation of a slow-slip sheet: sharp finite-aperture and depth limits
A model-specific geodetic theorem and archive feasibility check. No real slip event or hazard is inferred.

## Model and exact scalar-pressure null
Linear quasi-static flat halfspace z≥0 (depth positive), laterally uniform horizontal isotropy. Displacement u real; in homogeneous proof constant Lamé moduli (μ>0, stable bulk). Externally normal pressures (ocean, atmospheric, surface water) allowed arbitrary x,y/time shape on flat surface, zero horizontal tractions. Scalar pore-pressure stress −α(z)p(x,y,z)I permitted anywhere with integrable decaying response and no laterally anisotropic coupling/poroelastic shear source. Decomposing horizontal Fourier k into longitudinal and transverse e_T=(-k_y,k_x)/|k|, the transverse force and traction of either class vanish. With plane layering of locally isotropic elastic materials the homogeneous SH ODE is (μ W′)′−μ|k|²W=0; top μW′=0, finite energy and vanishing deep boundary term (or sufficiently decaying solution), continuous W and traction at material interfaces. Multiply by conjugate W, integrate: ∫μ(|W′|²+|k|²|W|²)=0, so W=0 for k≠0. Thus horizontal curl ω=∂_x u_y−∂_y u_x vanishes in these pure-load models at all receiver depths. Physical affine frame transforms are treated as additive **displacement nuisances** u_err=A(x,y)+b; not applying arbitrary large coordinate rotations to the known loop/rake geometry. Lateral heterogeneity, anisotropic pore eigenstress, sloping/topographic loads (nonvertical normal), tangential loading, rotational frame gradients, other dislocations, and shape/material uncertainty may all violate attribution. Scalar pressure may trigger genuine slip; this separates modeled deformation modes, not origin of a physical event or an exclusion of hydrological causation.

## Exact fixed-rake depth trade and sharp leakage
Now homogeneous μ and sheet z=d>0 with upper-minus-lower horizontal tangential jump s a(x,y), fixed unit rake s, continuous transverse traction; finite signed scalar measure a is allowed algebraically; nonnegative is required for mass bounds. For k≠0, above-sheet transverse displacement A cosh(|k| z) from top Neumann, below B exp(−|k|(z−d)) by decay. Traction continuity gives B=−A sinh(|k|d), hence jump A exp(|k|d), so
W_T(k,z) = cosh(|k|z) e^(−|k|d) (e_T·s) â(k) for 0≤z<d.
Translation k=0 can be fixed by frame/decay and does not affect curl. At z=0, with s=e_x, ω=−∂_y(P_d*a), P_d(q)=d/[2π(d²+|q|²)^(3/2)], ∫P_d=1, Fourier multiplier exp(−|k|d). This is horizontal curl (twice infinitesimal vertical rotation), not symmetric tensor shear. Elastic station data can include useful independent longitudinal/vertical information, but the SH channel alone at ANY other known depth above both alternatives is exactly the same linear functional of the surface channel via cosh transfer. E.g. 170m and λ=50km changes spectral transverse gain only ~0.023%, even before noise; this is SH-only, not claim all borehole data redundant.
Let d>d'>z receiver, same s. Equality of ideal full SH or curl at z holds iff b=P_(d−d')*a. Divide by nonzero e_T·s off rake-parallel line; zero/curl frequencies and the entire missing line are recovered for the scalar measures by continuity of their Fourier transforms (dense complement), so no invisible finite fixed-rake measure remains there. Total masses agree by k→0. Do not extend iff to differing rake or arbitrary vector slip; gradient-like vector jumps have invisible transverse component. Any open aperture's noiseless continuum equality extends in this homogeneous model by real analyticity for z bounded away from sheets (multipliers decay exponentially), not a stable reconstruction from finitely many/noisy samples.
For M=∫a>0, a≥0, δ=d−d'>0, any disk D_R, the exactly equivalent shallower b must put at least fraction δ/√(R²+δ²) outside D_R. Indeed, translated radially strictly decreasing Pδ maximizes mass in a fixed disk when centered (layer-cake disks and area of intersection ≤ min of the areas, equality for concentric disks); integrate exact radial tail ∫_R∞ δ r/(r²+δ²)^(3/2)dr. Sharp for a centered point concentration, attainable as limit with smooth compact sources. Example δ=10 km, R=50 km requires fraction ≥1/√26≈19.6116%. No exact match if both nonzero positive sources required fully compact, or if shallower leakage budget below bound. This is an exact ideal-data test, not noise-robust lower residual. For approximate comparison one can instead truncate the exact b outside disk: if fraction η omitted, then absolute surface curl error ≤η M ||∂_y P_(d')||∞ (double if redistribute to preserve M), where ||∂_y P_d'||∞ =3/(4π(d')³)*(4/5)^(5/2), from radial maximum at transverse distance d'/2. This is an upper bound for a proposed approximate mimic, not a guaranteed finite-data separation; no assumption of identical maxima/unknown signs. A second shallow map cannot automatically repair ambiguity; a different polarization, source geometry constraint, or receiver below one candidate is a distinct matter not addressed here.

## Opposite-flank finite-aperture separator, with sharp constants
Fix horizontal coordinates rake x, transverse y, source support S=[−S_x,S_x]×[−S_y,S_y], d known for benchmark. A right-hand (CCW in x,y) loop through rectangle Q_+=[−X,X]×[Y,Z], Z>Y>S_y; reflected rectangle Q_−=[−X,X]×[−Z,−Y] also oriented CCW. V=Γ_+−Γ_−, Γ=∮u_h·dl=∫_Q ω. This finite continuous measurement vanishes for load classes above and for any affine additive horizontal frame field: constant curl times equal area cancels. A single loop would be contaminated by spatially constant frame yaw. Pure nonnegative x-rake slip yields V=∫_S K(s) da(s), with K(s)=G(s_x,Y−s_y)+G(s_x,Y+s_y)>0 and G(c,t)=∫_(-X)^X[P_d(x−c,t)−P_d(x−c,t+Z−Y)]dx. Against these null models no unknown pressure amplitude appears in this exact continuous statistic.
Write D_u=d²+u² and p(t)=d/(2π)(D_u+t²)^(−3/2); for fixed t>0 and L=Z−Y>0 the positive p(t)−p(t+L) decreases in |u| (derivative w.r.t. u² strictly negative). Symmetric interval convolution thus decreases with |s_x|, by endpoint derivative, so the lower bound is always at maximal |s_x| and upper at zero. If 4(Y−S_y)²≥3[d²+(X+S_x)²], then p‴(t)=(15d/(2π))t(3D_u−4t²)/(D_u+t²)^(9/2)≤0 on the entire arguments, so G is convex in t. Its even sum is minimized at s_y=0 and maximized at |s_y|=S_y. Consequently exact shape-independent bounds
K_min=2G(S_x,Y) ≤ K ≤ G(0,Y−S_y)+G(0,Y+S_y)=K_max.
Analytic evaluation uses primitive F(u,t)=d/(2π)·u/[(d²+t²)√(d²+t²+u²)]; G is endpoint difference at t minus that at t+L. For fixed integrated nonnegative mass M the best possible bounds K_min M and K_max M are attained by point source measures at extrema and arbitrarily approached by positive smooth slip densities concentrating there (no amplitude cap claimed). Signed fixed rake a=a_+−a_− supported S: V≥K_min M_+−K_max M_−, sharp using opposite extrema; since extremizers distinct, this is a uniform positive guarantee iff reverse/forward ratio M_−/M_+<K_min/K_max (for M_+>0). No guarantee for arbitrary signed slip. Separate variation: if x-slip is nonnegative and |d a_y|≤m d a_x, local derivative ratio |∂_xP|/|∂_yP|=|x−s_x|/|y−s_y| gives |K_y|≤[(X+S_x)/(Y−S_y)] K_x after reflection/difference and triangle inequality. Thus V≥[1−m(X+S_x)/(Y−S_y)]∫K_x da_x. When the bracket is nonnegative this gives V≥K_min M_x[1−m(X+S_x)/(Y−S_y)]; for a negative bracket the conservative lower bound instead uses K_max. The positive threshold is sufficient, not asserted sharp in the wedge.
Illustrative model, not actual deployment/geometry: d=30km, S_x=S_y=15km, X=20km,Y=55km,Z=80km (both loops perimeter 130km; convexity condition strict since 6400>6375 km²). K_min≈8.078389646e−7 m^−1, K_max≈1.146074848e−6 m^−1, sharp reversal cutoff ≈.704874525; orientation sufficient m<8/7. Integrated slip 20mm across 900km² is M=18e6 m³, V≥14.5411 m², equivalently ≥55.927 micrometers of perimeter-normalized signed displacement across the two loops total (not each GNSS station or actual forecast). Nondetect cannot exclude slip below these levels or outside the prescribed geometry/format.

## From perfect loops to sparse data, not assumed exact
Each straight segment length ℓ, tangent t, s arclength: tangent scalar f(s)=u_h·t and f′(s)=t^T ε_h t. Given co-located endpoints with both displacement and calibrated surface symmetric strain, use I≈ℓ[f(0)+f(ℓ)]/2−ℓ²[f′(ℓ)−f′(0)]/12, the integral of the cubic Hermite interpolant, remainder ≤ℓ⁵ ||f⁽⁴⁾||∞/720 (integral of x²(x−ℓ)²/4!). Thus a conservative pair-of-loops error is sum of these quadrature remainders plus ℓ/2 per endpoint displacement error and ℓ²/12 per endpoint strain error. Using a borehole directional strain at h instead of surface adds h sup|∂_z ε_tt| and local instrument-transfer error per endpoint; sparse B003/B004 do not produce exact loop cancellation or local ω. Without those geometry/regularity/error caps a finite affine-cancelling vertex formula is not a load-null channel.
Hypothetical four-corner per-loop plain trapezoid, eight different ideal co-located GNSS corner vectors, independent isotropic per-Cartesian marginal error σ on matched event displacement, zero interpolation/model error *optimistically*: variance=2σ²[(40 km)²+(25 km)²]. One-sided Gaussian α=.05/power=.8 against the minimum 14.541 m² needs σ≤.0877 mm; annotated conditional forecast, not a statement about an actual stack. Full covariance must include common-mode, regional rotation and temporally correlated reference frame, calibration and detrending/tidal/pressure uncertainties; atmospheric pressure at one hole is not basin pressure. Deterministic mid-loop frame/loading residual leakage must be bounded separately; if each hypothesis has same postcontrast bounded bias E, robust separation requires predicted worst gap >2E plus the statistical margin, not the ideal threshold above. Archived ALBH daily formal E/N scales around .67/1 mm in inspected event rows are not a credible residual covariance of eight ideal corners. Verified no eligibility for empirical attribution.
