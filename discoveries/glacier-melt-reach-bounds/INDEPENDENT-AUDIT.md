# Independent mathematical audit

Checked the signed-tide criterion and both of the exact high-ridge moment endpoints. The independent standard-library replay verifies synthetic controls, not measured or forecast glacier motion.

## 1. Signed differential tide criterion

[Rignot et al. (2024) primary paper](https://www.pnas.org/doi/pdf/10.1073/pnas.2404766121), Materials and Methods “Differential Interferometry” and “Predictions of SSH”, actually subtracts consecutive 1-day pairs, or two disjoint 1-day pairs over gaps; the signed stencils follow algebraically (overall orientation sign arbitrary). The source's reported 8-cm SD is the model-versus-DInSAR residual summary, not an independently certified inland pointwise gain/error bound; CATS2008 is evaluated offshore and inverse barometer uses ERA-5 there. Section 2.2's sign/amplitude argument concerns its fixed-hinge alternative, not the general moving-contact/viscoelastic null. Section 2.5 instead attributes a farther South Ridge signal to **inferred subglacial water redistribution**, not a direct fluid-composition measurement. Nonlinear asymmetric tidal migration has strong prior demonstrations, e.g. [Tsai–Gudmundsson (2015)](https://nora.nerc.ac.uk/id/eprint/511211/1/Tsai%20et%20al%202015.pdf) and [Warburton et al. (2020)](https://doi.org/10.1029/2020GL089088); the added content is the sharp scalar-memoryless match criterion, not the first observation that the physical response can be nonlinear.

For a single common absolutely continuous memoryless response \(f\), at signed tide-height atoms on a co-registered patch, \(\sum c_j f(\eta_j)=-\int f'G\) because \(\sum c_j=0\). The midpoint-plus-radius interval for bounded slopes follows by maximizing the sign of \(G\), attained by unconstrained switching of \(f'\) within the declared slope box; affine invariance and common-origin shifts are exact. If additionally the signed first moment vanishes, the twice-integrated Peano kernel \(P(s)=\sum c_j(\eta_j-s)_+\) removes both integration constants. With an **alternative** \(C^{1,1}\) bound \(|f''|\le K\), the attainable radius is \(K\int|P|\), by choosing \(f''=K\operatorname{sign}P\). Not valid as two simultaneous independent sharp intervals if both slope and curvature bounds are imposed. Replay finds \(\|G\|_1=160\) cm, both slope-box endpoints \(\pm20\) cm, and \(\|P\|_1=3000\ {\rm cm}^2\) for the synthetic control. An exact 24 h time stencil filters any strictly daily-periodic input response, not a mixed history-dependent actual tide. Unknown genuine residuals and tide errors must be bounded independently with acquisition sharing and actual offsets; none of the algebra assigns a measured residual to seawater.

## 2. Fixed and mixed triangular melt moments

[Poinelli et al. Eq. (2)](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC13658800/), and earlier [Robel–Wilson–Seroussi (2022) Eq. (22)](https://tc.copernicus.org/articles/16/451/2022/), are genuinely linear upstream taper to specified distance, not probability calibration of a time-varying instantaneous reach. The checked model is an explicitly conditional quasistatic extension of that constitutive proxy, with fixed spatial origin and unit width, nonnegative integrable amplitude \(A\) and measurable \(L\). Define spatial raw moments \(M_j=\int_0^\infty x^j\overline q(x)\,dx\); direct integration gives
\[
 M_j=\langle A L^{j+1}\rangle/((j+1)(j+2)).
\]
If a **single** positive-width fixed ramp is assumed and \(M_0>0\), \(L=3M_1/M_0\), \(A=2M_0/L\). For time variation this two-moment fit is \(L_{\rm eq}=\langle AL^2\rangle/\langle AL\rangle\), size-biased by heat-delivering amplitude times reach; it is not generally the unweighted or simple amplitude-weighted mean. A third directly measurable-in-a-model spatial moment has a one-line diagnostic, \(M_0 M_2\ge(3/2)M_1^2\) by Cauchy–Schwarz. Equality holds iff \(L\) is constant under \(d\nu=AL\,dP_t/\langle AL\rangle,\quad dP_t=dt/\text{duration}\), allowing otherwise invisible zero-reach or zero-amplitude episodes. The normalized excess \(2M_0M_2/(3M_1^2)-1\) is \(\operatorname{Var}_{\nu}(L)/(E_{\nu}L)^2\). No such in situ moment-data premise was established. Distinguish this optional diagnostic from the main proof's chosen **different** budget of known \(A_0=\langle A\rangle\) and total \(M_0\), optionally adding \(M_1\).

Independently integrating above the ridge coordinate gives \(g_R(l)=(l-R)_+^2/(2l)\), zero at \(l=0\). Its kink at \(R>0\) is \(C^1\), convex derivative, and curvature above \(R\) is \(R^2/l^3\). Under amplitude weighting \(\pi\), Jensen and endpoint chord are exact sharp; \(E_\pi L^2=s_2>R\mu\) is both necessary and sufficient to *force* distal melt if only mean/second moment/peak budget is known. Endpoints and exceptions: \(\mu^2\le s_2\le b\mu\) exactly feasible for \(0<\mu<b\) using \(\{0,s_2/\mu\}\), \(\mu=0,b\) one-state (under \(\pi\)). High ridge \(R\ge2b/3\) makes \((1-R/l)_+^2\) convex on the size-biased law \(\nu\), giving the claimed exact lower formula with that same atom law. The all-ridge bound from \((s_2-R\mu)_+\le E[L(L-R)_+]\) and Cauchy–Schwarz uses \(EL^3\le b s_2\); only evaluate division for positive \(s_2\). For the upper endpoint the sign of derivative of \(g_R(l)/(l-a)^2\) above \(R\) is the sign of \(2(R-a)l-(l-R)(l-a)\). It is concave; at \(R\) positive if \(a<R\), and at \(b\) it is \(b(3R-b)-a(b+R)\ge0\) under the stated hypothesis. This proves the dual majorant touches exactly the required two support points (or extra contacts in degenerate equality, not needed). Do not assert the formula outside its stated regime. The independently audited extended upper for all \(R\ge2b/3\) uses \(a(c)=(c\mu-s_2)/(c-\mu)\) and \(a_{\rm tan}(c)=c(3R-c)/(c+R)\). When \(a(b)>a_{\rm tan}(b)\), the former strictly increases and latter strictly decreases above \(R\), so their unique crossing after \(\max(R,s_2/\mu)\) is before \(b\), with \(a(c)<R\). Expanding that equality gives the cubic in the proof; the same concave derivative-sign quadratic is positive at \(R\), zero at \(c\), and negative thereafter, certifying global rather than local contact. This complete high-ridge formula does not extend by this argument to \(R<2b/3\). Replay uses \(a=899/245,c=29/5,R=4,b=6\) with equal atomic masses; \(81/580\) strictly exceeds the endpoint-\(b\) construction \(22707/164221\). Replay's independent exact three-support finite LP on a 1/8-km grid had 1,651 nonnegative weight triples and extrema 3/50, 2/15 times \(A_0\); the analytic proof, not grid density, certifies the continuous range. \(R\) and \(b\) are schematic distances and all areas per unit-width transect. The full ideal mean profile yields \(\overline q''=A_0\,\pi|_{(0,b]}/x\) on the positive half-line in distributions, including the \(b\) endpoint via zero continuation, not temporal sequence or robust differentiation from noisy observations.

Primary limits: [Gadi et al. (2023)](https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1029/2023GL105869) actually models time-dependent tides and documents modeled seawater averaging about 72% of Petermann's modeled cavity; [2026 Yelmo paper, §2.2 and Appendix A](https://tc.copernicus.org/articles/20/5345/2026/) uses height-above-flotation controlling a spatially varying reach and a linearly tapered fraction, not a resolving measurement of the conditional moment problem; [2024 finite intrusion feedback model](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41561-024-01465-7) estimates coupled hydrology/heat and threshold behavior from a different model. Prior does not leave authors first to note variability, time averaging, or uncertain widths. The audited contribution is a scoped same-budget extremal theorem plus sharp memoryless-null test; the result does not depend on the reported model outcome summaries. No new claim about Thwaites temperature, actual melting, irreversible retreat or the world at 2100.
