# Prior scope and source anchors

- [Combes, Ferrie, Cesare, Tiersch, Milburn, Briegel and Caves, *In-situ characterization of quantum devices with error correction*, arXiv:1405.5656](https://arxiv.org/abs/1405.5656), especially §§II–III and V, their Eqs. (5), (10), Fig. 1 and choice of duration versus counter-unitaries. This is the closest active-inference prior. It studies an arbitrary encoded state of the 3-bit repetition code, already distinguishes coherent/stochastic components by applying physical counter-rotations or changing dwell times, explicitly discusses the passive symmetry and compares spatially correlated stochastic hypotheses. The measurement/probe concept and passive 3-bit ambiguity are not new. Their rotation-offset and duration estimators are not the partial-midpoint graph law or minimax null used here.
- [Orsucci, Tiersch and Briegel, *Estimation of coherent error sources from stabilizer measurements*, PRA (2016)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.93.042303) and [erratum](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevA.99.059901) use stabilizer data and changed measurement bases to infer coherent errors in graph states. This precedes graph-dependent stabilizer identifiability, but not the specific role of the connected components of **which intermediate checks are physically measured** in this fixed odd repetition echo experiment.
- [Iverson and Preskill, *Coherence in logical quantum channels*, NJP (2020)](https://doi.org/10.1088/1367-2630/ab8e5c) ([author PDF](https://preskill.caltech.edu/pubs/preskill-2020-coherence.pdf)), Fig. 1 and their repetition-code conditional channel calculations; [Greenbaum and Dutton, *Modeling coherent errors in quantum error correction*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1612.03908) already use the complementary error paths of a full syndrome to quantify coherent logical rotations and their temporal buildup. Ordinary full-check law or logical effects are not claimed as new. [Bravyi et al. (2018)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41534-018-0106-y) treats coherent errors in the surface code; the present repetition-only readout result does not extend automatically.
- [Takou and Brown, *Estimating and decoding coherent errors of QEC experiments with detector error models*, arXiv:2510.23797v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.23797v1), §III.1 and §III.3: no coherent enhancement of the physical DEM rates in ordinary code-capacity repetition, while surface boundaries and circuit-level repetition can show enhancement and extra hyperedges. [Hines et al., *Simulating Quantum Error Correction beyond Pauli Stochastic Errors*, arXiv:2603.18457](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.18457) uses coherent-path expansions for detector-model approximations; this does not identify an arbitrary fitted Pauli null from existing data. [Huang, *Tomography for coherent noise*, arXiv:2610.19779v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.19779v1) targets logical process tomography under structured channels rather than this syndrome-only experiment. [Wagner et al., *Pauli channels can be estimated from syndrome measurements in quantum error correction*](https://quantum-journal.org/papers/q-2022-09-19-809/) and [*Efficient learning of logical noise from syndrome data*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.22286) address identification within Pauli models.
- [Cai et al., *Mitigating coherent noise using Pauli conjugation*](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41534-019-0233-0) explicitly distinguishes Pauli-invariant stochastic noise from interferometric changes under Pauli conjugation and uses it to mitigate coherent errors. [*Randomized compiling in fault-tolerant quantum computation*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.13752) uses stabilizer and logical twirls to remove forms of coherence. [Witzel, Ganti and Metodi, *Correcting coherent quantum errors by going with the flow*, arXiv:2602.21076v1](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.21076v1), §§III–VI and Fig. 4, already applies Pauli flips to affect temporal logical-error buildup on repetition codes. Neither echo conjugation, Pauli randomization, nor grouping interfering paths is new.
- Real hardware context: [*Quantum computer error structure probed by quantum error correction syndrome measurements*, PR Research 6, 043249](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevResearch.6.043249) reports syndrome-rate controls with dynamical-decoupling pulses, including a negative source-probe result. [*Correlated Phase Error Bursts in a Gap-Engineered Superconducting Qubit Array*, PRX (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1103/1bl4-b2f7) already uses an echo as a diagnostic/mitigation in a repetition experiment. [*Demonstrating real-time and low-latency quantum error correction with superconducting qubits*, Nature Communications (2026)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-026-73331-6) studies omission of a stabilizer for circuit simplification and logical performance. None is treated as an empirical holdout for the two-slot matched experiment specified here.

The bounded screen did not find the same all-joint-character echo formula, a tight component-size shot exponent with versus without reported-bit noise, or this condition on selective intermediate checks under an arbitrary echo-invariant Pauli-trajectory comparator. The claim is narrowly these mathematical results, not first use of effects, methods, or circuitry. No original hardware data were acquired, and no external source text or code is bundled.
