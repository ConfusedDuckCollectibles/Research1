# Locked diagnostic question

Use odd-length nearest-neighbor repetition for one physical Pauli error axis, ideal projective checks, two matched noise slots, selected ideal midpoint checks, and full final syndrome with no logical readout. A randomized, actual Pauli conjugation of the second slot toggles signs without intentionally changing its noise environment. The null is any Pauli-trajectory law (including correlated and fully refitted) invariant under randomized echo choice; compare also exact known own Pauli twirl. No QEC hardware or externally executed operations.

Target: determine the complete two-time syndrome law and which echo dependences are possible, quantify check omissions and sharp small-angle shot scaling. Full mid-check negative control, partial local check example, unknown logical input, arbitrary time/space-correlated echo-independent Pauli comparator, and the calibrated context-dependent null caveat are mandatory. A finite exact statevector (or path projector) check is a second implementation. Small-angle exponents hold for fixed finite code graph, at known nonzero stable same-axis rotations in the two equal slots, and ideal syndrome detection; weak/mismatched controls or measurement noise are not silently ignored.

Prior anchors before derivation: Combes et al. arXiv:1405.5656 uses counter-units, repetitions and interval-length variation for repetition syndrome calibration; Takou–Brown arXiv:2510.23797v1 measures coherent noise and detector signatures and sees no ordinary code-capacity enhancement on repetition; Iverson–Preskill arXiv:1912.04319 exact conditional logical channel; pure echo or varying-dwell mechanisms alone are not new. Test only whether exact partial-measurement topology and matching minimax code/shot exponent add a genuine distinction.
