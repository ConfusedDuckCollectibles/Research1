# Independent exact check: three Pasch wings in dimension 18

**Verified:** Any real equiangular line system in dimension 18 containing an isometric copy of the 21-line union of the three seven-line wings from Munemasa–Szöllősi–Yoshino's construction has at most 57 lines, and every such core attains 57. No ambient-lattice assumption on other lines is needed, and no claim is made about a 58- or 59-line set without such a core.

## Sources and scope

The core comes from [Munemasa–Szöllősi–Yoshino, arXiv:2606.10421v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.10421v2), in particular their normalized seven-cliques, Proposition 5.2, and construction, Theorem 6.1. The latter proves a 57 maximum **within their \(A_5^3\oplus A_1^4\) overlattice \(L\)**. Crucially their **Theorem 7.5** already proves arbitrary same-dimension maximality **when all 57 published lines are held fixed**, not just lattice maximality. That proof uses the full-set span of \(L\) or \(L_{54}\) up to isometry; it does not exclude 58 retaining only the 21 named wing lines. Their Theorem 8.1 establishes a higher-dimensional extension, consistent with the rank-specific argument here. The related [Lin–Munemasa–Taniguchi–Yoshino](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.laa.2025.06.015) paper instead uses \(A_9^2\oplus A_1\) and proves strong maximality for its separate constructions. The unconditional known global interval \(57\le N(18)\le59\) comes from [Greaves–Syatriadi–Yatsyna](https://doi.org/10.1090/mcom/3832) and [Greaves–Syatriadi](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcta.2023.105812), respectively. I found no matching arbitrary-extension bound at the 21-line wing-only scope in these primary sources.

## Independent reduction and checks

1. A unit line direction \(\ell\) lifts to \(x=(\sqrt{5/2}\,\ell,1/\sqrt2)\) in \(18+1\) orthonormal coordinates, and \(\rho=(0,\sqrt2)\). Thus \(x^2=3,\ x\cdot\rho=1,\ x\cdot x'\in\{0,1\}\) for distinct equiangular lines of angle \(\arccos(1/5)\); \(-\ell\) corresponds to \(\rho-x\). This holds for arbitrary real lines. For the code I instead operate horizontally with \(z=2(x-\rho/2)\), whose squared norm is 10 and whose dot with each distinct wing is \(\pm2\).
2. Encode the three \(R^6\) zero-sum blocks by five coordinates each with metric \(I_5+J_5\), and the three horizontal \(R^2\) zero-sum blocks by one coordinate each with metric \(2\). The fourth \(R^2\) block contains \(\rho\) and no horizontal coordinate. The primary notation in §3.2 and Lemma 5.1 yields \(-1\) on the indicated three-set, \(+1\) on its complement within the \(R^6\) block, and auxiliary nonzero coordinates \((\epsilon,1)\) in the prescribed cyclic blocks for each doubled horizontal wing. Enumerating the actual 40-vertex normalized wing graph yields 960 size-seven cliques, precisely four 240-member orbits under the internal \(S_6\). Applying physical coordinate block permutations and sign flips, then decoding each line's switched representatives independently, partitions the 64 pattern triples into the four checked representatives \(000,001,002,003\) with sizes 8, 8, 24, 24. This is reduction by explicit isometries; it does not claim distinct full geometric isometry classes.
3. Each checked wing union spans the horizontal 18-dimensional space. Let \(A\) be the exact matrix whose rows are the metric-weighted coordinates of 18 independent doubled horizontal wings. Every new unoriented line can be fixed to have a positive first basis dot, so \(Az=2\epsilon\) with exactly \(2^{17}\) sign signatures to try. Exact rational inversion, integer norm, and the other three wing-dot tests exhaust all possibilities. A line already in the core fails its own wing test. No roots or integrality assumption is used for rationality; it follows from full rank of \(A\). The computed sets of 864, 1053, 864, 1053 lines independently match all original HNF output coordinates after rescaling. Choosing an isometry between cores fixes coordinates for the whole ambient 18-space; there are no extra orthogonal directions.
4. Original HNF completeness also checks independently. With \(H\) the basis columns of \(T=\langle\rho,\text{wings}\rangle\) in six-times reduced coordinates and \(G=H^T M H/36\), dual vectors have coordinates \(HG^{-1}z\) for integer \(z\). The root lattice \(\Lambda\) in reduced integer coordinates is \(6\mathbb Z^{19}\), and \(\Lambda\subset T^*\) since \(T,\Lambda\subset L\) with \(L\) integral. Thus \(Q=6GH^{-1}\) is integral, and diagonal ranges from its column-HNF index \(\mathbb Z^{19}/Q\mathbb Z^{19}\) by successive triangular reduction. Each possible vector has a unique quotient representative and a root-lattice displacement. The height coordinate must be exactly \(1/2\) in the terminal \(R^2\) coordinate; the residual squared norm \(5/2\) gives the independent nonnegative budget on all other blocks. Their bounded residue enumeration therefore cannot omit any target. Candidate pairs within the same \(T\)-coset give the same integrality profile against every dual vector.
5. My checker groups candidates directly by the closed integrality matrix rows: in doubled horizontal coordinates with common denominator \(D\), affine dot is \((p^T M q+2D^2)/(4D^2)\). Equal rows give adjacent true twins and a uniform relation between groups. These compress to 4/39/4/39 groups rather than the old, finer HNF-discriminant groups; each real compatible clique lies in an expanded maximal clique of this quotient. Exact Bron–Kerbosch yields four 216-vertex branches in every case, additionally thirty-six 93-vertex branches in each odd case. All 216 branches have independently checked proper 36-colorings by pairs of coordinate-block projections modulo independent signs. An alternative exact certificate for **all 72 branches of size 93**: each complement splits into six connected components of size 8 and three of size 15; each component has independence number exactly 4 by the include/exclude recurrence \(\alpha(S)=\max(\alpha(S-\{v\}),1+\alpha(S-N[v]))\). Thus the compatibility clique maximum there is exactly \(6\cdot 4+3\cdot 4=36\), without reliance on the author's clique-search branching bound.
6. The 36 cyclic Latin-square triples give an exact control compatible with each wing union, as both their membership in the direct candidate list and all coordinate agreement counts are checked. Together with the 21 wings they attain 57. The differential between prior 57-lattice classification, prior maximality for fixed full 57, and a core-only bound is therefore confirmed.
