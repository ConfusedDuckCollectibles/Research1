# Independent size-170 audit

**Pass:** There is no 170-point spectral pair in \(\mathbb F_{17}^3\). The narrow LP gate, Boolean-shadow reductions, exact atlas and both endgames check. This does not settle all \(\mathbb F_{17}^3\) Fuglede cardinalities.

## LP and geometric reduction

Use \(E\subset V\), \(A\subset V^*\) in dual copies. A projective Fourier zero is a uniform normal by prime cyclotomic irreducibility; for the spectral pair, \(D(A)\subseteq U_E\) and \(D(E)\subseteq U_A\). The familiar pencil arguments ([Ferguson–Iosevich, Lemma 8.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.30712v1)) give affine caps 17 per plane and \(\min(10,17-10)=7\) per line. Verify each member has at least one pencil with at least 15 uniform normals: otherwise its complementary active/nonnull set is a 4-fold projective blocker. Take an inclusion-minimal 4-fold blocker \(B\) there. With \(b=|B|\), the balanced witness \(h=\mathbf1_B+(b-4p)\delta_0\), lifting projective points to punctured lines, vanishes at every nonzero difference of the partner and has \(\widehat h(v)=p(|B\cap v^\perp|-4)\ge0\) for \(v\ne0\), \(\widehat h(0)=p(b-4)\). By [Malikiosis’s Fourier LP method](https://arxiv.org/html/2212.13592), \(|A| \le p^2(b-4p)/(b-4)\). [Bishnoi–Mattheus–Schillewaert, Theorem 1.1](https://doi.org/10.37236/7810) gives \(b-4\le(17/2)(\sqrt{233}+3)\), so \(b\le159\) and the resulting upper bound is \(289\cdot91/155<170\). Equivalently the unrounded strict comparison is \(49\cdot233<107^2\). This is a fourfold extension of the witness construction; directly quoting Malikiosis’s threefold Eq. (6.1) would not reach 170. The witness argument holds after interchanging the partners.

A pencil with 15 uniform normals yields an injective projection along its annihilator: if a fiber contains \(a\) points, the pencil identity \(170+17a\le15\cdot10+3\cdot17=201\) gives \(a\le1\). The planar shadow is Boolean of size 170, and its complement has size \(119=7\cdot17\) and at most three special (nonuniform) directions. [Ghidelli, Theorem 1.3](https://arxiv.org/abs/1903.03881) gives at least \(\lceil(17+7+2)/(7+1)\rceil=4\) special directions unless the complement is contained in seven lines. Equality of total cardinality with capacity \(7\cdot17\) forces those lines disjoint, full and parallel. The original shadow is ten full parallel lines, so each member has ten full 17-point layers.

Each 17-point cap-7 planar section determines at least 14 directions. `independent-planar17.cjs` fixes one point to the origin after PGL normalization of any five missing directions and branches on the shortest remaining required line among the five parallel classes. Full coverage follows because exactly 17 points would use each line once per missing direction. The four tree counts were 9352, 8716, 9479, 9149 with zero witnesses, and a cap-17 control had 115. Given a layer normal \(a\) of \(A\), this supplies at least 14 labels in \(U_E\cap a^\perp\). Furthermore \([a]\notin U_A\), so \([a]\notin D(E)\) and projection of \(E\) along \(a\) is injective. Its Boolean complement now has at most four special directions. Wang’s [v2 Theorem 1.2, Table 3](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.20495v2) permits exactly-four sets over \(\mathbb F_{17}\) only at 136 and 153, not 119 or 170. Hence the preceding line-cover argument applies again and gives a layer normal \(e\in a^\perp\) of \(E\).

The fresh self-contained shadow proof also passes. At exactly four special labels normalize forms to \((x,y,y-x,y-rx)\). The Boolean Radon sum equals \(21+17\mathbf1_C\); coefficients above degree two vanish by the Vandermonde rank (three y-containing monomials first isolate the three finite directions). A nonconstant residue-affine profile has least mass 136>119, and a nonuniform constant-residue profile must be a union of seven full lines (only one special direction). Thus quadratic leads are all nonzero, proportional to \((r(1-r),r-1,-r,1)\); translations kill their linear terms simultaneously as this rank-two translation family is the entire linear kernel. Distinct linear forms are independent under uniform planar coordinates: their centered profiles are orthogonal, and the squared Radon identity gives total profile sum-of-squares \(1190\), or 35 when divided by 34. `independent-shadow.cjs` enumerates every lead, constant, and lift of residue 0 to 17, then exact admissible lead tuples; 96 tuples pass this variance and none pass the constant congruence. This independently verifies the required special case of Wang's existing classification, not a new planar classification.

## Charts, atlas and final contradiction

Choose a primal basis \(v_2=a,\ e(v_3)=1\), completing with \(v_1\in\ker e\), along with its dual basis. Since \([a]\notin D(E)\) and \([e]\notin D(A)\), these are linked graphs \(E=(x,f_s(x),s), A=(u,t,g_t(u))\) with \(|S|=|T|=10\); this construction works even if a fixed dot product represents a perpendicular pair as the same isotropic point.

For an active \(r\), meaning not all \(C_s(r)=\sum_x \zeta^{rx+f_s(x)}\) vanish, let \(I_r\) be all cross slopes \((g_t(u)-g_{t'}(u-r(t-t')))/(t-t')\), \(t\ne t'\). The normalized difference is \((r,1,w)\), so every \(w\in I_r\) annihilates the same sum \(\sum_s C_s(r)\zeta^{w s}\). The [Chebotarev–Tao bound](https://arxiv.org/abs/math/0308286) gives \(|I_r|\le|\{s:C_s(r)\ne0\}|-1\le9\). There are at least 14 active \(r\) because each 17-point \(f_s\) graph determines at least 14 finite slopes, and \(C_s(r)=0\) iff \(f_s(x)+rx\) permutes the field. No conclusion about inactive charts is used.

`independent-atlas.cjs` generates 75 affine orbits on the 19448 ten-point domains and 95 on the 24310 forbidden eight-slope masks, by explicitly applying all 272 affine maps to each first unmarked minimum bitmask. This covers every domain/slope pair since first affinely relabeling \(t\) only changes slopes by an affine map, and then \(y\mapsto cy+dt+b\), \(c\ne0\), realizes any further affine map on slopes without moving that domain. A palette of fewer than nine allowable slopes can be enlarged to nine. Fixed normalized height at one label removes only common height translations. The MRV exact search propagates 17-bit candidate-height domains under every allowed pair difference and forbids any eighth point on an already saturated line; a full leaf is independently rechecked by all pair directions and line pair multiplicities. Results: 7125 cells, 558464 nodes, 9 nonempty distinct domain orbits, 10 normalized heights (8 singleton cells, 1 double), max affine rank 1. The expected JSON records all ten rows; direct line-family histograms additionally check all have exactly nine directions and maximum line count six. The minimal-mask double cell has domain 3551 and forbidden mask 765, with equal two-model differences at label pairs \((1,3)\) and \((10,11)\).

**Fixed-pair endgame:** Fix the affine domain relabeling once. For each active \(r\) the rows \(g_t(x+rt)-g_{t_0}(x+rt_0)\), as \(x\) varies, use one palette \(I_r\); all of them are graphs in a common affine plane for that \(x\) and inherit cap seven. The unique viable palette orbit for the fixed normalized domain permits only the indicated fixed difference between two chosen distinct labels. Palette scale/shear does not move those labels and changes their difference by one common scalar and additive constant, independent of \(x\). Thus \(g_t(x+rt)-g_{t'}(x+rt')\) is constant in \(x\) for the same pair at every active \(r\). Comparing two distinct \(r\)'s gives constant finite difference of one \(g\) along a nonzero step, so iteration in the prime field makes both affine. This would give a forbidden 17-point affine line in a layer.

**Independent alternate rank-two endgame:** This avoids the unique palette orbit and marked pairs entirely. For each active \(r\), subtract the row at \(x=0\) and interpolate in \(x\); the nonconstant coefficient rows of the normalized height vector lie in a space of dimension at most one since there are at most two normalized height vectors. Adding back the common polynomial in the baseline label increases rank by at most one. Therefore the shifted nonconstant coefficient matrix has rank at most two. A pair of reduced \(g_t,g_{t'}\) of degree at most eight would have shifted difference image at most two in every active chart, hence constant there (a nonconstant degree-at-most-eight polynomial has fibers of size at most eight and image at least three). The two-chart argument just given forbids such a pair. At least nine \(g_t\) thus have degree at least nine. They have at most two distinct degrees by shifted leading-degree triangular independence. Choose three of one degree \(d\). On rows \(d,d-1,d-2\) their \(3\times3\) determinant is a polynomial in \(r\) of degree at most 3, with the coefficient of \(r^3\) equal to the product of nonzero leading coefficients, nonzero \(\binom d0,\binom d1,\binom d2\), and a Vandermonde of the three distinct labels. Since \(d\le16\), it is nonzero but vanishes at every active \(r\), contradiction.
