# Independent audit: the spectral Chvátal inertia conjecture for near-cones

**Result verified.** If a downset has a coordinate \(i\) with antichain deletion-minus-link residual, there is an explicit disjointness-supported real symmetric matrix with exactly \(|\operatorname{link}_i|\) positive and all remaining eigenvalues negative. In particular this answers the inertia conjecture in Ellis–Filmus–Friedgut §5 for every shifted downset and more generally every one-coordinate-compressed downset (near-cone). Holding the ordinary cross block fixed, this residual condition is also necessary. The proof does not settle that conjecture for general downsets, and the ordinary Chvátal cardinality result for these classes is old.

## Model and congruence

Write \(U=\{A\in D:i\notin A\}\), \(E=\{T:T+i\in D\}\subseteq U\), \(R=U\setminus E\). The vertices of Kneser \(K(D)\) are all faces, including \(\varnothing\) with its allowed loop; intersecting families exclude it. Thus choose
\[
 M=\begin{pmatrix}C&B\\B^\top&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad B(A,T)=1_{A\cap T=\varnothing},
\]
where the star vertices in the second block have labels \(T+i\) and \(C\) must itself be symmetric disjointness-supported on \(U\). The full upper-left/lower-right dimensions are \(|U|,|E|\). All matrices in what follows are finite and real.

Use \(Z_F(A,S)=1_{S\subseteq A}\), with row and column indices in a downset \(F\). Its inverse is \(Z_F^{-1}(A,S)=(-1)^{|A\setminus S|}1_{S\subseteq A}\). This holds because every intermediate subset is present. Put \(S_E=\operatorname{diag}_{T\in E}((-1)^{|T|})\), \(J_E:\mathbb R^E\hookrightarrow\mathbb R^U\) the coordinate embedding. Inclusion-exclusion yields \(B=Z_U J_E S_E Z_E^\top\), because the common subsets of \(A,T\) already belong to \(E\). Congruence via \(\operatorname{diag}(Z_U^{-1},Z_E^{-1})\) makes the cross block \(J_E S_E\); put \(H=Z_U^{-1}C Z_U^{-\top}\). In transformed order \(E_0,E_1,R\) the leading block is \(\begin{pmatrix}H_{EE}&S_E\\S_E&0\end{pmatrix}\) with \((|E|,|E|,0)\) inertia: set \(y'=S_E y+H_{EE}x/2\) to obtain the form \(2x^\top y'\). Its inverse has zero upper-left block, and the coupling to \(R\) enters through \(E_0\) alone, so the Schur correction on \(H_{RR}\) vanishes. Equivalently also add \(H_{ER}z\) to \(y'\). Therefore
\[
 n_{\ge0}(M)=|E|+n_{\ge0}(H_{RR}).
\]
This is a statement about the number of **nonnegative** eigenvalues, including zeros; eigenvalue locations away from signs are not preserved by congruence.

## Exact correction and converse

For \(F\in R\) let \(d_F(A)=1_{A\cap F=\varnothing}\). The rank-two symmetric term \(Q_F=e_Fd_F^\top+d_Fe_F^\top\) is supported on allowable disjoint pairs, including when \(F=\varnothing\). From direct Möbius subtraction,
\[
 (Z_U^{-1}e_F)(A)=(-1)^{|A\setminus F|}1_{F\subseteq A},\qquad
 (Z_U^{-1}d_F)(A)=(-1)^{|A|}1_{A\subseteq F}.
\]
If \(R\) is an antichain, these restrict to \(e_F\) and \((-1)^{|F|}e_F\). Starting \(C=0\), take
\[
 C=-\frac12\sum_{F\in R}(-1)^{|F|}Q_F .
\]
Thus \(H_{RR}=-I\); the certificate’s inertia is \((|E|,|U|,0)\), which is tight since the star of size \(|E|\) itself is an intersecting family. In particular its maximum size is \(|E|\) without assuming Chvátal's theorem. If one instead starts from the ordinary \(U\)-block, its transformed contribution is \(\operatorname{diag}((-1)^{|A|})\); now the correction coefficient must be \(-[1+(-1)^{|F|}]/2\) (minus one for even \(F\), zero for odd \(F\)). This is not the same correction as when starting from zero.

Conversely, \(R=U\setminus E\) is relatively upward closed in \(U\). If it contains a chain, take adjacent \(F,F+j\in R\). Let \(z=e_F+e_{F+j}\), \(w=Z_U^{-\top}z\). Then \(\ker B^\top=Z_U^{-\top}\operatorname{span}\{e_A:A\in R\}\), so \(w\) belongs to this kernel. At \(A\subseteq F\) the two Möbius coefficients cancel, and outside \(A\subseteq F+j\) both vanish. The remaining support is exactly all subsets of \(F+j\) containing \(j\), nonempty and pairwise intersecting. Hence \(w^\top C w=0\) for **every** allowed \(C\). In residual coordinates \(z^\top H_{RR}z=0\), which prevents strict negativity and forces \(n_{\ge0}(M)\ge |E|+1\). This is a rank-one dual stress for the fixed ordinary cross block, not a counterexample to Conjecture I. Nonzero row/column diagonal rescalings of this ordinary \(B\) have the same obstruction since diagonal congruence preserves supports.

A small sharpened bound: for fixed \(j\ne i\), let \(r_j\) count pairs \(F,F+j\in R\) with \(j\notin F\). Their \(z\)'s have disjoint coordinate supports, so the corresponding \(w\)'s are independent. All lie on the \(j\)-star and their whole span is totally isotropic for any allowed \(C\). Thus \(n_{\ge0}(M)\ge |E|+\max_j r_j\), with zero extra bound if no such \(j\). In fact \(r_j=\dim(\ker B^\top\cap\operatorname{span}(j\text{-star in }U))\): if \(w\) is supported on sets containing \(j\), then \(Z_U^\top w\) has equal coordinates at \(F,F+j\) when both present in \(U\), and zero at \(F\) if the partner does not exist. Requiring support on \(R\) admits precisely the named pairs.

## What shifted and generic mean here

The antichain property is **equivalent** to one-coordinate compression: every \(A\in U\) and \(j\in A\) must have \(A-j+i\in D\). If the one-coordinate property holds and \(F\subsetneq G\) both lie in \(R\), replace any \(j\in G\setminus F\) by \(i\); the resulting member contains \(F+i\), contradiction. Conversely a missing \(A-j+i\) means \(A-j\notin E\), so \(A-j,A\in R\), by \(E\)'s downset property, contradicting antichain. Thus for an ordered shifted family take the least coordinate, justifying all ranks and nonpure families. The empty family is vacuous; \(D=\{\varnothing\}\) is certified by \([-1]\), relying on the permitted loop. A ghost coordinate with a larger nontrivial \(D\) cannot meet the antichain criterion.

For an arbitrary allowed cross block \(B\) of rank \(k\), change bases to pair its rank-\(k\) row and column spaces. This gives hyperbolic inertia \((k,k)\), \(e-k\) star null axes for \(e=|E|\), and the compression \(C|_{\ker B^\top}\) (the same zero Schur correction). Thus \(n_{\ge0}(M)=e+n_{\ge0}(C|_{\ker B^\top})\); exact \(e\) means strict negativity on that kernel. By finite-dimensional separation, failure is equivalent to a nonzero \(Y\succeq0\) with range in \(\ker B^\top\) and \(Y_{A,F}=0\) for each disjoint pair in \(U\). Indeed separate the linear subspace of allowed compressions from the open definite cone and use the PSD dual, then lift from the kernel to \(U\). A rank-one \(Y=ww^\top\) occurs exactly when \(w\) is supported on an intersecting family.

For a maximum star \(e\), generic allowed \(B\) can avoid all rank-one stresses: for every intersecting \(\mathcal A\subseteq U\), its link-neighbor disjointness set \(N(\mathcal A)\subseteq E\) has \(|N(\mathcal A)|\ge|\mathcal A|\). Indeed \(\mathcal A\) together with the \(e-|N(\mathcal A)|\) mutually intersecting starred faces is intersecting, and its size is at most \(e\) by the already proved cardinal Chvátal theorem. Apply Hall on each \(\mathcal A\) (all its subfamilies still intersect) and use the nonzero minor implied by one matching; there are finitely many families, so independent generic weights meet all their minors. This proves nothing about high-rank stresses, and neither a proposed spectral certificate for arbitrary \(B\) nor a solution beyond the scoped classes is asserted. One may restrict a search for successful \(B\) to full column rank: add an arbitrarily small nonexceptional multiple of \(B_0\), which has full column rank, and strict negativity on the varying kernels persists by compactness of the unit sphere and continuity.

## Exact controls and scope

Run python3 check.py. This independent pure-Python exact replay uses actual bitset subset/disjointness tests, direct signed Möbius columns, and exact rational diagonal/hyperbolic Schur elimination. It exhausts all 168 labelled downsets on four potential ground elements, including empty and ghost cases: 276 of 672 coordinates yield the exact inertia, and the other 396 have verified rank-one ordinary-\(B\) stresses. It also checks three independent examples.

1. EFF's shifted example \(D=\{A\subseteq[7]:|A|\le3\}\) has inertia \((22,42,0)\). Assigning fractional-independent weight \(1/2\) to triples, \(1/4\) to pairs, zero elsewhere is feasible: at most seven ground elements support any clique of disjoint subsets, which gives \(2n_2+3n_3\le7\) and \(n_2/4+n_3/2\le1\). Total weight \(91/4>22\) proves the certificate is not a disguised disjointness-clique cover for the entire near-cone class.
2. Universal apex \(1\) joined to an induced 4-cycle on \(2,3,4,5\) is a one-coordinate-compressed rank-two downset but is nonshiftable under any order (a shifted graph has no induced \(C_4\): on the induced four with ordered labels \(a<b<c<d\), any edge at \(d\) first forces \(ad\), then \(ad\) forces both \(ab\) and \(ac\), making degree three at \(a\)). It yields inertia \((5,9,0)\). This illustrates equivalence to the conventional near-cone condition rather than being a new class for the old cardinal problem.
3. \(D\) generated by disjoint edges \(12,34\) has chain residual, and ordinary \(B_0\) fails. But set the column for starred face \(1\) equal to one on \(U\) rows \(2,34\) only; set the column for starred face \(12\) equal to one on \(\varnothing,3,4\) only. In \(C\) use \(C_{\varnothing,\varnothing}=-1\), \(C_{2,34}=C_{3,4}=1\) symmetrically, zero elsewhere. On \(\ker B^\top\), \(x_2=-x_{34}\) and \(x_\varnothing=-x_3-x_4\), so the compressed quadratic form is \(-2x_{34}^2-x_3^2-x_4^2\), exact matrix inertia \((2,5,0)\).

An independent elementary class for context, not a claim of priority: any rank-at-most-two downset has Conj. I by [Vizing's classical edge-coloring theorem, 1964 English reprint](https://www.tu-ilmenau.de/fileadmin/Bereiche/MN/komgra/BackMatterWiley.pdf). In the underlying graph the largest star has size \(e=\Delta+1\); color edges with at most \(e\) colors, assign each member singleton to an incident-edge-unused color, and add \(\varnothing\) to any class. The resulting at most \(e\) classes are cliques of mutually disjoint faces. Each \(J-I\) block has exactly one nonnegative eigenvalue, even size one. If the family consists only of \(\varnothing\), use the loop as above. So the matching control is not isolated, whereas the seven-point three-skeleton is beyond exact partition.

## Source check and recommendation

[Ellis–Filmus–Friedgut, arXiv:2609.28404v3 dated 22 October 2026](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2609.28404v3), §5 states Conjecture I as universal tightness of the number-of-nonnegative-eigenvalues inertia bound and allows the empty-face loop; Conjecture H is a different question. Their Theorem 1 gives the cardinal result and §4 the projection-packing result, but §5 explicitly says their proofs do not supply either conjecture's tight weighting. [Chang–Liu–Liu arXiv:2609.19123](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19123) prove the underlying general cardinal and correlation statements, not this particular inertia certificate. [Kwan–Wigderson 2024](https://doi.org/10.1112/blms.13127) already provide non-tight inertia bounds for unrestricted graphs, not this downset-Kneser result. The incidence algebra is classical; [Moran–Rashtchian 2016](https://drops.dagstuhl.de/entities/document/10.4230/LIPIcs.MFCS.2016.70), §5, even uses a closely related downset subset-monomial triangular basis and disjointness matrix invertibility, but not a signed near-cone weighting and exact inertia.

The original [Chvátal 1974 pp. 61–66](https://users.encs.concordia.ca/~chvatal/conjecture.pdf) already proved the all-rank left-compressed **cardinality** result. [Snevily 1992 original article](https://doi.org/10.1016/0097-3165(92)90059-4) already proved the cardinal result for exactly the one-coordinate-compressed regime (the publisher's primary abstract explicitly states it). The star-bound class, basic shift concept, and ordinary Chvátal conjecture are not this work's novelty. Snevily's listed result is not a Conjecture I certificate; nor could a universal rank-one-per-clique partition explain the rank-three control documented above. No checked primary source gave the same near-cone tight inertia certificate or fixed-cross iff characterization; the search was bounded, and there is no absolute priority claim.

Recommend a focused theorem note naming **Conjecture I of EFF for near-cones**, plus the ordinary cross-block sharp obstruction. Preserve the distinction from cardinal Chvátal, global spectral conjectures, and changing-weight failures. All exact arithmetic passes; no blocker in the stated mathematical scope.
