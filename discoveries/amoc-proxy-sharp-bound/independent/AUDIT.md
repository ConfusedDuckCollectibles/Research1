### Independent midpoint proof and discrimination

For \(U=\Psi_2-\Psi_1\), fixed support and endpoint traces mean \(U=U'=0\) at both edges. Equal depth-integrated advective heat with common zonally uniform \(T=T_0+T'z\), \(T'\ne0\), gives \(\int U=0\), assuming identical fixed \(\rho_0 c_p\) and no hidden unequal tracer covariance. Integrate \(U''\) successively against \(1,z,z^2\) by parts to obtain zero. With \(t=2(z-z_m)/H\), \(q(t)=U''/\kappa\), \(\kappa=gD/(\rho_0 f)\), the two-sided midpoint kernel is particularly direct:
\[
U(z_m)=\frac H4\int_{z_a}^{z_b}|t|U''(z)\,dz
=\frac{\kappa H^2}{8}\int_{-1}^1|t|q(t)\,dt .
\]
The piecewise-linear Green kernel with left-edge data reduces to the displayed symmetric one after subtracting a first moment; this argument works even before symmetrizing \(q\).

Write \(a=(\sqrt5-1)/4,\ b=(\sqrt5+1)/4\). Replace \(|t|\) by \(\mathcal R(t)=|t|-(t^2+ab)/(a+b)\) in either formula, using the vanished lower moments. The residual is the negative quadratic with roots \(a,b\) on the positive half, with even reflection. Then \(\int\mathcal Rq\le\int|\mathcal R|\) for \(|q|\le1\), and equality requires \(q=\operatorname{sgn}\mathcal R\) a.e. off four points. Its moments vanish since \(b-a=b^3-a^3=1/2\) and the odd moment vanishes by symmetry. The normalized maximizer is therefore unique a.e. for the signed objective, and the negative control is its unique minimizer. Integrating gives \((\sqrt5-2)/16\). The knots are those of the standard unweighted \(L^1\) sign pattern of the Chebyshev polynomial of the second kind \(U_4(t)=16t^4-12t^2+1\), with a minus sign; do not describe them as a new approximation method or as Legendre nodes. If \(\kappa=0\), the bound follows immediately from clamping rather than normalization. The weighted density-contrast scalar \((Hg/(4\rho_0 f))\int\mathcal R(t)\Delta(\rho_E-\rho_W)\,dz\) exactly equals the midpoint difference. The half-diameter minimax statement is for a scalar feasible interval with the declared pairwise cap (or each candidate within \(D/2\) of the same contrast profile), not a Gaussian error claim.

The distinct odd alternating curvature control also passes: on \(r\in[0,1]\) it is \(-,+,-,+\) at breaks \(c=(1-1/\sqrt2)/2,\ 1/2,\ 1-c\). Its moments vanish by antisymmetry and a weighted \(t\)-length split at \(1/\sqrt2\); exact layer propagation verifies \(|p(2c)|=(3-2\sqrt2)/8>0.021>0.015>(\sqrt5-2)/16\). This refutes applying the midpoint constant to every fixed depth; it is not a global optimum or a matched-salt off-center example.

### Physical consistency and strict checks

Use the upward geographic Coriolis direction and downward coordinate \(z\): \(p_x=\rho_0 f v\), \(p_z=g\rho\). With flat fixed walls, integrate the first identity across width then differentiate in depth to obtain \(\Psi''=g(\rho_E-\rho_W)/(\rho_0 f)\) without a leftover width factor. Units are \(\kappa=\mathrm{m/s}\), so \(\kappa H^2\) is volume transport \(\mathrm{m^3/s}\). The example \(v=\Psi'/L,\ \rho=\bar\rho+(\rho_0 f x/g)v',\ p=\bar p+\rho_0 f x v\) satisfies the stationary ideal primitive equations distributionally: all fields are independent of \(y\), velocity is meridional only and divergence-free, tracer advection vanishes, and there is no normal flow through \(x\) sidewalls or horizontal top/bottom and material internal interfaces. Take periodic or open \(y\); do not claim a closed meridional basin. Continuous velocity and pressure avoid interface sheets. Uniform pressure at the two outer surfaces follows from compact support of \(v\); equal surface density/salinity and matched flat SSH follow from the same construction. No claim of linear/nonlinear dynamical or baroclinic stability. Strict positive static stratification can be obtained outside the band simply by choosing a common smooth background with a small positive derivative there.

The prescribed linear EoS gives exactly the printed salinity anomaly. At each depth its zonal mean and its zonal covariance with \(v(z)\) both vanish; these are distinct statements. The common vertically piecewise affine salt profile contributes only \(-\int U\,d\bar S\) to the difference. Its smooth affine part is zero by the heat condition. The two internal margins are \(1/20\) and \((2\sqrt5-1)/20\) kg m\(^{-3}\). Usual physical positivity of \(T,S,\rho\) can be enforced by common intercepts and does not affect the equalities; the scenario remains a stylized box.

For the centered angular \(\sin^2\) baseline, the lower peak gap \(4M(r-1/2)^2\) strictly dominates the upper anomaly gap \(\kappa H^2(r-1/2)^2/4\) for both signs, and above-zero midpoint exceeds the zero exterior. ### Smooth limiting family, not exact attaining

For \(s<1\) close to 1 replace both the baseline and \(U(z)\) by \(s^2F(z_m+(z-z_m)/s)\) and move the four salt switches and two support edges into the reduced band. Their relative salt contribution stays zero, and the curvature cap stays unchanged. Third derivatives in smooth regions scale by \(1/s\), so the verified strict margins persist. Keep the common affine \(T,\bar S\) slope in a buffer containing the entire active band and keep a strictly increasing common density in exterior smooth layers. Then smooth the fields with an even nonnegative unit-mass vertical kernel supported inside the newly freed margins. Derive pressure from the smoothed densities and velocities, or equivalently convolve the extended hydrostatic solution. The linear EoS and balance commute with averaging, static stratification survives distributional convolution, and heat, support and curvature constraints remain exact. The coefficient of the inner salt jump amplitude in the residual salt moment tends to a nonzero positive multiple of \(2p_{\rm in}\); its unique nearby adjustment restores exact salt equality without eating the strict margins. The peak remains centered by evenness, \(C^2\) convergence in a fixed neighborhood of the center, and a uniform positive peak gap outside it. Each smooth pair shares its own proxy observations; this is a class-wide sharp supremum, not proof that some independently mandated data fiber contains all the approximants. Continuous \(U''\) cannot equal the four-switch profile a.e., so a nonzero smooth extremizer cannot attain the exact value.
