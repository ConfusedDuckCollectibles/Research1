# Exact midpoint ambiguity of an AMOC-like section transport under heat and pressure matching
A theorem about a prescribed idealized balanced section. No data fit or inference of an actual ocean trend is made.

## Pre-set comparison and data gate
Review target: a ≥1 Sv/decade difference between two frozen, matched-observable 26.5°N depth-space proxy trends at one-sided 5% and 80% power with free holdout intercept, after checking calendar lineage, shared forcing/calibration, and temporal autocorrelation. Analytic pivot target: a balanced, statically admissible same-observation pair with ≥2 Sv difference in depth maximum and a sharp tradeoff, not a hidden arbitrary unphysical profile. This is a measurement-model theorem, not a test of the sign of the observed AMOC trend.

## Sharp analytic result (new scoped diagnostic)
Coordinates: z positive downward, x zonal distance from section center (width L), y north. Fixed-latitude, flat-bottom, vertical sidewalls, Boussinesq hydrostatic/inviscid f-plane, constant f>0, g and inertial ρ0. No wind term in the witness, or same known term subtracted in the balanced transport diagnostic; no allowance for unmatched ageostrophic flow, β-plane/topography, nonlinear equation of state, eddy tracer covariance, or vertically unresolved velocity outside the declared band. The physically constructed pair is stationary and hydrostatic/geostrophic, not asserted immune to dynamical/baroclinic instability or persistent in the real ocean. Mathematical admissibility here includes strict positive downwards density gradient in smooth layers and positive jumps across piecewise smooth material interfaces. Pressure and velocity are continuous, density piecewise bounded with finite jumps; no tangential vortex sheet. No interventions are proposed.

Define section volume Ψ_j(z)=∫_0^z L v_j(s)ds (here v_j uniform zonally, the thermal wind identity for section transport also applies with zonally varying v if f constant and walls fixed). The pair is volume balanced, with same Ψ and derivative outside common internal interval [z_a,z_b], H=z_b−z_a, and their common T(z) is affine with nonzero gradient in that band, uniform zonally. The difference U=Ψ_2−Ψ_1 thus has U=U'=0 at both edges even for weak derivatives; equivalently the two have equal edge transports and equal cumulative flow there. Surface and bottom pressure matching alone does not fix an internal anomaly band; it is explicitly stipulated for this H, and a fully general entire-column version requires those pressure data at outer edges and total volume closure, with affine T through the entire column. Use exact equal section advective heat, computed with constant ρ0 c_p (or equal overturning-only heat after externally calibrated gyre/eddy part); not numerically equal upstream air–sea heat loss. This gives ∫ U dz=0 by parts, because Q_2−Q_1=ρ0 c_p∫ T U' dz=−ρ0 c_p T'∫ U dz. Surface salinities and full-depth salt flux are also equal in the physical extremizers below, but not needed for the upper bound.

D is a declared deterministic *pairwise pointwise* bound on the difference between candidates' eastern-minus-western density contrast profiles in the band, D=ess sup_z |(ρ_E,2−ρ_W,2)−(ρ_E,1−ρ_W,1)|, not a measured RAPID error or an individual sensor standard deviation. Two candidates each within δ of a common density-contrast product differ pairwise by at most 2δ; if separate endpoint budgets are each δ per candidate, a conservative pairwise cap is 4δ. Under hydrostatic-geostrophic balance, p_z=gρ, p_x=ρ0 f v, hence U''=(g/(ρ0 f))Δ(ρ_E−ρ_W); let κ=gD/(ρ0 f). With center z_m=(z_a+z_b)/2 the exact conditional sharp bound is
|Ψ_2(z_m)−Ψ_1(z_m)| ≤ ((√5−2)/16) (gD/(ρ0 f)) H².
It is an exact identification diameter attainable by piecewise smooth physical fields with the specified observations. As a centered minimax recovery problem with pairwise diameter D, the largest uncertainty radius is half this diameter. It is not a bound on the maximum for arbitrary unknown turning depth; the two attaining fields below have their genuine full-column maxima at the midpoint, so the same ambiguity can occur for the actual max metric.

### Proof and explicit sharpness
Set r=(z−z_a)/H, t=2r−1, and u(r)=U''(z)/κ. Boundary and closure give ∫_0^1 u dr=∫_0^1 r u dr=∫_0^1 r² u dr=0, from U'(1)=0, U(1)=0, and ∫U=0. Center objective is unchanged by reflecting about r=.5 and averaging, since endpoints and zero derivatives ensure reflected U integrates the reflected u uniquely. Symmetrized |u|≤1. In t≥0 denote this even control by h(t); it satisfies ∫h=∫t² h=0, and U(center)/(κH²)=¼∫_0^1 t h(t)dt. Let a=(√5−1)/4, b=(√5+1)/4; b−a=b³−a³=1/2. The polynomial P(t)=(t²+ab)/(a+b) interpolates t at a,b. For any allowed h, ∫ t h=∫(t−P)h≤∫|t−P|, attained exactly by h=sgn(t−P): −,+,− on [0,a],[a,b],[b,1], because its two constrained moments vanish. The payoff is ¼(b²−a²−1/2)=(√5−2)/16, with opposite sign attainable. On r∈[0,1], the resulting U/(κH²) is the explicit C¹, piecewise quadratic compactly supported function p(r)=−r²/2+∑_{j=1}^4 (−1)^(j−1)(r−r_j)_+² with r_j=((3−√5)/8,(5−√5)/8,(3+√5)/8,(5+√5)/8). It vanishes in value and derivative at both edges, has zero mean, p(.5)>0 and p'(.5)=0, and p(r_1)=p(r_4)=(-7+3√5)/64<0, p(r_2)=p(r_3)=(-11+5√5)/64>0. Golden ratio φ=(1+√5)/2 satisfies p(r_1)+φ p(r_2)=0 exactly. These controls are exact, not sampled approximations. In a strict C∞ class, the bang-bang second derivative is not attainable: dual equality would force its sign away from the four zeros. The same sharp value remains the supremum for smooth, statically stable pairs (construction below).

### Same-observation balanced physical extremizers
Select z_a=100 m, z_b=1900 m, z_m=1000 m, H=1800 m, total depth >1900 m (e.g. 4000m), f=6.5e−5 s^-1, g=9.81 m s^-2, ρ0=1025 kg m^-3, D=0.30 kg m^-3, M=17 Sv=17e6 m³/s. Pick base streamfunction B(z)=M sin²(πr) inside band, zero otherwise (C¹), and Ψ_2=B+U/2, Ψ_1=B−U/2. They have common max z_m: at u0=r−.5, M−B=M sin²(πu0)≥4M u0², while ½|U(z)−U(z_m)|≤κH² u0²/4 from U'(z_m)=0 and second derivative cap. Explicit 16M>κH², so other depths in band are strictly lower; outside the band both are exactly zero and midpoint values >0. Depth maxima thus differ by U(z_m)=2.1116169861 Sv; this calculation uses approximate f for the declared illustrative midlatitude box, not real RAPID error bounds or reconstructed anomaly. 2 Sv threshold is pairwise D=0.2841424387 kg m^-3 for these declared parameters.

Physical fields: v_j=Ψ_j'/L. East-west coordinate x∈[-L/2,L/2], density ρ_j(x,z)=\barρ(z)+(ρ0 f x/g)v_j'(z) and pressure p_j(x,z)=\bar p(z)+ρ0 f x v_j(z), with \bar p'=g\barρ, flat atmospheric pressure and matched sea surface. Velocity vector (0,v_j(z),0) and tracers invariant northward (use periodic/open y channel, not blocked ends); nonlinear advective acceleration, advection of each tracer, and divergence vanish. Hydrostatic and geostrophic equations exact in the ideal model, lateral no normal flow at vertical x walls and no flow across top/bottom. Since v and v' vanish near surface and bottom in both, identical pressure everywhere on those surfaces, equal sea surface height, temperature and salinity; \barρ and both zonal-mean tracer profiles are shared. Potential-temperature gradient T'=-1/90 K/m in the band, common salt profile \bar S'=-1/900 psu/m between jumps (continue common smooth bounded profiles outside, density nondecreasing). Set α=1/5000 K^-1, β=1/1250 psu^-1 and linear EoS ρ=ρ0[1−α(T−T_ref)+β(S−S_ref)], using S_j=\bar S+(f x/(g β))v_j'; T=T(z). Zonal anomaly in salinity is antisymmetric and v_j uniform x, so its advective covariance vanishes at each depth. Thus salt transportation difference is exactly ∫\bar S U' dz=−∫ U d\bar S, and both have same freshwater transport (fixed reference salinity and zero total volume). The affine part of differential heat/salt cancels because ∫U=0.

The common staircase density jump (via salinity only) is J_o=1/5 kg/m³ at the two outer r_j; J_i=φ/5 kg/m³ at the two inner r_j; J_e=1/2 kg/m³ at each band edge. These are compensating stratification jumps in the model, not measured haloclines. Salt moment from the four internal jumps is exactly zero by p_outer+φ p_inner=0; edge masses multiply U=0. Common baseline smooth density derivative on band is ρ0(α/90−β/900)>0; perturbation |∂_zρ−\barρ'|≤ρ0 f 4π³M/(2gH³), since U'''=0 between switches, and this is rigorously smaller. For an internal jump subtract at most D/2 in either state, less than J_o and J_i; at each band edge subtract at most (ρ0 f/(2g))*[2π²M/H²+κ/2]<1/2, so density jumps downward are strictly positive in both. One-sided states match pressure and velocity at all interfaces; piecewise equation holds distributionally. Smooth strict stability and the exact salt match are attained arbitrarily close to the upper bound: first shrink support about midpoint en bloc by factor 1−ε with κ unchanged, rescale the streamfunctions by the square of the support contraction to preserve the curvature cap, then convolve velocities, density, Ψ and common salinity vertically with the same compact even positive kernel of much smaller width than the freed margins. Differential U retains zero integral and bound, common pressure/geostrophic/hydrostatic and continuity hold, and T stays affine on active/buffer region (continue it slightly). Density monotonicity preserved by convolution if one first picks common positive margin. For salt, use that ∫ U_smooth dS_smooth is linear in the common inner jump amplitude and its coefficient has a strictly positive unsmoothed limit. Keep other jumps fixed and adjust the common inner jump amplitude continuously back to exact zero, preserving strict jump/derivative margin (equivalently adjust pre-convolution amplitude so original piecewise profiles remain stably stratified). For arbitrarily small smoothing, centered profiles remain symmetric and 16M>κH² is strict; by C² uniform convergence near midpoint and a uniform positive baseline gap away, both keep the unique max there. Original strict cap approximated from below. This supplies a supremum, no claim of exact bang-bang equality with continuous density. Observations are matched within each smooth pair; they need not be identical across the sequence of smooth approximants.

### Scope against sources / what observation matters
McCarthy et al. 2015 https://doi.org/10.1016/j.pocean.2014.10.006 and [Waldman et al. 2021, §3e Eq. (16)](https://doi.org/10.1175/JPO-D-19-0284.1), including its piecewise-linear depth kernel, already use endpoint density, mass closure and geostrophic separation. Buckley and Marshall https://doi.org/10.1002/2015RG000493 §2 and Tooth et al. https://doi.org/10.1029/2023GL107399 give standard depth heat–volume definitions and distinct covariances; no claim those kernels are new. Deep boundary reconstruction/instrument sparsity was examined by https://journals.ametsoc.org/view/journals/atot/30/4/jtech-d-12-00149_1.xml and https://doi.org/10.1029/2025JC023093, not reproduced here. Best L¹ interpolation, sign/zero-moment duality and perfect splines are classical optimal-recovery tools (e.g. https://doi.org/10.1007/978-3-0348-9110-3_11); scoped contribution is exact conditional numerical threshold for this combination of proxies and a matched salt/pressure physically balanced attainability certificate. No match found in screened primary ocean work. This specifically does NOT undermine full-depth east/west hydrography: if it directly identifies the entire contrast, D=0 and (with reference fixed) there is no such ambiguity. One explicit efficient discriminant is the corresponding depth-weighted endpoint-density contrast (weight proportional to |t|−P(t) after reflecting; using lower moments as controls); direct matched boundary profiles or such independent shear information are outside proxy-only match. Do not transport the illustrative D=.30 number to actual sensor precision, observed decline or global AMOC. More realistically calibrated theorem would require independently anchored D(z), nonaffine zonal temperature/covariance and out-of-band support, and would change the extremal dual.

## Scope addendum: off-center evaluation is different
The sharp midpoint coefficient cannot be transferred to every depth, even under the same edge and heat constraints. In dimensionless r, take curvature s(r) with signs −,+,−,+ separated at c=(1−1/√2)/2, 1/2 and 1−c. This control is odd under reflection about the midpoint, giving ∫s=∫(r−1/2)²s=0; its remaining moment vanishes since on t=2r−1∈[0,1] the weighted lengths ∫_0^(1/√2) t dt and ∫_(1/√2)^1 t dt both equal 1/4. The twofold primitive P with P(0)=P'(0)=0 therefore meets both right-edge conditions and zero heat moment. At r=2c its value equals −(2c)²/2+(2c−c)²=−c²; thus |P|=(3−2√2)/8≈0.0214466, greater than (√5−2)/16≈0.0147542. For an exact comparison, 1.4142<√2<1.4143 and 2.2360<√5<2.2361 follow by squaring rational bounds. This is a separate mathematical counterexample under edge+heat constraints only, not a claim of the sharp global coefficient or a re-use of the physical matching salt staircase at an off-center peak. The explicit balanced pair in the main theorem keeps both maxima exactly centered.
