# Size 34 in F_17^3: no seven-uniform pencil

Private proof and replay checkpoint, 26 November 2026. **Theorem.** In a hypothetical 34-point spectral pair in F_17^3, every projective line contains at most six uniform normals for either member, and every affine plane contains at most six points of either member. In particular each difference-direction set has at most 91 projective points. This strengthens the earlier cap of seven; it does not exclude cardinality 34. No use of the independent size-204 closure.

## Definitions and independent plane-cap refinement

A normal is uniform for E if every plane of the parallel family contains two points. For a spectral pair (E,A), D(A) is contained in the uniform set U(E), and conversely (the square character matrix is unitary after scalar normalization and so is its transpose). Here D refers to projectivized difference directions; complex Fourier zero and uniformity are equivalent by the minimal cyclotomic polynomial. We use the published first F17 reductions [Research1, pinned checkpoint](https://github.com/ConfusedDuckCollectibles/Research1/blob/fb527f58b5bb9a89c7f78cf87be165e32d238fa4/discoveries/fuglede-prime-cubic-moments/CHECKPOINT.md), giving line cap two, plane cap seven, and at most seven uniform normals per pencil for both members. The nearby primary baseline is [Ferguson–Iosevich §§8–9](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.30712v1), which excludes size 22 at p=11 by this type of Radon-profile argument; it does not do the present p=17 gates.

There is already a sharper affine plane cap of six. If seven points of E occupy a plane, no three are collinear. The 21 pairs contribute at most three per direction, so their D has at least seven and, from the partner cap, exactly seven. This is impossible over F17: the classical [Beutelspacher–Wettl 1993, Theorem 2.3(d)](https://doi.org/10.1007/BF01388411) applied to the arc and all its 11 missing points on infinity gives the necessary divisibility of 16,17,or 18 by seven. The finite replay is self-contained even without importing that theorem. Up to PGL(2,17), there are ten possible seven-direction masks, normalized to contain infinity. Any hypothetical 7-cap determines every allowed direction, so translate one vertical secant to begin at (0,0) and scale both coordinates by a common field scalar to make its other endpoint (0,1); this leaves slopes unchanged. The remaining points are enumerated in lex order, allowing only permitted directions and no collinear three. The ten recursion counts are 125,156,109,111,97,133,101,126,105,117 (1,180 total); no case reaches seven. This also has an independent triangle-normalized check by the auditor. Thus moving forward all occupancy profiles have values in [0,6].

## Exactly seven uniform normals: algebra and gauges

Suppose a projective line has exactly seven uniform directions for E. Project onto the quotient plane along the annihilator of this line. Let f be the resulting integer multiplicity (0,1,2 by the line cap; it need not be Boolean). Select coordinates with a uniform normal vertical. The eleven actual bad normals are the distinct finite slopes s_i, and g_i(t) are their parallel plane populations, each mass 34 and values between zero and six. The integer identity is

    Σ_i g_i(y−s_i x) = 20+17 f(x,y).

It counts all masses once, the reference point 18 times, and subtracts the seven uniform line masses. Write g_i as its reduced polynomial modulo 17. For each j>0, comparing homogeneous terms gives Σ_i c_(i,j)s_i^h=0 for 0≤h≤j. With w_i=1/Π_{k≠i}(s_i−s_k), the nullspace is exactly c_(i,j)/w_i=R_j(s_i), deg R_j≤9−j for 1≤j≤9, and c_(i,j)=0 if j≥10. This is the usual Lagrange basis argument, as in F–I Lemma 8.4; binomial factors are invertible at all reduced degrees. Constants must sum to 20 mod17. The projection formula is needed only as a necessary condition: no choice passes even the requisite modular coefficients; the proof does not infer realizability from any partial system.

We normalize the bad 11-mask using all projectivities mapping an ordered pair of bad points to 0,1 and a good point to infinity. The resulting normalized finite masks (0,1 contained) have count C(15,9)=5005, covered by ten orbits. Any normalized image with these labels has distinct preimages of the stated type, so the traversal is exhaustive. The alternate all-projective-mask enumeration has 31,824 masks: seven orbits of size 2,448 and three of size 4,896. These coordinate changes act on actual plane profiles by argument scales, not merely by exchanging raw coefficient labels. The implementation takes numeric slopes in each representative and regenerates the allowable profiles at their dictated leading coefficients, so the scale is respected.

A common simultaneous scaling of x and y scales the degree-j coefficient of every g_i by u^j for any u≠0 and leaves the slopes fixed. A common translation shifts the argument on each slope by h+k*s_i; in a profile of degree d its next coefficient changes by d times its leading coefficient times that shift. Thus that coefficient can be killed on any chosen two slopes with nonzero leading coefficient; alternatively an everywhere proportional affine pattern can be killed everywhere. Individually, every degree d≤9 profile has a unique centered translate (coefficient degree d−1 zero); raw bounds and mass survive these shifts/scales.

The least possible residue mass for nonconstant degrees 1..6 is 136,102,85,51,51,34 (verified by complete centered coefficient census). Degree zero gives constant occupancy two because all individual values are strictly below 17, so cannot be a bad slope. Therefore the possible highest degree is 6,7,8,or 9.

## Highest-degree six branch

All eleven profiles have degree six. The leading weighted values Q(s_i)=c_(i,6)/w_i form a polynomial Q of degree≤3, nonzero at all slots. Sixth powers give the nonzero squares, so normalize first actual lead c_(0,6) to 1 or 3. The weighted degree-five coefficient has form R(s)=s(s−1)H(s), deg H≤2 by translating to kill it at the first two slopes. For a given actual leading coefficient there is exactly one centered sextic of mass 34 (sixteen total, all cap six and even), and its shift is h_i=R(s_i)/(6Q(s_i)). Let A_i=Q(s_i)c_(i,4,centered)/w_i. Taylor expansion gives

    Q(s_i) R_4(s_i) = A_i − R(s_i)^2,

because binom(6,2)/6²=−1 mod17. Thus the A_i interpolate a polynomial A of degree≤8, independent of the shift, and R²=A modulo Q. These necessary checks are followed by explicit quartic coefficient equations even when deg Q<3 (divisibility alone need not constrain quotient degree to five). Among 53,360 leading gauges, 170 pass degree eight. There are 276 shift choices matching the congruence, 152 pass quartic equations, four pass constants, and zero meet the remaining degrees.

## Highest-degree seven branch

Let Q=R_7 have degree≤2, allowing up to two zero slots, which must be sextics. Normalize the first nonzero actual septuple lead to 1 (seventh powers permute nonzero elements). There are 307 valid leading gauges per mask including all positions of first nonzero. Translate to kill R=R_6 at the first two nonzero-leading slopes. Thus R of degree≤3 is a product of their two linear roots and an arbitrary linear factor. For every septic slot take any of its three centered cap-six profiles; put A_i=Q(s_i)c_(i,5,centered)/w_i. As binom(7,2)/7²=−2 mod17, 

    Q(s_i) R_5(s_i) = A_i − 2R(s_i)^2.

At septic slots, A_i must interpolate a degree≤6 polynomial (at least nine septic slots, so there is uniqueness). Generate it from seven and check each remaining septic slot, retaining all centered forms matching its prescribed coefficient. Check that A−2R² is divisible by Q with quotient degree≤4. At any sextic root, R(s_i) must be nonzero and gives actual leading coefficient w_i R(s_i); the quotient R_5 gives the next coefficient and hence unique translate of the centered sextic. At each septic slot the shift is R(s_i)/(7Q(s_i)). Check the resulting profiles. Across ten masks, 6,714,090 first-seven assignments yield 52,833 degree-six polynomial matches and 48,537 complete profiles after enforcing the weighted fifth; zero meet the quartic coefficient equations.

## Highest-degree eight branch

Q=R_8 is affine, nonzero at all but at most one bad slot. Eighth powers are ±1, so normalize the first nonzero actual degree-eight lead to one of 1..8. Translate to kill R=R_7 at the first two octic slopes, making it rho times the product of their two linear factors. The cap-six centered octic list has 136 forms for leading representatives 1..8 and 272 all-lead forms (scale the argument by 3 to switch lead sign). At each octic slot let A_i=Q(s_i)c_(i,6,centered)/w_i. Since binom(8,2)/8²=10 mod17,

    Q(s_i) R_6(s_i) = A_i + 10R(s_i)^2.

Therefore all octic A_i must interpolate a polynomial A of degree≤4; pick any five slots (smallest domains in the replay), interpolate and test all remaining octic slots, returning all shapes with each needed c6. Require A+10R² divisible by Q with quotient degree≤3; if Q is constant, impose that degree bound directly. At a zero slot of Q, the dictated actual c7=w_i R(s_i) and c6=w_i R_6(s_i) decide all cases: if c7≠0, any centered septic of that lead is shifted by h=c6/(7c7); if c7=0, c6 must be nonzero and the unique centered sextic is allowed all shifts. Thus no mixed degree case is omitted. At octic slots the shift is h_i=R(s_i)/(8Q(s_i)). Then weighted c5 must have degree≤4, so generate it from five slots with fewest surviving shape alternatives and check every remaining slot, retaining ties. Of 377 leading gauges (after the inadmissible zero-inventory leads), 2,283,597 first-five A-prefixes yield 2,059,611 interpolated A matches. There are 2,059,283 possible divisible phases and 2,052,278 after requiring all slots have a legal profile. The complete c5 stage has 30,644 tuples; zero meet the quartic coefficient equations.

The octic enumeration is direct, not from examples: split any centered g(t)=E(t²)+tO(t²) with E quartic, O quadratic. For t=1..8 and fixed O, the difference of the two integer pair values g(t),g(−t) must be the unique representative of their difference residue in [−8,8] and have absolute value d_t≤6. The possible pair means are d_t/2+k in the field for 0≤k≤6−d_t; actual pair mass is d_t+2k. Choose the four smallest domains and test all other positions including zero and total mass 34. ## Highest-degree nine branch

All eleven profiles must have degree nine: R_9 is a nonzero constant. Ninth powers permute the nonzero elements, so normalize that constant to 1. R_8 is affine, hence a single translation kills it on all eleven slopes (after dividing by constant R_9); all profiles are now centered. Enumerate the centered monic degree-nine forms g(t)=E(t²)+tO(t²), deg E≤3 and O monic quartic. Fixed O, list every bounded possible ±t pair in [0,7] with the requisite difference, choose the four most restrictive midpoints, interpolate E and check remaining positions and mass 34. This includes all 17^4 odd parts; 23,638 viable odd parts and 1,779,218 tested interpolation leaves yield 336 mass-34 centered monic forms with maxima histogram 4:7, 5:57, 6:124, 7:148. Applying the newly proved plane cap six leaves 188. Any other nonzero lead is generated by unique input scale from monic.

For each normalized pencil rep and each allowed weighted c7 quadratic (all 17^3 assignments at first three slopes), select four slopes with smallest c6 allowed sets given those c7 values. Interpolate weighted c6 cubics from these four, discarding any whose required prefix is absent elsewhere. Then choose five slots with fewest surviving profiles, interpolate the weighted fifth (degree≤4) from their candidate values, and enumerate all completions matching it, allowing multiple shapes at tied prefixes. Exact totals over ten reps: 49,130 quadratics; 132,509,280 first-four cubic prefix choices; 2,807,552 complete cubic patterns; 2,828,549 first-five choices; ten complete weighted fifth tuples. ## Replay, conclusion, remaining scope

The standard-library Node verifier is `verify-k7.cjs`. Run it without arguments for everything, or with any subset of g 6 7 8 9 to replay particular stages. `expected.json` gives per-mask and aggregate counts. Example wall times were about 6.6s (degree 6), 52.0s (degree 7), 79.7s (degree 8), 57.6s (degree 9 after its catalogue), and negligible planar DFS. All checks are integer/field arithmetic. The interpolation choices reduce search by necessary coefficient equations, and all remaining branches are enumerated. 

Every possible nonuniform profile's degree is accounted for. Hence no K=7 pencil survives, and both U sets meet every projective line in at most six points. By duality this also bounds D(E) and D(A) line intersections; taken through any point of a nonempty D, the 18 lines give |D|≤1+18*(6−1)=91. [Ball's multiple-blocking theorem, Theorem 4.2(ii)](https://doi.org/10.1112/jlms/54.3.581) improves the bound to 86: the complement of each uniform set in PG(2,17) meets every line in at least 12 points and therefore has at least 221 points. The difference sets inherit the bound. Either count guarantees at least seven parallel secants among the 561 unordered pairs. Alternatively an explicit K6 pencil has L=12 profiles of degree≤10; its cap6 even/odd catalogue and higher coupling are not computed here. The cardinality remains open, and no other p17 branch has been touched.
