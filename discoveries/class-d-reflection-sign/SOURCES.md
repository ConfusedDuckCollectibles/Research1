# Primary work, overlap and what is added

## Direct scattering and paired-count precedents

- [Akhmerov, Dahlhaus, Hassler, Wimmer and Beenakker, *Quantized conductance at the Majorana phase transition in a disordered superconducting wire*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1009.5542) (2011), and [Fulga et al., *Scattering formula for the topological quantum number of a disordered multi-mode wire*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1101.1749) (2011): the sign of the class-D reflection determinant, the associated real basis, and ideal long-wire scattering interpretation are established. The former concerns quantized thermal conductance and electrical shot noise at a phase transition, not recovery of the open two-mode reflection determinant by this local count statistic.
- [Beenakker, Dahlhaus, Wimmer and Akhmerov, *Random-matrix theory of Andreev reflection from a topological superconductor*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1012.0932) (2011): pure reflection, Andreev-eigenvalue degeneracy and conductance statistics are prior. In contrast the reflection block here can lose quasiparticles by transmission. For one physical electron channel, the reflection determinant is already the elementary particle-hole intensity difference \(|r_{ee}|^2-|r_{he}|^2\); it is not the novelty here.
- [Strübi, Belzig, Schmidt and Bruder, *Full counting statistics of Majorana interferometers*](https://arxiv.org/html/1506.08774v2) (2015), especially Eq. (2) and §4.3, is the closest counting prior: it uses the two-filled-electron and empty-hole scattering energy slice, fermionic Slater minors, real orthogonal total scattering and output charge events in Majorana interferometers. In particular a neutral electron–hole coincidence can be invisible to a net-charge counter. Neither the incoming pair nor counting amplitudes are new here. The result here is a three-normal-basis linear statistic for the determinant of a **subblock** that may be nonunitary, and the two-basis and phase-blind identifiability separations.
- [Haim, Berg, von Oppen and Oreg, *Current correlations in a Majorana beam splitter*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1509.00463) (2015) derives beam-splitter charge correlations in isolated-mode settings. [Grabsch, Cheipesh and Beenakker, *Pfaffian formula for fermion parity fluctuations in a superconductor and application to Majorana fusion detection*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1903.11498) (2019) uses an open-subsystem parity Pfaffian. Neither the probability determinant calculus nor the connection between scattering and parity is itself claimed novel. The measured count witness here is not a fusion or island-parity experiment. [Danon et al.](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.124.036801) extract BCS-charge information via nonlocal conductance relations, distinct from an exact recovery of \(\det r\) for this two-mode setup.
- [Marciani, Schomerus and Beenakker, *Effect of a tunnel barrier on the scattering from a Majorana bound state in an Andreev billiard*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.05995) (2015/2016) anchors the class-D finite quadratic scattering realization and the zero-energy real orthogonal basis.

- [Spengler, Huber, Brierley, Adaktylos and Hiesmayr, *Entanglement detection via mutually unbiased bases*](https://arxiv.org/abs/1202.5058v2) (2012): sums of mutually unbiased basis coincidences as probes of correlation are established. The Pauli coincidence identity used here is elementary and not the novelty; the connection to the class-D reflection minor and the exact physical data separations are the specific results.

## Current interpretation and feasibility

- [Alex Krasnok, *Reflection Zeros Set the Scattering Invariant of Finite Majorana Wires*](https://arxiv.org/html/2611.17001v1) (November 2026 preprint): near-zero reflection zeros and contact-dependence distinguish the finite-device sign from long-wire topology. Its zero-bias conductance sign threshold concerns the stated isolated low-energy pair/narrow contact symmetry regime, not a blanket arbitrary multimode inference. We do not use its pole-counting theorem as a proof of the count identity.
- [Pikulin et al., *Protocol to identify a topological superconducting phase in a three-terminal device*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2103.12217) (2021), [Aghaee et al. (2023)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevB.107.245423), [Legg's 2026 Matters Arising](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10567-8) and [Aghaee, Alcaraz Ramirez and Zilke's reply](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-026-10568-7) concern transport-gap certificates or their interpretation. [*20 Second Parity Lifetime in an InAs–Pb Tetron Device*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.03884v2) reports a separate 2026 parity experiment. These results establish context only: no such dataset or material platform has been tested or reclassified here.
- [Bocquillon et al., *Coherence and indistinguishability of single electrons emitted by independent sources*](https://doi.org/10.1126/science.1232572) (2013), and [Ubbelohde et al., *Partitioning of on-demand electron pairs*](https://doi.org/10.1038/nnano.2014.275) (2015) experimentally demonstrate related electron optical ingredients. [Bisognin et al., *Quantum tomography of electrical currents*](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41467-019-11369-5) (2019) gives an independent electrically calibrated tomography direction. These precedents are not evidence that the two-mode/multi-energy source, nonperturbing conjugate output mixer and coincidence readout jointly meet this contract in a Majorana device.

The proposed contribution is narrow: a three-setting, non-postselected local phase-sensitive-in-orbital-space count estimator for a two-mode **finite reflection block**, together with a matching impossibility for arbitrary two fixed normal output bases under this count protocol and a physically admissible full two-lead |S|² counterexample. No bulk diagnostic, device-engineering implementation, newly claimed topological phase, or comprehensive priority claim follows from this note.
