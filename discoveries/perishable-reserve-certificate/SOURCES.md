## Operations prior
- [Liu, Adan & Perry, Two perishable inventory systems with one-way substitution](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10479-016-2128-1) ([author-hosted paper](https://pure.tue.nl/ws/portalfiles/portal/17129905/AdanTwo2016.pdf)): age/time and nested substitution in a dynamic two-class stochastic queue; assumes independent Poisson input/demand streams and a specified substitution rule. Our static scope is narrower in chronology, and varies unknown same-token linkage rather than specifying stochastic dynamics.
- [Threshold-Based Allocation Policies for Inventory Management of Red Blood Cells](https://doi.org/10.1287/msom.2017.0650): exact structural performance in a stylized queue and a calibrated simulation for age–availability/outdate tradeoffs under a threshold rule. Age-sensitive stock is established.
- [Dillon et al., two-stage stochastic model](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0925527317300361), [multi-product multi-period stochastic model](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10729-022-09593-5), [stochastic substitution and transshipment](https://doi.org/10.1080/24725579.2024.2396848): use specific scenarios and costs or network decisions for shortage/expiry planning, not the reported two-margin partial-identification target.
- [HosseiniFard, Khatami, Abbasi & van Houtum 2026](https://doi.org/10.1177/10591478261426078): important contemporary problem, already explicitly models type and recorded age, historical demand and variability of incoming age with sample-average approximate optimization. Treat as the closest applied comparator, not a reproduction.

The distinct contribution is a reduction from unlinked reports to virtual matchability and a simultaneous service/protection frontier, with the sharp adverse scenario menu, not a new Fréchet bound or fixed-graph theorem. Bounded search did not establish priority or novelty against all mathematics literature.
