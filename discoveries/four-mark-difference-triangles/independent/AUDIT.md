## Definitions and both lower bounds

A normalized integer ruler here has four marks \(r_0=0<r_1<r_2<r_3\). All \(6L\) positive within-ruler differences across the \(L\) rulers must be distinct. The maximum span (scope) is at least \(6L\); equality is equivalent to these differences being the entire interval \([1,6L]\). Do not confuse the notation \((L,3)\) (four marks) with \((L,4)\) (five).

For a ruler with consecutive gaps \(x,y,z\), the six differences sum to \(3x+4y+3z=4(x+y+z)-(x+z)\). Thus \(4S\) is exactly the sum of all \(6L\) positive differences plus the sum of the \(2L\) outer gaps. Both multisets have distinct positive integers. Therefore
\[
S\geq \tfrac14\left(\tfrac{6L(6L+1)}2+\tfrac{2L(2L+1)}2\right)=5L^2+L.
\]
Equality holds if and only if both multisets are the respective initial intervals. In particular, the minimum-span certificates also automatically achieve minimum scope. The data were originally generated by exact-cover searches; the replay validates each witness directly. The size-19 and size-22 witnesses came from a separate search after the initial five-size release. Seeds 6, 10, and 15 are positive controls, and duplicating a ruler is a negative control.

## Product arithmetic

Let \(X,Y\) be two perfect four-mark families of sizes \(A,B\) and total spans \(S_X,S_Y\); set \(K=6B\), \(v=2K+1\). Form a new family that includes \(Y\), all \(vX\), and the twelve rulers of shape
\[
r_i=vx_i+y_{\pi(i)}-y_{\pi(0)},\qquad i=0,1,2,3
\]
for each pair \(x\in X,y\in Y\), where \(\pi\) runs through the affine permutations of \(\mathbb F_4\). Since \(x_0=0\), each new ruler is normalized. Since \(v>K\), the order of the marks is strict even when the \(y\)-coordinates decrease. The affine permutations act sharply 2-transitively: specify distinct inputs and distinct outputs, and the affine slope and intercept are uniquely determined.

For a fixed first-factor positive difference \(d\) with its unique ruler and mark pair, the mixed differences have the form \(vd+\delta\). The affine permutations produce each directed pair of second-factor marks exactly once per ruler, so \(\delta\) ranges once over \([-K,K]\setminus\{0\}\); \(vX\) supplies zero. The centered intervals \([vd-K,vd+K]\) are adjacent for successive \(d\), and the first follows the interval \([1,K]\) from \(Y\). This proves perfection and shows that the number of rulers is \(vA+B\). All endpoint corrections \(y_{\pi(3)}-y_{\pi(0)}\) sum to zero over the permutations. Summing the mixed, unmixed scaled, and second-factor spans gives \(S'=v^2S_X+S_Y\).

Iterating with a fixed seed \(Y\) as the **second** factor (starting from that seed at \(j=1\)) gives
\[
L_j=(v^j-1)/12,\qquad S_j=S_Y(v^{2j}-1)/(v^2-1).
\]
A sharp seed \(S_Y=5B^2+B\) yields exactly
\[
S_j=\Bigl(5+\tfrac1{6B+1}\Bigr)L_j^2+
\Bigl(1-\tfrac{B}{6B+1}\Bigr)L_j.
\]
For the larger seed \(B=22\), \(v=265\), and the first nonseed size is 5,852 with scope 35,112 and total span 171,491,892. The former \(B=21\) seed is still replayed at 5,334 rulers and total span 142,486,260. A separate \(B=18\) replay also verifies 3,924 rulers with scope 23,544 and total span 77,133,420. The family is perfect, not claimed to minimize total span at \(j>1\). As a control on factor order both mixed products have 3,490 rulers and exactly the same scope, but the two total spans differ, as the asymmetric recurrence predicts. An unrestricted larger *nonoptimal* seed could also improve the coefficient, since for any perfect seed the coefficient is \(6S_Y/[B(6B+1)]\); this is another reason not to say “best known” without a much broader survey.

## Primary source audit and exact scope of contribution

- [Shehadeh–Kschischang–Sukmadji–Kingsford, *Higher-Order Staircase Codes*, arXiv v5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2312.13415v5), §VI-C, (28)–(29), Propositions 7–8 and Appendix B; [journal DOI](https://doi.org/10.1109/TIT.2025.3544168). It proves the same lower bound, supplies exact equality witnesses for \(6\le L\le15\), conjectures equality apart from \(L=2,3,4,5\), and uses its largest exhibited sharp seed \(B=15\) to obtain \(5+1/91\). The product is already Theorem 5 and Proposition 6 of the paper, crediting [Wild 1986](https://doi.org/10.1112/blms/18.2.127) and Kotzig–Turgeon. Neither the product nor the lower bound is a contribution here. No implication for any size not checked, and no comparison of the two recursive constructions at the same \(L\).
- [Liu–Feng–Wang–Zhang, *Perfect difference families, perfect systems of difference sets and their applications*](https://arxiv.org/html/2510.20446), Theorem 6.4 and §7; [journal DOI](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jcta.2026.106199), JCTA 223 (October 2026), 106199. They already prove minimum **scope** for all positive \(L\) except 2,3. Their conclusion separately asks the exact \(5L^2+L\) **total-span** question. The already specified scope-optimal examples for \(L=16,18\), formed by Lemma 1.2 with Appendix A \(m=15\) and Lemma 4.2 \(t=2\), respectively, were transcribed privately and independently evaluated: all positive differences are consecutive, but totals are 1333 and 1676, exceeding the lower bound by 37 and 38. Lemma 3.3's (108,4,1)-PLDF is nine ordered 4-multisets of *weighted layered differences* (some repeated marks inside blocks), not a perfect 18-ruler DTS. Prior perfect-scope data in [Ge–Miao–Sun 2010](https://doi.org/10.1002/jcd.20259) are also well documented; their publisher supplement and Shearer's archived online table did not load here, so do not claim exhaustive priority over their enumerated blocks or over unknown records.
- [Shehadeh–Kingsford–Kschischang, *New Difference Triangle Sets by an FPGA-Based Search Technique*](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2502.19517v2), and [Orden's 2026 essay and linked artifacts](https://cyborgmathematics.com/writing/difference-triangle-sets-from-zero) concern five-mark scopes in the relevant tables and are not competing four-mark / minimum-total-span data. Orden explicitly searches among five-mark sets with a small total span as a heuristic, but reports optimality of maximum scope, not a total-span result.

**Recommendation.** A short, precisely scoped construction note is defensible: exhibit certified equality at the stated sizes and a smaller explicit coefficient than HOSC Prop. 8. Do not assert original optimal scopes, a new product theorem, first worldwide instances, a global asymptotic record, statistical/hardware impact, or solution of the full simultaneous-optimality conjecture.
