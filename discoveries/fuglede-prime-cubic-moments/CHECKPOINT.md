# First exact gates for Fuglede in F_17^3

Scoped research note, 26 November 2026. No new full field or cardinality exclusion. The constructive shape of the finite calculation is based on Ferguson–Iosevich §§8–9 and adapted to p=17; no priority claim. The separate sharp cubic lemma is uniform in the prime.

## Known ledger

For a spectral pair (E,A), write D(E) for projective directions of differences and U(E) for projective normals whose p parallel layers all have equal size. Character orthogonality and the rational minimal polynomial Phi_p give D(E) subset U(A) and vice versa. See [Malikiosis, 2024, §2](https://doi.org/10.1007/s43670-023-00078-2) and [Ferguson–Iosevich, arXiv:2610.30712v1, §§8–13](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.30712v1), the latter establishing p=11, not p=17. The checked Research1 F13 project combines separate exact notebooks of sizes 26,39,52,78,104,117 with these inherited facts and arithmetic.

The only possible non-tile spectral sizes in dimension three are p*m for 2<=m<p. A spectral set of size 1,p,p²,p³ tiles, and every tile is spectral. The line and plane caps of a nontrivial pair are min(m,p−m) and p, respectively (F–I Lemma 8.2 / Fallon–Mayeli–Villano). In p17, [Malikiosis Theorem 1.4](https://doi.org/10.1007/s43670-023-00078-2) excludes m=14,15,16: the exact test is 17*(17−m)^2<256. Its conditional Eq. (6.1) at p17 is about 184.15 points; any hypothetical m12 pair must have each member in 12 full parallel layers, but those layers are not excluded here. Division here means projective points, not the punctured vector-space cardinality.

The general existing Butson determinant criterion provides the arithmetic part, not a new theorem. If q is a prime different from p and a power of q is −1 modulo p, q is unramified in Q(zeta_p) and complex conjugation fixes each prime ideal above q. For a character matrix H of order N=p*m, det(H) times its complex conjugate equals N^N. The valuation on the left is even, so N*v_q(m) must be even. Consequently for p=17, whose nontrivial residue classes have even order a power of two, odd m must be a square. This excludes m=3,5,7,11,13,15; it excludes **no even m**. See [Armario et al., §2.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2412.16579) for the general standard obstruction and [arXiv:1407.6548](https://arxiv.org/abs/1407.6548) for the Brock perspective. The same odd-only square condition holds for Fermat primes for m<p; it is not an extra p17-specific obstruction.

After known general/arithmetical exclusions the unclosed m are exactly **2,4,6,8,9,10,12**, sizes **34,68,102,136,153,170,204**. "Unclosed" means not removed by the checked evidence, not a claim that no source has ever studied the case. The centered planar-four-special classifications of [Wang, arXiv:2609.20495v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2609.20495v2) allow at p17 only sizes 136,153 (Table 3 and complements); his result is for Boolean shadows only. [Ghidelli 2020](https://arxiv.org/abs/1903.03881) implies a nonlayered Boolean shadow of size 68 needs at least five special directions. Neither directly excludes p17 m4. [Ghidelli–Kiss–Somlai, arXiv:2609.21779](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.21779) also distinguishes multisets from Boolean shadows; no Boolean classification is silently invoked on a general projection below.

## 34-point theorem, precise scope

For both members of any hypothetical size-34 spectral pair, every affine plane contains at most 7 points, and every projective line contains at most 7 uniform normals. The line cap is two. In particular no 34-pair with a larger slice or larger uniform pencil is possible. The low-pencil branch remains open.

### Prior parametrized reduction and general no-two-layer argument

In any 2p spectral pair of odd prime dimension-three field, neither member can be contained in two parallel planes. This is the proof of [F–I Lemma 9.1](https://arxiv.org/html/2610.30712v1#S9), with p replacing 11, not claimed original. For if E is layered, each slice has p points and is a cap. Counting pairs shows at least p tangent directions per slice, since each direction gives at most (p−1)/2 pairs. D(E) contains no full projective line: a full line in U(A) would force mp+pa=(p+1)m for a line occupancy a via the pencil identity, impossible. Therefore both slices use the same p directions, each making (p−1)/2 pairs and one singleton. For any such direction d a uniform normal perpendicular to d must put the two slice singletons in the same plane, since the p−1 pairs occupy full two-point bins. Their difference is transverse to d, leaving at most one uniform normal on each of the p projective lines d^perp. The remaining pencil line has at most p uniform normals. Thus |D(A)|<=|U(E)|<=2p. By the known parallel-plane criterion (F–I Lemma 8.3), the p uniform normals in the tangent projective line of A force A itself into two layers. They give p in-plane directions and >p transverse directions: otherwise their two p-point projected sets P,Q satisfy |Q−P|=p, all Q−x for x in P coincide, and invariance under a nonzero period makes Q a full affine line. This contradicts the cap bound. 

For a pencil with K>=8 uniform normals, project along its annihilator. Write f(x,y) for multiplicities (not assumed Boolean); make one uniform normal vertical and label the L=18−K actual nonuniform normals by slopes s_i in F_17. Their integer occupancy functions g_i(t) have mass 34, are between zero and the current plane cap, and obey the following Radon identity and coefficient conditions (generalization of F–I Lemma 8.4):
    
    sum_i g_i(y−s_i x) = 2(L−1)+17 f(x,y).
    w_i = 1 / product_{h != i}(s_i−s_h).
    c_(i,j)/w_i = R_j(s_i), degree R_j <= L−j−2, for j>=1.

The identity counts every other quotient point once and the base point 18 times, then drops K uniform fibres of mass two. Reduce it modulo 17, represent profiles by degree <=16, and match homogeneous coefficients: sum_i c_(i,j)*s_i^h=0 for every 0<=h<=j. The Lagrange/Vandermonde nullspace yields the displayed degree bound. In particular all bad profiles have degree <=L−2, and the constants sum to 2(L−1) modulo 17. Translation of (x,y) shifts each argument by h+k*s_i and preserves all constraints. Common rescaling of x,y multiplies the coefficient of degree j by u^j, preserving slopes, admissible integer profile multisets and plane cap. In characteristic 17 a polynomial of nonzero leading degree d<=8 has a unique centered translate (coefficient of d−1 zero).

Given a plane with n>=3 points and no three collinear, its direction set has size at least n: otherwise it must be exactly n−1 and would be a hyperfocused arc, impossible over odd q. [Giulietti and Montanucci, On hyperfocused arcs, §1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/math/0601488) cites the classical result. An elementary argument uses an affine triangle (0,0),(1,0),(0,1) and the other points (x,y). The product per point of y/x, (x+y−1)/y, and x/(x+y−1) equals 1. In the n−1-slopes equality case each triangle vertex sees each direction once. These factors in local slopes s are s, (s+1)/s, and 1/(s+1), respectively. For slopes outside {0,−1,infinity} they cancel in the product over points. The extra terms for slopes −1 from the first vertex, infinity from the second, and 0 from the third multiply to −1, a contradiction. Thus a bound K_max on pencils of U transfers to the partner's plane cap, with no realization gap.

### Exact gates K>=9

Degree d=1..6 minimum sum of least nonnegative residues is 136,102,85,51,51,34 respectively. To obtain this exhaustive table, for each leading coefficient and zero next coefficient enumerate all intermediate coefficients; evaluate the 17 residues initially with constant zero, and add each possible constant via a cyclic histogram update. At d=6 exactly 16 centered polynomials attain 34, unique by leading coefficient, all even. Constant nonuniform profiles (in this initial plane cap 17) must have residue zero and occupancy 17 on two levels, already excluded by the prior no-two-layer argument; constant residue 2 gives uniformity. Thus K>=11 is impossible. If K=10 (L=8), all eight profiles are sextics, c6/w is common nonzero lambda, and c5/w is affine. Translate x,y to kill c5 because the same affine shifts apply to the ratio c5/c6. Check all 24,310 eight-slope masks and 16 nonzero lambdas: 1,360 pass the constant and none satisfy the quartic component. Thus K<=9 and plane cap<=9 for both.

For K=9 (L=9), enumerate the centered monic septics exhaustively (17^5 intermediate coefficient shapes, all constants); any leading is attained by scaling since gcd(7,16)=1. Of the five mass-34 profiles four obey the raw cap nine; their largest values are 4,6,6,8. All 16 sextics meet cap nine. Normalize two distinct bad slopes to 0,1 (affine slope map), leaving 6,435 masks. If c7 is present, c7/w is a nonzero constant, normalized to 1 by common scaling; c6/w is affine and translation kills it simultaneously. Three supplied values of c5/w determine the quadratic and all other values, allowing every compatible profile at a repeated coefficient. Of 411,840 first-three trials, 18,909 full tuples match c5 and 81 pass constant 16; none pass degree four. If no c7, all profiles are sextic, c6/w is affine nonzero at every bad slope. Normalize the first lead to 1 or 3 (sixth powers are the squares; 3 is nonsquare), and choose a translation killing c5 at slopes 0,1 so c5/w equals F*s*(s−1) for one F in the field. Each prescribed c5 determines the shift of its unique centered sextic via division by six times its leading coefficient. Among 1,969,110 trials, 112,842 pass the constants then none the quartic component. Thus K<=8 and plane cap<=8.
    
### Exact K=8 gate

L=10. Enumerate all 6,435 masks of 10 slopes containing 0,1 and excluding infinity, then form full orbits on this normalized domain by each projective map taking an ordered pair of bad slopes to 0,1 and one good slope to infinity. Every allowable image preserving normalization arises this way; projective transforms of the pencil are induced by quotient coordinate changes preserving mass and plane cap. Exactly 17 representative masks suffice. This orbit compression retains hypotheses and does not identify integer profile tuples without the matching argument scale.

Cap-eight admissible centered octic profiles use g(t)=E(t²)+t*O(t²), with E quartic and O quadratic. Given the odd part, for each t=1..8 the signed difference g(t)−g(−t) is its unique congruent number in [−8,8]. Write its absolute value d_t. The even midpoint E(t²) must be d_t/2+k modulo 17 with integer 0<=k<=8−d_t, and the pair mass is d_t+2k. Choose the four positions with largest d_t; those E values plus the leading coefficient determine the rest by cubic interpolation after removing the known leading term. Any omitted position is checked directly. The E(0) mass and bound and complete mass are also checked, and every emitted polynomial is evaluated independently from power sums. Eighth powers are ±1, so letting the leading run through 1..8 covers the classes up to input rescaling; new profiles at the negative leads are obtained by scaling by 3. Of 4,913 O choices, 2,625 have minimum pair mass <=34, yielding 836,264 bounded four-pair assignments and 284 valid centered profiles on the eight lead representatives, covering the other sign as stated. The census by representative lead is [1,85,6,78,12,52,16,34].

If c8 is nonzero, it is nonzero on all slopes, with c8/w constant normalized to one of 1..8; c7/w is affine and common translation kills c7. Then c6/w is quadratic and c5/w is cubic. Pick any three slots, using the three with the smallest catalogue counts for speed, interpolate the former, and pick a fourth to interpolate the latter. Allow every match of both coefficients in all other slots. At 17 masks times eight lead scalars, 716,837 first-three assignments give 596,010 all-slot degree-six matches, 2,580,022 assigned fourth profiles, 33,402 all-slot degree-five matches, and 793,853 full tuples. Exactly 5,652 pass constant 1, none pass degree four.

If highest degree is seven, c7/w is affine and nonzero at all but at most one bad slope. Normalize its first nonzero actual c7 to 1, using a common scale. Center c6 at the first two septic slots (possible by translation, because their leading terms do not vanish). Then c6/w is a scalar times the monic quadratic with roots the two selected slopes. Each septic shift is determined; a single sextic slot, if present, has this c6 and arbitrary shift. Interpolate c5/w from the first four septic slots, with the other slots retaining all profiles matching. Of 1,288,192 first-four placements, 1,616 satisfy degree five, 115 the constant 1, and zero degree four.

If highest degree is six, every bad slope is sextic, and c6/w is quadratic. Normalize the first lead to 1 or 3, and translate to kill c5 at 0,1. Thus c5/w is s(s−1)*(u*s+v), with u,v arbitrary. Use the unique shifted sextics as in K9. Of 1,690,072 placements, 96,335 pass the constants 1 and none satisfy degree four. These are all cases because the low-residue table excludes nonconstant degree <=5 and the cap excludes constant bad profiles. Therefore K<=7 and both plane caps <=7.

## Remaining

All seven numerical cardinalities remain. For 34 the most narrowly defined next gate is K=7 with cap seven and profile degree at most nine. To make its univariate half manageable, write centered monic g(t)=E(t²)+t*O(t²) where E has degree <=3 and O is monic quartic; gcd(9,16)=1 covers all leads. Applying the same signed ±t pair mass and selecting four restrictive squares computes E exactly. But a complete K7 conclusion must still couple all L=11 slope profiles, including the degree-six through degree-eight cases, and then resolve K<=6 if K=7 is impossible. None of those stages are claimed here. A more structural follow-up is to combine universal cubic moments with additive geometry or stronger moment/bilinear rank; in the 34 branch every projective line has at least 11 nonuniform normals and probability-free profile bounds, but M<=3 does not make the incidence weights integral. F–I's tight weighted divisibility criterion cannot be blindly applied with C=15.
