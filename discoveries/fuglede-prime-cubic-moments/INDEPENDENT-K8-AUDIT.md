# Independent 34-point K8 and cubic audits

## Exact K8 exclusion

After the K9 gate in INDEPENDENT-34-AUDIT.md, both affine-plane caps and both maximal uniform-pencil sizes are at most 8. If some pencil attains \(K=8\), there are \(L=10\) nonuniform finite slope labels and degree at most 8, with \(c_{ij}/w_i\) of slope degree at most \(8-j\). The profiles all have mass 34, actual integer entries in \([0,8]\), and are nonconstant as polynomials; the same residue check excludes degrees below 6. Here is a separate exact replay; nothing relies on any 34-point coordinate fixtures.

**Centered octic catalogue.** Write \(P(t)=E(t^2)+t O(t^2)\) where \(E\) has degree 4 with nonzero leading coefficient and \(\deg O\le2\), since the degree-7 term was centered away. For each odd polynomial \(O\), each pair of inputs \(t=\pm j,\ 1\le j\le8\), needs an integer pair \(u,v\in [0,8]\) with \(u-v\equiv2jO(j^2)\). The direct list of such pairs provides possible \(E(j^2)\equiv(u+v)/2\) and the minimum pair mass. There are 2625 odd polynomials with total minimum at most 34. Fix the true leading coefficient \(a=1,\dots,16\). For each of three selected opposite-input pairs (smallest individual lift lists) and \(P(0)\), run over all possible integer values, respecting lower bounds of the five other pairs. These four values, after subtracting \(a(j^2)^4\), determine the remaining cubic even part by interpolation. Test the remaining pairs and exact mass. This independent generation gives 568 centered octics, 284 modulo exchanging the sign of the leading coefficient by common argument scaling. It evaluates at zero plus three other nodes, unlike the primary author's four-other-node census. Lower-degree profiles are generated independently by the same histogram algorithm as the previous gates.

**Masks.** An unlabeled bad mask is a ten-point subset of the projective pencil. Generate projective direction permutations by \(s\mapsto s+1,\ s\mapsto3s,\ s\mapsto1/s\), extended to infinity; they generate every projective linear map because the first two generate all affine maps and a nonaffine fractional linear map is an affine map followed by inversion and another affine map. Orbit queue closure on all \(\binom{18}{10}=43758\) masks gives 17 orbits. Choose each orbit's lowest mask with \(\infty\) uniform and 0,1 bad, which is always possible by three-transitivity. The sum of these finite within-orbit alternatives is 6435. All coefficient degree conditions transform covariantly under projective reparametrization, as justified by the determinant weight argument in INDEPENDENT-34-AUDIT.md. On the finite chart this change corresponds simply to one invertible linear transformation of the quotient coordinates with the needed rescaling of profile arguments.

**One octic.** Then all ten are octic because \(c_{i8}/w_i=\lambda\ne0\). A common dilation normalizes \(\lambda\) modulo eighth powers, to one of \(1,\dots,8\) as the eighth powers are \(\pm1\). A common translation kills every \(c_{i7}/w_i\) because they are affine and change by \(8\lambda\) times a common affine function. Bucket centered catalogues by \(c_6/w_i\): for each quadratic, its values on any three distinct slopes determine all the others. Within those compatible buckets similarly interpolate cubic \(c_5/w_i\) from four labels, and enumerate all remaining options with multiplicity. This yields 793853 assignments, of which 5652 satisfy the necessary constant congruence \(\sum_i c_{i0}\equiv18\equiv1\). Zero satisfy all degree-4 coefficient identities.

**A septic and no octic.** Write \(c_{i7}/w_i=b+a s_i\) nonzero as a polynomial, and choose its projective scaling (all nonzero scalars are seventh powers); the independent code uses \((b,a)=(1,0)\) or \((b,1)\). There is at most one sextic label, the sole possible root. Translation changes \(c_{i6}/w_i\) by \(7(b+a s_i)\) times an affine function, so it can kill that ratio at the first two septic labels \(s_0,s_1\), and the remaining quadratic is \(F(s-s_0)(s-s_1)\). If the sextic label exists its prescribed degree-6 coefficient is nonzero. For each septic label, the ratio prescribes translation of the centered septic, so each option's \(c_5/w_i\) is known. At the optional sextic label, any degree-5 value prescribes the translation of its unique centered sextic. Interpolate the cubic \(c_5/w_i\) at four actual septic labels and filter all remaining labels, retaining all multiplicities. Results: 1616 assignments, 110 with the constant congruence, zero after quartics.

**All sextic.** The leading \(c_{i6}/w_i\) is quadratic with no root among labels. Fix first actual leading coefficient to 1 or 3 by common argument scaling, since sixth powers are squares. The other two quadratic degrees of freedom are exhaustively tried. Translate to kill \(c_{i5}/w_i\) at the first two labels, since its change is \(6(c_{i6}/w_i)\) times an affine function and both leading coefficients are nonzero. The remaining cubic is \((s-s_0)(s-s_1)(A s+B)\), tried for all \(A,B\). Each ratio prescribes the shift of the unique centered sextic with that lead. Results: 1690072 assignments, 97361 with constant congruence, zero after quartics.

These are independent gauges; counts of a constant-only prefilter on not-yet-compatible tuples can change under a change of projective representatives followed by a different centering of two labels. Such a prefilter count is not an invariant; all three terminal counts agree at zero. It follows \(K\le7\) for both members and the affine-plane cap is 7, since eight planar arc points determine at least eight directions. Line cap remains 2. These are necessary conditions, not nonexistence of 34-point spectral pairs. ## Cubic difference bound (independent algebra audit)

Let \(E\) affinely span \(K^3\) in characteristic greater than 3, have affine-plane cap \(c\), and have affine-line cap \(\ell\). Suppose nonzero homogeneous cubic \(F\) vanishes on \(E-E\). Then
\[
|E|\le \max\{4,c+1,\min(\lfloor3c/2\rfloor,3\ell)\}.
\]
Without a supplied line cap omit \(3\ell\).

Translate \(0\in E\), and polarize \(F(x)=T(x,x,x)\) with \(T\) symmetric. For \(x,y\in E\), \(F(x-y)=F(x)=F(y)=0\) gives \(T(x,x,y)=T(y,y,x)\). Choose a basis \(b_i\in E\); \(B(b_i,b_j)=T(b_i,b_i,b_j)\) defines a symmetric bilinear form. On each \(x\in E\), \(T(x,x,b_i)=T(b_i,b_i,x)=B(x,b_i)\) gives \(T(x,x,\cdot)=B(x,\cdot)\), and \(B(x,x)=F(x)=0\). Therefore every \((x:1)\) is a singular point of the nonzero projective cubic
\[
 G(X,w)=F(X)-\frac32wB(X,X).
\]
The three spatial derivatives cancel by the last identity for \(T\); the derivative in \(w\) vanishes by \(B(x,x)=0\).

Use four affine-independent points of \(E\) as projective coordinate axes. Singularity at each axis kills its cube and all squared-coordinate terms, giving \(G=\sum_i a_i\prod_{j\ne i}X_j\). If all four coefficients are nonzero, support size two forces an outside derivative nonzero and support size three an inside derivative nonzero. At a point of full support, Euler's identity gives \(G=0\); writing \(G=(\prod_j X_j)\sum_i a_i/X_i\), its four derivatives would then force every \(a_i=0\). Thus there are only the four axes. With exactly three coefficients nonzero, the cubic is \(w(Axy+Bxz+Cyz)\) with \(ABC\ne0\); the ternary quadratic is nondegenerate (its symmetric matrix determinant is \(ABC/4\)). The singular set is its conic in the plane \(w=0\) plus one vertex. Even if some projective points are at infinity in the original affine chart, this gives at most \(c+1\) affine points of \(E\).

With one or two coefficients nonzero, the cubic is a product of three independent linear forms. Its singular set consists of the three pairwise-intersection projective lines, all concurrent. Each point belongs to at least two of the three planes, so \(2|E|\le3c\) in the original affine chart (an original infinity plane contributes no affine points). Each of the three lines contributes at most \(\ell\), and at most \(3\ell-2\) in total if the concurrency point is in \(E\). These statements require no assumption about the projective chart containing every singular point. For \(\ell=2,c\ge5\) the bound simplifies to \(c+1\); \(c=4,\ell=2\) permits six, attained by two points on each of three parallel affine lines with noncollinear quotient points. The differences then have quotient projections in the three side directions of a triangle, so the product of their three annihilating linear forms is a valid cubic, and plane cap is four.

As a sharpness check without a line cap, choose equal-size truncated coordinate axes, including their common origin for odd \(c\) and omitting it for even \(c\), if the field has enough nonzero coordinates. The cubic \(xyz\) vanishes on every difference and the plane cap is \(c\), attaining \(\lfloor3c/2\rfloor\) for \(c\ge3\). This check is algebraic; it does not suggest existence of a spectral pair.

Cross-reference check: feeding the primary author's lex-first **slope-list** representatives into this independent checker (instead of its least **integer-mask** representatives) gives the author's exact three constant counts \(5652,115,96335\) and the same three zero terminal counts. Thus the prefilter differences are explained by the different masks/centering gauges. Both original orbit programs used 17 representatives.

In the size-34 application each member affinely spans \(\mathbb F_{17}^3\) by its plane cap. For each \(j=1,2,3\), the homogeneous moment polynomial \(M_j^E(v)=\sum_{x\in E}(v\cdot x)^j\) vanishes at all directions in \(D(A)\) by uniformity of the normal and \(\sum_{t\in\mathbb F_{17}}t^j=0\); if nonzero, multiplying by a power of any nonzero linear form gives a nonzero cubic vanishing on \(A-A\). The affine cap bound for \(A\), with \(c=7,\ell=2\), gives at most eight points, contradicting 34. The symmetric deduction shows all moments through degree three vanish identically for both members.
