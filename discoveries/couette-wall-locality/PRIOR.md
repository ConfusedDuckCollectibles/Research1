# Additional primary priority checks

- The reflection/echo paper is by **Herberthson, Özarslan, Knutsson, and Westin**, [JCP 146, 124201 (2017), doi:10.1063/1.4978621](https://doi.org/10.1063/1.4978621). Section III discusses gradient reversal and Section IV discusses time-varying restricted propagators. The signal is an integrated fixed-preparation magnetization, not the operator norm over arbitrary inputs.
- [Cai, Williamson, Basser, JMR 390, 108118 (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jmr.2026.108118) demonstrate localization-driven signal contrast in a single reflecting interval using constant-gradient spin-echo waveforms, matrix simulations and fixed signal preparation/readout. Spatial wall-localized survival as a physical phenomenon is not new. This study does not give the finite-width (L^2)-operator-norm comparison, exact essential spectrum, or arbitrary-history sharpness proved here.
- [Liu, Masmoudi, Zhao, arXiv:2605.21663v1 §2.1 (2026)](https://arxiv.org/html/2605.21663v1#S2.SS1) explicitly give the constant-shear Dirichlet half-line Fourier-mode Airy eigensolution in the auxiliary equation for nonlinear Navier–Stokes with Navier slip. Our Airy calculation is a known control. Their aim is long-time self-similar vorticity with a moment, not finite-width operator-norm comparison at fixed elapsed scaled time.
- [He, Niu, Zhao, arXiv:2607.17468 (2026)](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.17468) treat enhanced dissipation and perturbative stability for the full Couette flow with Robin velocity/friction boundary conditions; not the present passive scalar, independent absorbing/reflecting scalar walls and temporal affine-shear control.
- [Yolcu et al., PRE 93, 052602 (2016)](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevE.93.052602) and [Özarslan et al., arbitrary waveform path integral](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4864615/) discuss exact or calculable diffusion attenuation for varying gradient histories. The Fourier square-completion is an elementary baseline, not a novelty claim.

Searches combined “complex Airy,” “Bloch–Torrey,” “Couette,” “finite section,” “essential norm,” “operator norm,” “singular values,” “large interval,” and “half-line” across primary sources. No reviewed result supplied the same explicit amplitude-independent finite-width (L^2) norm comparison with the sharp uniform (pi^2) coefficient, but this is a scoped search rather than a definitive first-priority claim.

The closest locality precursor found in the audit is [Grebenkov, JMR 248, 164–176 (2014), Eq. (13)](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jmr.2014.08.016): the constant-gradient pointwise propagator is expected to recover the bulk law far from a barrier, and §3.7 discusses when well-separated barriers interact. This directly prefigures the spatial intuition. It does not state an amplitude-independent sharp (L^2) finite-width bound or the exact essential spectrum for general time histories. [van den Berg, *Heat equation and the principle of not feeling the boundary* (1989)](https://people.maths.bris.ac.uk/~mamvdb/pdfs/Publication27.pdf) is an older tool-level precedent for Gaussian locality.
