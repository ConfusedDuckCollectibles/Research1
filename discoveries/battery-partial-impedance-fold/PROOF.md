# Exact nonlocal ambiguity with impedance at one charge point

### 1. Declaration of observations and physics

Use a monotone local equilibrium two-electrode charge-balance model at fixed temperature, with no hysteresis or continued aging. q is known transferred charge **relative to the observed center**, not an age-rescaled percentage state of charge or measured full endpoint. Let the unknown strictly positive electrode capacities be Qp,Qn, b=1/Qp and c=1/Qn, the unknown center lithiation fractions ap,an be in (0,1), and xp(q)=ap+bq, xn(q)=an−cq throughout the partial interval. Normalized units are for the mathematical model. Effective charge-equivalent inventory is L=Qp ap+Qn an, which is conserved versus q. Relative to a common known initial (Qp0,Qn0,L0), LAMi=1−Qi/Qi0 and LLI=1−L/L0; L is active-domain model inventory, not a direct assay of lithium or its microscopic loss mechanism.

Assume known absolute stable OCP functions on the relevant lithiation intervals:
```
U_p(x)=A_p−gamma_p x+(alpha/2)x^2,
U_n(x)=A_n−gamma_n x−(beta/2)x^2, alpha,beta>0.
```
Require both U_i'(x) strictly negative there; the exact benchmark below makes them so on all [0,1]. Let total equilibrium voltage V=U_p(xp)−U_n(xn), and observe the entire V(q) on a nontrivial partial interval about q=0. Its differential voltage, or incremental capacity where invertible, is the same data, not an independent sensor. Define positive labeled slope magnitudes g_p(q)=−b U_p'(xp), g_n(q)=−c U_n'(xn), so −V'=g_p+g_n. Give the observer the **ideal separated, labeled** g_p(0)=u, g_n(0)=v, u,v>0. This is more informative than ordinary uncalibrated terminal EIS.

As a concrete admissible linear-diffusion closure, use a homogeneous spherical particle for each electrode with known radius/diffusivity ratio tau=R^2/D (per-particle diffusion time, not number of particles), an independently calibrated lumped series/interface/electrolyte residual Z_b, and known labels for two diffusion kernels Gp,Gn. The model conserves mean concentration; solving the radial Laplace equation with uniform boundary flux gives G(s;tau)=(1/s)[w² sinh w/(3(w cosh w−sinh w))], w²=s tau. This follows because the radial solution is proportional to sinh(w r/R)/r and the conservation relation fixes mean concentration as transferred-charge phasor divided by Q_i; the surface/mean ratio is the bracket. Its low-frequency expansion is G=1/s+tau/15+O(s), so distinct known tau give nonidentical kernels, though may be numerically close and need not resolve at a chosen frequency. The negative voltage response to positive discharge current has impedance
```
Z(s,q) = Z_b(s,q) + g_p(q) Gp(s) + g_n(q) Gn(s).
```
With Z_b calibrated, voltage slope known, and Gp≠Gn at a frequency, subtract Z_b and (u+v)Gn to read u from a calibrated complex residual divided by Gp−Gn. With a second bias the same logic applies. If Z_b is unknown but constant across q, it cancels in the two-bias contrast, and the measured change in total voltage slope removes the Gn contribution. This recovers the change of the labeled split. It does not recover the absolute center weights or all degradation states without independent calibration of the center split. The ideal witness permits, e.g., unchanged individual particle time constants while active particle counts change, and equal/known non-diffusive residuals. Identical entire linear spectra then follow from identical weights, **even with distinct labeled kernels**. Unknown q- and aging-dependent kinetics, charge-transfer areas, diffusion, electrode/electrolyte processes, or conditional correlations with chemistry can invalidate this decomposition or provide additional genuinely independent information; a good fit or unlabeled pole assignment is not evidence those assumptions hold. Bizeray’s model supplies prior for the diffusion closure and already flags the role of known local OCP gradients/starting stoichiometries; here absolute OCP shapes but not starting electrode stoichiometries are given.

### 2. Complete saturated count and why local rank does not certify it

Let X=alpha b², Y=beta c², K=X+Y=V''(0)>0. Let Vstar=[A_p−gamma_p²/(2alpha)]−[A_n+gamma_n²/(2beta)], and D=2[V(0)−Vstar]. Completing each square and substituting the separated slopes gives
```
V(q)=V(0)−(u+v)q+Kq²/2,
D = u²/X + v²/(K−X),       0<X<K.                (1)
```
Conversely any such root gives b=sqrt(X/alpha), c=sqrt((K−X)/beta), ap=(gamma_p−u/b)/alpha, an=(v/c−gamma_n)/beta, and recovers every frozen observable exactly, provided state bounds and any pristine-baseline constraints are met. The right-hand side of (1) is strictly convex, diverges at both ends, and is minimized at X=Ku/(u+v) with Dmin=(u+v)²/K. There are zero, one, or two roots before domain pruning as D is below, at, or above Dmin. Its quadratic polynomial is D X²+(v²−u²−DK)X+u²K=0; above the fold the root separation is
```
sqrt([DK−(u+v)²][DK−(u−v)²])/D.
```
It is nonzero and locally regular on each branch. The mapping from states to (u,v,K,X) has a smooth explicitly invertible formula above on the positive admissible domain. The further map to (u,v,K,D) has determinant proportional to dD/dX, nonzero on each of the two roots. Thus a full-rank local Jacobian/Fisher check does not detect this two-sheet ambiguity. The ambiguity occupies an open set in the four quadratic data coordinates on which both roots meet the domain constraints strictly, not an open set of arbitrary entire voltage curves. At the fold, global equality of roots does not imply good conditioning.

### 3. Exact strict-interior witness

Take U_p=21/5−(8/25)x+x²/10, U_n=3/10−(3/25)x−x²/10. Their gradients are at most −3/25 on [0,1]. Use |q|≤1/20 and initial Qp0=Qn0=L0=5/4, achievable with initial center lithiation fractions both 1/2. Two candidate aged states:

- A: (ap,an,b,c)=(19/25,1/10,5/6,1). Qp=6/5, Qn=1 and L=253/250. LLI=19.04%, (LAMp,LAMn)=(4%,20%).
- B: (ap,an,b,c)=(9/10,6/25,1,5/6). Qp=1, Qn=6/5 and L=297/250. LLI=4.96%, (LAMp,LAMn)=(20%,4%).

Affine coordinates remain strictly between zero and one at both endpoints for both states. Q and L are each strictly positive and below the corresponding pristine values. Direct polynomial substitution gives the *same* V(q)=93189/25000−(7/25)q+(61/360)q², strictly decreasing throughout, and u=v=7/50. Roots X=5/36,1/5 have derivatives dD/dX=∓32879/62500, respectively, and gap 11/180. These are not an interchange of impedance pole labels, half-cell laws or implied materials: p and n laws, sign of curvature, and labeled time constants are held fixed. The 14.08 percentage-point inventory difference and 16-point exchange in both LAMs clear the locked gates. A rational oracle evaluates distinct passive RC residual functions with common conservation pole as a second model check, not an approximation justified for any named commercial cell.

### 4. Which extra observation changes the answer?

1. **One calibrated absolute same-cell electrode potential at the center** is sufficient even for any known strictly monotone differentiable half-cell laws with nonzero slopes when given u,v. Recover the other electrode OCP from V(0), invert the two known laws for ap,an, and read b,c from the local split and these gradients. In the quadratic witness, the positive reference prediction changes by 539/25000 V=21.56 mV at center, so with otherwise exact hypotheses and a single adversarial error ±sigma the candidate intervals are disjoint exactly for sigma<10.78 mV (touch means no guaranteed distinction). Unknown arbitrary reference offset cannot be treated as calibrated. A stable uncalibrated additive offset cancels in the two-point *relative* positive electrode voltage; its prediction gap is only |Delta X|q²/2: 11/144000 V at the toy boundary (hard tolerance half). This is a scalar data-design comparison, not actual accuracy or battery instructions.
2. **One independent calibrated slope/impedance split at any distinct known q** is sufficient for the quadratic class: g_p(q)=u−Xq, recover X uniquely and then all variables. Branch separation in g_p is exactly |Delta X||q|, and in one corrected frequency the corresponding complex spectral gap is |Delta X q||Gp−Gn|. Equal deterministic hard metric error balls are exactly disjoint when radius < half this gap. The abstract witness at q=1/20 gives gap 11/3600 V per normalized charge unit (hard tolerance 11/7200 for the split), versus precisely zero at center or at extra frequencies at center. Absolute vs relative reference and separated weight units are different, so do not compare their numbers as if using the same sensor. Real measurement/other-model nuisance uncertainty reduces this usable margin; if the uncertainty spans Gp−Gn or the additional sample has a sign-correlated calibration change, the proposed observation can be uninformative.
3. A further option outside linear readout is **nonlinear curvature-sensitive impedance**, already studied by Kirk et al. In the ideal no-other-nonlinearity closure, a complex current phasor I induces a second-harmonic voltage phasor I²[X Gp(iω)²+(K−X)Gn(iω)²]/4 from the two known quadratic OCPs. It can separate the central branches when squared kernels differ (unless unmodeled competing nonlinear terms confound it). This is a scope note about the linear no-go, not a priority claim or any prescription for exciting a real cell.

If the unknown shape is near but not exactly quadratic, the danger need not vanish at useful precision. Work with C³-known shapes near the witness on its two admissible coordinate ranges. The same exact central data (V(0),V'(0),V''(0),g_p(0)) have two feasible continuations by the inverse/implicit function theorem at the two nonsingular witnesses. Local nonsingularity and the strictly interior domains persist; the central g_n and thus linear Z are exactly equal in the declared closure. Let F(q)=V_A(q)−V_B(q). F(0)=F'(0)=F''(0)=0. Taylor with U3=sup|F'''| on the window yields sup|F|≤U3 w³/6 and sup|F'|≤U3 w²/2 for |q|≤w. The reciprocal differential capacity has the same O(w²) order where the slope is bounded away from zero; in overlapping voltage coordinates the charge difference adds only O(w³) when curvatures are bounded. By contrast, the g_p difference is O(w) with nonzero first derivative near the witness, hence Θ(w) at a chosen nonzero endpoint, and the same for a separated finite-frequency spectrum when the kernel difference is fixed nonzero. The cubic bound is sharp in order: perturb the known positive OCP by a nonzero sufficiently small lambda x³, and the negative OCP not at all. On the IFT-continued branches F'''=6 lambda(b_A³−b_B³) nonzero by continuity. Close-to-quadratic **full noiseless** partial voltage can then distinguish mathematically; the finite-window amplitude is third-order, while the additional split responds first-order. There is no universal claim about arbitrary known OCPs or all aging mechanisms.

The [independent audit](independent/AUDIT.md) supplies a separate exact nuisance counterexample: when the bias-independent additive impedance is not independently calibrated, two physically admissible effective states with different central splits and model inventories can agree on the entire voltage curve and all linear spectra at **every** bias, by compensating with different passive backgrounds. The backgrounds are constant across bias, not flat across frequency. This does not contradict the calibrated-center result.

### 5. Primary prior, native archive gate, and scope

- [Lin–Khoo, JPS 2024](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpowsour.2024.234446) derives the electrode differential-voltage fractions and Fisher/window sensitivity, with nontrivial electrodes and practical limiting regimes. [Birkl et al. 2017](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jpowsour.2016.12.011) empirically imposes losses and benchmarks half-cell OCV fitting. [Li et al., eTransportation 2026](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.etran.2026.100593) benchmarks multi-objective degradation-mode fitting against known LFP/graphite and aged LCO/graphite data; the [public primary data/code inventory](https://github.com/REIL-UConn/Multi-Objective-Optimization-for-Lithium-Ion-Battery-Degradation-Diagnostics) lists a multi-cell LFP_Data.xlsx of half-/full-cell measured Q–V and no paired EIS for the frozen contract. No new claim for conditioning in partial windows or for a generic DVA fit improvement.
- [Bizeray et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1702.02471) and its journal [primary full text](https://ora.ox.ac.uk/objects/uuid%3A7971ecb3-53fe-4303-b699-336c41df5647/files/md902596844c3c73ae0483725e12b1731), §§II–III, identify grouped linear SPM dynamics conditional on known starting stoichiometries, nonzero OCP gradients and ordered electrode parameters. [Kirk et al. 2023](https://doi.org/10.1149/1945-7111/acada7) and [author PDF](https://eprints.soton.ac.uk/495667/1/Kirk_2023_J._Electrochem._Soc._170_010514.pdf) derive first and second harmonics in a richer SPM, with electrode balancings externally set and additional dynamic kinetic-label symmetries. [Grandjean et al.](https://doi.org/10.3390/en10010090) discusses twofold **RC electrode/pole** assignments; that is not this ambiguity with known separate labels, strictly nonzero positive weights and the same full voltage across q. [Laue et al.](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10800-021-01579-5) and [Hallemans et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2412.10896) already use multichannel calibration and/or several SOCs to improve battery dynamic identification. [Lee et al.](https://doi.org/10.1149/1945-7111/ab8c83) addresses aging-induced changes in actual half-cell potential. Exact two physical preimages despite central absolute-shape calibration and labeled linear impedance, with a matched central-jet sharp window order and quantified extra-observable gap, was not located in bounded prior review.
- [Hofmann et al. 2026 native archive, DOI 10.57880/rdspace-ubt-54](https://rdspace.uni-bayreuth.de/entities/researchdata/0bb95cc2-8ce9-4dcb-b7c0-4eda5949f9ed) is valuable: CC BY-SA 4.0, Dataset.zip 8.29 GB. [Hofmann accompanying dissertation §2.3, Table 2, Fig. 5 and §3.1](https://epub.uni-bayreuth.de/id/eprint/8980/1/Hofmann%20%282026%29%20-%20PhD%20Thesis.pdf) documents 18 ~10-mAh laboratory NMC/graphite PAT cells, 6 aging paths ×3, 11 repeated checkups; technically 6297–6314, 6298 full EIS only and 6313/6314 no EIS, therefore 15 cells configured for both full & negative electrode EIS (not 15 verified complete all-checkup cases). See raw master/slave pOCV .txt and raw Gamry .dta by SN/checkup/T/direction/SOC; Fig. 5 example 6299, CU2_MOL, T25, EIS. Positive OCP is reconstructed as full V plus measured NE-reference potential; positive EIS generally full minus NE and share error/sign conventions. Preprocessed df_pOCV.csv, df_HPPC.csv, df_EIS.csv are derived outputs: pOCV-based LAM/LLI labels fit electrode potentials and pristine shapes, not independent chemistry measurements, and published fits use other measurements as part of context. Their separate paper compares reference-derived labels to a partial-voltage estimator already. The archive could support a withheld **raw** reference-potential test (frozen cell/checkup/temperature/direction/start charge calibration, no repeat-row pseudoreplication, no full-cell differences reused as independent “truth,” and track reference drift and instrument limits). It does not establish a model-independent inventory assay for these cells. Scope new holdout to matching endpoint from a different voltage stream or compare actual reference prospectively, not df_pOCV targets alone. No archive files were downloaded or fit, and native raw rows were not independently verified. Archive documentation is cited to distinguish a possible reference test from chemical validation. This is a measurement-design result, not a diagnosis of any cell or a device procedure.
