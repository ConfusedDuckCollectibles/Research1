# Dwarf galaxies: exact full-velocity aperture camouflage and a resolved-core contrast
An exact mathematical result in a spherical equilibrium model, separate from the observational literature screen.

## Frozen gate and independent archive outcome
Require genuinely independent native proper-motion epochs or a new held-out tracer set on the same dwarf, with known sky mask and both projected PM components plus compatible LOS/membership/shape controls. Primary physical target is **ρ∝r^−1 versus a resolved finite central core (ρ slope 0), not two profile fits at large radius**. A positive empirical test requires radial/azimuthal support within a declared central validity radius, independent field-level calibrations, 5% test and 80% power for the frozen contrast; do not average away depth, perspective, anisotropy covariance, or use older baseline pixels in both as independent. Screen observations/lineage before any outcome fit. Negative control: shape/rotation or unpaired projected-radius support cannot be used.

## Exact full-law aperture theorem (main negative result)
Let r be physical radius in units of the projected Plummer half-light radius a=1, G=1 and reference halo mass=1. Collisionless spherical stationary stellar tracer of negligible self-gravity, ν(r)=(1+r²)^(-5/2). Relative gravitational potential Ψ→0 at infinity; g=−Ψ′>0. The r^−1 Hernquist halo is g_H(r)=(1+r)^(-2), Ψ_H=(1+r)^(-1), M(r)=r²g(r), ρ_H=M′/(4πr²).
For any ε>0 choose a nonincreasing C∞ cutoff χ(r), equal 1 on [0,ε/2] and 0 for r≥ε, flat at the ends. For r<ε prescribe log g_C derivative ℓ(r)=χ/r+(1−χ)(−2/(1+r)) with g_C(ε)=g_H(ε); explicit g_C(r)=g_H(ε) exp(−∫_r^ε ℓ(s)ds). For r≥ε use exactly g_H. Near 0 g_C=c r exactly, hence a finite positive constant density core, analytic as a Cartesian function; smooth density everywhere on r>0 including splice. Define Ψ_C(r)=∫_r^∞g_C(s)ds, exactly Ψ_H outside ε, not merely + unknown additive constant. Let u=r log(g_C)′=(1−χ)u_H+χ, u_H=−2r/(1+r). Then −2<u≤1 and u′=(1−χ)u_H′+χ′(1−u_H)≤0. Its density ρ_C=(g_C/r)(2+u)/(4π) is positive; both factors are nonincreasing because (g_C/r)′/(g_C/r)=(u−1)/r≤0. Central c≥g_H(ε)/ε (upper c≤2g_H(ε/2)/ε), so finite for each ε but can grow as ε^−1, load-bearing: no uniform bound on core density derivatives or sharpness through the family. Equal total masses/enclosed mass outside, and dark density exactly equal outside ε. The halo is a physical nonnegative spherical gravitating density; if requiring a collisionless halo rather than an external potential it has a nonnegative stationary circular-orbit measure with uniform orbital orientations/phases and shell weights 4πr²ρ dr. No claim either halo's own DF is smooth/isotropic after cored replacement, or either equilibrium dynamically stable. The tracer proof is genuinely for a smooth-in-velocity interior-support DF with nonzero radial/tangential dispersions for r>0, except binding-energy/angular-momentum boundaries, not circular-shell tracer fitting.
For either potential take tracer f(E,L)=L F(E), E=Ψ(r)−|v|²/2, L=r v_t≥0, f=0 at E≤0. At each r>0 integrate over all velocity directions/speeds: ∫ f d³v=2π²r∫_0^Ψ(Ψ−E)F(E)dE (since 2π∫_0^π sin²θ dθ=π² and v³ dv=−2(Ψ−E)dE); anisotropy β=−1/2. Let h=(ν/r) resolved as function of Ψ. Since h and dh/dΨ vanish at Ψ=0 (asymptotic h~r^−6 and Ψ~r^−1), use F(E)=h″(E)/(2π²). Positivity: q(r)=−d(ν/r)/dr=(1+6r²)/[r²(1+r²)^(7/2)]>0. h″(Ψ)=(q/g²)[−q′/q+g′/g]. For Hernquist bracket =2/[r(1+6r²)]+7r/(1+r²)−2/(1+r), whose numerator after multiplying by r(1+6r²)(1+r²)(1+r) is 2+9r²−5r³+42r⁴+30r⁵>0 (9−5r+42r² positive via discriminant). For C, ℓ≥g_H′/g_H, so strictly positive too. High-binding endpoint F may diverge as Ψ→Ψ(0); for each r>0 accessible energies bounded away from it, reconstructed density exactly ν and finite total mass/second moments (bounded escape speed globally). Define r=0 data by a.e. limit; the singular phase-space point does not invalidate the probability measure. F(E)>0 for 0<E<Ψ(0), hence f>0 wherever L>0 on bound velocity support.
Crucially h_C(Ψ)=h_H(Ψ) for 0<Ψ≤Ψ_H(ε), since both r(Ψ) mappings coincide there. The derivative inversion is local in Ψ and uses only lower energy support; at any r≥ε all accessible E≤Ψ_H(ε), so the two joint phase-space laws are exactly the same there. A line of sight at R≥ε visits only r≥ε. Thus entire conditional full 3-velocity law (sky Cartesian, or rotated PMs plus LOS), not only three projected moments, is identical for all R≥ε; unbinned sky coordinates, even perfect spectra, calibrated velocities and convolution with common noise do not change that. This exact pair is not the parametric "coreNFW" generic prediction; the transition compact support is deliberate and equilibrium validity proved.

### Quantitative aperture and sampling limit without Gaussian errors
Projection direction fixed, distant plane-parallel geometry. For an independent tracer observed at fixed projected R, probability of actual r<ε is p(R)=(3w−w³)/2, w=√((ε²−R²)_+/(1+ε²)): normalize ∫(1+R²+z²)^(-5/2) dz through z=±√(ε²−R²), substituting t=z/√(1+R²+z²), integrand proportional to (1−t²)dt. The stated conditional weights require position-only selection by projected coordinates or independent photometry, with no postselection by velocity or depth. A common unconditional observation or noise kernel cannot increase total variation, but subsequent conditioning on accepted stars can change the common mixture weight and requires a separate calculation. Draws are independent stars, not repeated measurements of one star. Both models have the same Plummer positions, so conditional on R_i the mixed laws share common component of weight ∏(1−p(R_i)). Total variation ≤1−∏(1−p(R_i)); any size α (conditioned on that design) test has power ≤min(1,α+1−∏(1−p(R_i))). This is just coupling of identical outside components; exhaustive likelihood errors/priors cannot beat the ceiling. It is a necessary bound, not a guarantee of testability and not claimed attained by this pair.
For N iid draws selected on full projected aperture R≤A with A≥ε, p=ε³(1+A²)/[(1+ε²)^(3/2)A²] from spherical cumulative tracer count divided by projected cumulative count A²/(1+A²). In illustrative complete A=1 design, ε=1/20: p=2/(401)^(3/2)≈0.0002490654 and even N=119 perfect all-three-velocity draws have 5%-level power ≤0.07920745. N≥5,566 is necessary for 80%; ε=.1 gives N≥703. These are not actual empirical sample sizes/coverage (119 happens to be the Sculptor PM count but its design is not full projected A=1); central targeted observations can raise p greatly, p(0)~1.5ε. If all R>ε, two complete exact laws coincide, even infinite N. Dark models differ sharply only inside ε; a uniform-material resolved-core claim must bound ε from below and control actual geometry and covariance. Frames, relative proper motions and velocity-dependent selection are not silently ignored in any application.

## Positive resolved annular contrast (separate result)
Let known common member tracer ν>0 with finite nonzero ν(0) (externally valid central photometry), spherical, steady, and velocity **raw second moments in inertial galaxy rest frame** nonstreaming, or treat streaming in raw moments rather than subtracting unsupported gradients. Total enclosed gravitating force g=GM/r²≥0, not by itself a measurement of dark component if centrally concentrated baryonic matter allowed. If stellar ν has an unknown central hole or singular cusp, a statistic of νg is not a test of g absent separate calibration. Plane-parallel, projected pressures P_R=Σ⟨v_R²⟩ and P_T=Σ⟨v_T²⟩ (same stars/tracer selection/calibration). Let K(R)=∫_R^∞ν(r)g(r)/√(r²−R²)dr. Integrate radial component of steady Cartesian Jeans stress divergence along LOS, requiring T_Rz→0 at |z|→∞ (or matching vanishing tracer/edge stress). In the plane's circular basis divergence gives P_R′+(P_R−P_T)/R. Force along projected radial direction is −g(r)R/r, dLOS=2r dr/√(r²−R²), hence P_R′+(P_R−P_T)/R=−2R K. No artificial radial-aperture pressure boundary term, no fixed orbital anisotropy. Also follows by differentiating Cappellari/Leonard–Merritt projected profiles. Two full PM pressure profiles suffice in this ideal geometry; the superiority claim over full three projected moments is not that it supplies more information. RV useful for membership, systemic/distance perspective, independent shape/higher moments, and sanity checking covariance; converting both PM components by same distance ties the errors.
Take h>0 and 2h<A, φ(x)=(x−1)^2(2−x)^2 on [1,2], zero outside, I=∫φ(x)/x dx=4 ln2−11/4>0, J=∫ xφ dx=1/20. Define T_h=I^−1∫_1^2 φ′(x) K(hx)dx=−I^−1∫_1^2 φ(x) h K′(hx)dx. This is a compensated annular pressure statistic, requiring no differentiated data and no kinematic measurements beyond 2h: with η(R)=φ′(R/h)/h,
T_h=(2I)^−1∫_h^(2h) [(η′/R−2η/R²)P_R+η P_T/R²]dR.
Because η vanishes at edges, no unnoticed boundary stress. A uniform deterministic pressure error pair (e_R,e_T) biases at most [(4/(3√3)+1/4)e_R+(1/8)e_T]/(2I h²), by ∫|φ′|=1/8 and ∫|φ″|=4/(3√3), using x≥1. Finite stellar precision worsens toward the center, so the asymptotic statement below is not a finite precision promise. Within-mask selection weights/line-of-sight mixing, errors on Σ, common astrometric nuisance and calibrations need propagation; no derivative estimation needed.
Let F=νg/r, A fixed. For R<2h separate exterior K_out from r≥A with K_out′=R∫_A^∞ νg/(r²−R²)^(3/2)dr≥0. Thus its signed contribution to T_h is nonpositive, even with no boundary pressure assumption or no cap on the outer mass (assuming convergence). For cusp lower bound use independently imposed E_out=∫_A^∞νg/r³ dr, giving |T_ext|≤(J/I)h² E_out/[1−(2h/A)²]^(3/2). Inside K_in=∫_0^√(A²−R²) F(√(R²+z²))dz. Exact derivative −K_in′/R = F(A)/√(A²−R²)−∫_0^√(...) F′(√...)/√... dz. F(A) boundary here is an algebraic artificial split, not an assumed stress at observed edge.
* Any smooth-enough extended core F(A)≤B, |F′(r)|≤Cr on (0,A) has T_h≤(J/I) h² [B/√(A²−4h²)+C A]. Entire unmeasured exterior can only reduce it. Example interpretable controls: if ν≤νmax, ρ≤ρmax, |ν′|≤Cν r and |ρ′|≤Cρ r then B≤4πG νmax ρmax/3 and C≤4πG(ρmax Cν/3+νmax Cρ/5); use |ρ(r)−3M/(4πr³)|≤Cρ r²/5 by density average.
* A central r^−1 cusp has F(r)=A_c/r+F_0(r), A_c=ν(0)g(0+)>0. If |F_0(A)|≤B0, |F_0′|≤C0, its response from pure 1/r term is exactly −A_c d arcosh(A/R)/dR = A_c A/[R√(A²−R²)] for −K′. Thus T_h ≥ A_c −(J/I)h² [B0/√(A²−4h²)+C0 arcosh(A/h)+E_out/[1−(2h/A)²]^(3/2)]. Upper remainder also bounded by same absolute error plus A_c(1/√(1−(2h/A)²)−1) from leading term. In this class limit T_h→A_c, whereas bounded smooth core gives T_h→0 (lower bound follows by absolute errors and finite exterior). At fixed h, a positive T_h above core+tolerance floor can reject only the bounded-smoothness resolved core class, not every smooth but rapid crossover: the exact aperture pair supplies a converse for any ε≤h.
Exact numerical contrast control (dimensionless, synthetic): let A=1/4 (on the scale of either model below), h/A=1/10, choose core B≤2/A,C≤2/A³ and cusp A_c≥1,B0≤2/A,C0≤2/A²,E_out≤2/A². Then T_h(core)<.09, T_h(cusp)>.77, certified by rational bounds for log2, sqrt(0.96), arcosh(10)<3. These bounds are achievable with positive equilibrium witnesses, not assumed universality: ν Plummer as above, core force g=r(1+r²)^(-3/2) yields F=(1+r²)^−4, so B≤1,C≤8, and its same β=−1/2 tracer DF is positive since bracket =3/r−12r/(1+6r²)+4r/(1+r²)>0. Hernquist cusp exact above, H=νg with H(0)=1, |H′|≤5r+2≤13/4 on [0,A], |H″|≤5+35r²+20r+6≤291/16, F_0(r)=(H(r)−1)/r and derivative =∫_0^1 t H″(tr)dt, so B0,C0 meet inequalities; E_out≤∫_A∞dr/r³=1/(2A²). Actual annulus is only 0.025–0.05 projected half-light radii. Thus this verifies a finite geometrically resolved separation under explicit support and calibrations, not real dwarf precision or a finite annulus at 120–240 pc. It is compatible with complete outer Abel analysis and deliberately avoids generic "more PMs help".

### Scope
Lineage search confirms no fit was justified. Neither theorem identifies particle physics. The exact model uses no feedback, galaxy shape or star-formation inference, and not a statement about evolving/tidally stripped dwarfs. Positive contrast probes total νg and can only separate previously bounded model classes. The negative result gives full likelihood equality outside a photometrically specified transition and a conservative volume-driven upper bound for finite samples that may include it; no preferred prior or "all halos hidden."
