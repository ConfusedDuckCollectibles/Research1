# Independent audit: involutions of a hypothetical (3,10;40)-graph

**Result.** Pass. In a simple triangle-free graph on 40 vertices with independence number at most 9, every automorphism of order exactly 2 moves at least eight vertices. The additional class relative to the October 2026 Unsolved Lab report is (2,34). This is not a new upper bound for R(3,10), an exclusion of all order-two automorphisms, or a resolution of the 40-vertex decision problem. The identity is not an automorphism of order exactly 2.

## Independent short derivation

Let F be the fixed-vertex set of an order-two automorphism, and suppose |F|≥34. There are one, two or three moved pairs. Neighborhoods are independent, giving maximum degree 9. A vertex of degree at most 3 would leave 36 or more vertices after its closed neighborhood is removed; R(3,9)=36 supplies nine further independent vertices, yielding ten with that vertex. Thus the minimum degree is at least 4.

Every moved pair X={x,x'} is independent. An internal edge would forbid all fixed neighbors, as a fixed vertex adjacent to x is adjacent to both endpoints. An endpoint of an internal edge also cannot meet both vertices of any other pair: the involution would make the other endpoint meet both too. Its degree would therefore be at most 1+2=3.

For any moved pair let m be the number of fixed vertices adjacent to either endpoint (and hence to both). Let k be the size of the union of the endpoints' neighborhoods among the other moved vertices. The common antineighborhood among all 38 other vertices has order 38−m−k. It has no independent eight-set because X is an independent pair. R(3,8)=28 therefore gives m+k≥11 and, considering fixed antineighbors only, m≥|F|−27≥7. The number d of moved neighbors per endpoint is the same for both endpoints; their union has size k≤2d and d≤9−m. Thus 11≤m+k≤18−m≤11. This simultaneously proves m=7, k=4, d=2. In particular, |F| cannot be 36 or 38. This route needs no K(2,8) lemma or R(3,2), R(3,6) inputs.

In the remaining case |F|=34 there are precisely six moved vertices. Equality k=4 gives disjoint moved neighbor sets for endpoints of X and covers both other pairs. If x met both endpoints of another pair, x' would meet both by the involution, contradicting disjointness. Consequently each other pair is joined to X by a single perfect matching. The moved subgraph is 2-regular, on six vertices and triangle-free, hence a C6. Let B_1,B_2,B_3 be the three shared fixed neighborhoods. Each B_i has seven vertices and is independent. Two B_i are disjoint because a common fixed vertex together with an edge joining the associated pairs would form a triangle. The moved endpoints share their fixed neighborhood but are not global twins.

Put T=F\(B_1 ∪ B_2 ∪ B_3) of size 13. Denote the numbers of fixed-fixed edges joining different B_i by a, those joining their union to T by b, and those in T by c. There are no other fixed-fixed edges. Each vertex of a B_i already has two moved neighbors; summing their remaining degrees gives 2a+b≤147. Summing over T gives b+2c≤117. Each deletion F\B_i is a 27-vertex triangle-free graph with no independent eight-set, so it contains at least 85 edges. Summing over those three cards counts every a-edge once, every b-edge twice and every c-edge three times. Therefore
\[
255\le a+2b+3c
  =\tfrac12(2a+b)+\tfrac32(b+2c)
  \le (147+3\cdot117)/2=249,
\]
a contradiction. The finite input e(3,8,27)≥84 would already suffice, but 85 is the sourced value.

The alternative stable author proof also checks. Its K(2,8) exclusion covers the 30 vertices outside its complete bipartite block by vertices anticomplete to the size-two side (at most 27) or anticomplete to the size-eight side (at most two). Both sides are independent; no outside vertex may meet both. Its use of R(3,6)=18 on the antineighborhood of an independent union of two moved pairs is likewise valid. The independent pair-union argument above is shorter and derives the matching without that additional Ramsey threshold.

## Exact checks and primary sourcing

Independent JS enumerates all invariant moved graphs through three moved pairs and filters by degrees, triangle-freeness and pair antineighborhood bounds. It gives 217 triangle-free masks out of 512 for six moved vertices and exactly four survivor masks (labelled), all C6, with m=7 in each pair. For two or four moved vertices there are no survivors. Its separate capacity arithmetic loop proves the maximum in the integer relaxation, 249; no catalogue data are imported, and this is not an enumeration of 40-vertex graphs. Replay by extracting the one fenced code block into a CommonJS file and running it with Node; expected JSON is separately frozen.
